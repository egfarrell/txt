# WEEKLY PHYSICS + WORK TIMETABLE

Sleep target (most days): 23:30 – 07:30
Sleep target (Wednesday): 23:00 – 06:00

### MONDAY (Remote Work)
    - 07:30 – 08:00    Wake, breakfast
    - 08:00 – 09:30    Physics Study 1 (`3 blocks`)
    - 09:30 – 12:30    Work (3 hr)
    - 12:30 – 13:30    Lunch
    - 13:30 – 14:30    Physics Study 2 (`2 blocks`)
    - 14:30 – 18:30    Work (4 hr)
    - TOTAL WORK: 7 hr
    - 18:30 – 20:00    Dinner, rest, Exercise
    - 20:00 – 22:00    Physics Study 3 (`4 blocks`)
    - 22:00 – 22:30    Exercise 
    - 22:30 – 23:30    Wind down
    - TOTAL PHYSICS: 4.5 hr (`9 blocks`)
    - TOTAL EXERCISE: 1 hr

### TUESDAY (Remote Work)
    - (Same structure as Monday)
    - TOTAL PHYSICS: 4.5 hr (`9 blocks`)
    - TOTAL EXERCISE: 1 hr
    - TOTAL WORK: 7 hr

### WEDNESDAY (Office + Commute)
    - 06:00 – 07:00    Wake, cycle to station
    - 07:00 – 09:00    Commute - Physics Study Block 1 (1 hr)
    - 09:30 – 12:30    Work (3 hr)
    - 12:30 – 13:30    Lunch
    - 13:30 – 14:00    Physics Study Block 2 (0.5 hr)
    - 14:00 – 18:30    Work (4.5 hr)
    - TOTAL WORK: 7.5 hr
    - 18:30 – 20:30    Commute  - Physics Study Block 3 (0.5 hr)
    - 20:30 – 21:30    Exercise block 2 (30 min)
    - 21:30 – 23:00    Wind down
    - TOTAL PHYSICS: 2–3 hr (`6 blocks`)
    - TOTAL EXERCISE: cycle to/from station 1 hr

### THURSDAY (Full Study Day)
    - 07:30 – 08:00    Wake, breakfast
    - 08:00 – 09:00    Physics Study Block 1 (1 hr)
    - 09:00 – 09:30    Exercise block 1 (30 min)
    - 09:30 – 11:30    Physics Study Block 2 (2 hr)
    - 11:30 – 12:30    Break, lunch
    - 12:30 – 14:30    Physics Study Block 3 (2 hr)
    - 14:30 – 16:00    Rest, recovery
    - 16:00 – 17:30    Physics Study Block 4 (1.5 hr)
    - 17:30 – 18:00    Exercise block 2 (30 min)
    - Evening free
    - TOTAL PHYSICS: 6.5 hr (`13 blocks`)
    - TOTAL EXERCISE: 1 hr

### FRIDAY (Full Study Day)
    - (Same as Thursday)
    - TOTAL PHYSICS: 6.5 hr (`13 blocks`)
    - TOTAL EXERCISE: 1 hr

### SATURDAY (Deep Study Day)
    - 07:30 – 08:00    Wake, breakfast
    - 08:00 – 10:00    Physics Study Block 1 (2 hr)
    - 10:00 – 10:30    Exercise block 1 (30 min)
    - 10:30 – 12:30    Physics Study Block 2 (2 hr)
    - 12:30 – 14:00    Lunch, rest
    - 14:00 – 16:00    Physics Study Block 3 (2 hr)
    - 16:00 – 16:30    Exercise block 2 (30 min)
    - Evening free
    - TOTAL PHYSICS: 6 hr (`12 blocks`)
    - TOTAL EXERCISE: 1 hr

### SUNDAY (Recovery + Light Study Day)
    - 07:30 – 08:30    Wake slowly, breakfast
    - 08:30 – 10:00    Physics Study Block 1 (1.5 hr)

    - 17:00 – 18:30    Physics Study Block 2 (1.5 hr)

    - TOTAL EXERCISE: 1 hr

### WEEKLY TOTALS
    - Physics Study:     33 – 35 hours/week
    - Exercise:          7 hours/week
    - Sleep:             Fully sufficient
    - Structure:         Optimal and sustainable long-term
