### Change Presta or Schrader valves types
    - unscrew pump cap
    - push up thumb lock to help eject internal valve
    - reverse both parts of internal valve
    - push reversed parts back into pump
    - screw on pump cap again
    - https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I6J8D0RLmFo
