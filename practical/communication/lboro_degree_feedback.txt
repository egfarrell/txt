Dear Enda

Thank you for your thoughtful comments on the programme feedback form.
I will be collating the responses and circulating a summary to
colleagues.


1 I agree entirely that physics (and maths as well) is about concepts
and not rote learning of formulae.  Some of the pressure from the
latter comes from the need to have something to assess and to ensure
that everyone should be able to answer the questions. (In my case I do
try to concentrate on the concepts in Fundamentals of Quantum
Information and Quantum Computing; in Elementary Particle Physics
there is a lot more fiddly detail because we don't yet know what the
concepts are!)



2 Reducing the module load to 8 x 15 credits may be beneficial.  Some
departments indeed do this - however, this would require a major
shake-up of all our teaching, so would take some time to work out.



3 Taking an A4 sheet into exams is a good idea - indeed the process of
preparing such a sheet would be a useful exercise.  I think this has
been practice in some modules in other departments; we might need to
sound out experience there.  Someone told me some years ago that when
he tried it the marks fell - presumably the drop in performance of
those who get through by memorising formulae outweighed the increase
in performance of those who grasped the concepts.  But the change in
culture (of students and examiners) will be for the better.


4 More semester-long project, less exam.


5 Good to hear Loughborough offers helpful support.


All the best

John
