Personal Statement

My career to-date has been made up of two strands: technical and
care work. On one hand I have worked as a computer programmer and
studied Maths to degree level. However I have also worked as
a care worker in homeless services and in a nursing home
environment.

I enjoyed both types of work but often felt I would like to
combine them. This is why I am interested in training as a Maths
teacher in the FE sector. Previously, when I did computer work,
I missed the interaction with people. And while working in
a nursing home I often missed the challenge of analytical work.
Teaching would allow both sides to flourish.

I have a natural passion for Maths and also have significant
experience in this area. I worked as a Research Officer at the
Office for National Statistics (ONS) and received professional
training in statistical mathematics from ONS. My undergraduate
degree was a combination of Maths & Physics and I did a Research
Masters in Astronomy which had a high level of mathematical
content.

However, as well as having an excellent Mathematical knowledge,
I am passionate about teaching the basic concepts to learners.
I enjoy reading about Maths history which is useful when trying
to motivate the topic. For example, I like to explain why
a particular technique is important and when it was first used.
I also have basic teaching experience. In 2005 I worked for
4 months in Germany as an English 'TEFL' teacher which introduced
me to basic classroom techniques.

My years as a social care worker also taught me skills which are
transferable to teaching. For example, the importance of
self-care and how to avoid 'burning out', how to maintain clear
boundaries, how to deal with difficult behaviour etc. I am
currently attending a 'CELTA' English teacher course in
Nottingham where I will gain more classroom experience.

Overall I have a good combination of personal and technical
skills which would be beneficial for teaching. I am very
interested in working with people who are immigrants and
refugees, perhaps with Derby City Council or at Derby College. In
a sense I am an 'immigrant' myself from Ireland and would
enjoying teaching other nationalities the basic knowledge and
skills needed to find work in the UK and integrate into society.
