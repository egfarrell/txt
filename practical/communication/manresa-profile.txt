- Over my working life I've had various jobs without ever
  committing to one of them - IT Programmer, Astronomer, Care
  worker, Maths teacher. There's been a restless hovering
  underneath ever since I left corporate IT work and did a Jesuit
  Volunteer Community (JVC) twenty-five years ago.
- The JVC year and two long walking pilgrimages 'opened'
  something in me that has never really closed or healed. My
  prayer life has deepened over the years (mainly into silence in
  front of the Cross) and I have felt myself growing closer to
  the Spirit of Jesus. But I have never committed to a way of
  life.
- Over the past three years a gospel reading keeps surfacing:
  Mark 8-35 "For whoever wants to save their life will lose it,
  but whoever loses their life ..."
- I'm about to start a new admin job with the Civil Service and
  will be based in Dublin. I'm hoping to discern deeper what is
  this sense of 'call' hovering beneath for many years. I realise
  I need guidance and support – to be able to listen deeper. Too
  often in the past I've launched out in different directions
  without fully discerning if the Spirit was calling me that way.
- Best regards,
- Enda Farrell
