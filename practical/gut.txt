- After a long course of antibiotics like penicillin, gut
  bacteria can become imbalanced, leading to prolonged diarrhoea.
  To help restore gut health and ease symptoms, focus on foods
  that replenish beneficial bacteria, support digestion, and
  reduce inflammation.

### Foods to Restore Gut Balance:

#### 1. Probiotic-Rich Foods (Replenish good bacteria)
- Yogurt (preferably unsweetened, live cultures)
- Kefir (fermented milk with diverse probiotics)
- Sauerkraut (fermented cabbage, rich in probiotics and fiber)
- Kimchi (fermented spicy cabbage, good for gut bacteria)
- Miso (fermented soybean paste, useful in soups)
- Tempeh (fermented soy product, high in protein and probiotics)

#### 2. Prebiotic Foods (Feed beneficial bacteria)
- Garlic
- Onions
- Leeks
- Bananas (especially slightly green ones)
- Asparagus
- Oats

#### 3. Easily Digestible, Gut-Healing Foods
- Bone broth (rich in collagen and amino acids for gut repair)
- Cooked vegetables (easier to digest than raw)
- Steamed rice, potatoes, and oats (bland, easy on the stomach)
- Plain chicken or fish (lean protein sources)

#### 4. Hydration & Electrolytes
- Coconut water (natural electrolytes)
- Herbal teas (chamomile, ginger, peppermint for soothing digestion)
- Diluted bone broth

### Foods to Avoid:
- Sugar & processed foods (can feed bad bacteria)
- Dairy (except yogurt/kefir) (may worsen diarrhoea in some cases)
- Artificial sweeteners (especially sorbitol and sucralose)
- Excess caffeine & alcohol (can irritate the gut)
