### Types of payplan settlement
    - FULL SETTLEMENT 
        - clear all accounts on file by paying 100% of the
          outstanding balance; it will show as fully satisfied
          for 6 years on your credit file

    - SHORT SETTLEMENT  ("full and final")
        - clear all accounts on file by paying a percentage of
          the full balance and requesting a write off of the
          rest; it will show on your credit file as partially
          satisfied for 6 years

    - PARTIAL SETTLEMENT 
        - clear some of the accounts on file by Full or Short
          Settlement - it might be considered preferential
          treatment
