
### Tips
    - get a van with _window_ in the roof, nothing on the sides
    - _Tax_ as a commercial vehicle
    - Please be aware that _CO2_ builds up in the van if you
      sleep in it. There needs to be ventilation system of some
      kind. Basically one hole somewhere up and one hole
      somewhere down.
