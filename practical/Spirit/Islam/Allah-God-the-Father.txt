## Catholic Teaching on Muslims and the Worship of God

The Catholic Church teaches that Muslims worship the same one Creator God worshipped by Christians and Jews, although Christians and Muslims understand God differently.

This teaching was expressed clearly at the Second Vatican Council (1962–1965).

### Nostra Aetate
The Vatican II document *Nostra Aetate* states that Muslims:

> “adore the one God, living and subsisting in Himself; merciful and all-powerful, the Creator of heaven and earth.”

### Lumen Gentium
Another Vatican II document, *Lumen Gentium*, says:

> “Together with us they adore the one, merciful God.”

The Church therefore teaches:
- there is only one God;
- Muslims intend to worship that one Creator God;
- but Islam and Christianity differ profoundly in their understanding of Him.

The principal difference is the doctrine of the Trinity:
- Christians believe in Father, Son, and Holy Spirit;
- Muslims reject the Trinity and the divinity of Christ.

Thus the Catholic position is:
- same God intended,
- different understanding of God.

Also important:
“Allah” is simply the Arabic word for “God”. Arab Christians also use the word “Allah” in prayer and in Arabic Bibles.
