- It is within the scope of G-d's plan that we should come up
  against evil in this world, and have to fight a way for
  ourselves through it.

- Our Lord could have captivated the minds and hearts of the
  whole world by a single sermon, but he didn't. Alone, with
  a few faithful disciples, he lived in the midst of constant
  _hostility_.

- If we feel it is beyond us to understand this, we only have to
  recall the divine words:
    - "Your ways are not my ways..."
    - and from St. Paul: "O the depth of the riches and wisdom
      and knowledge of God! ... how unsearchable are his ways!"

- We must face reality, and not live in an unreal world, not even
  in an ideal world of our own imagining, which we build up with
  our poor and unsubstantial dreams. We make a great act of
  adoration and submission when we recognise the absolute
  sovereignty of G-d in _all that happens_.
    - Only such a thought can enable us to put up with the
      numberless abuses which surround us - in ourselves and
      others, in the Church and in the World at large.
    - (Not sure... is G-d sovereign over Auschwitz, or was G-d's
      heart ripped in two forever)

- On the practical plane, _do not rush_ your decisions, lest you
  spoil G-d's plan by substituting your own way of looking at
  things for his.
    - If, on any particular point, circumstances indicate
      a change which would seem to be advisable and is feasible,
      act on it...
    - But if, for various reasons, you are unable to carry it
      out, even though it appears to you to be desirable, then
      submit to the divine plan which, through the very
      impossibility of acting otheriwse, lets us understand that
      G-d wants to _tolerate the disorder_ for the present.
    - Wait for G-d's hour. G-d's patience is more than we shall
      ever understand. He could break the resistance of all
      evil-doers, and make all evil cease in an instant, but he
      does not.
        - (I find Process theology more true? The G-d is not
          omnipotent?)
    - Adjust then your pace to G-d's. _Do not destroy_ without
      considering the consequences. This thought flings us back
      on G-d, and makes us pass beyond all created things.

- We should always try to keep ourselves in the divine plan: that
  is, superior to things taken in themselves, and be glad that
  G-d has given us the opportunity to break our own will and so
  turn to him.
    - This is the best way to learn to live the life of the
      spirit. Following Our Lord's example, we should remain
      steadfast in G-d, and regard everything as part of G-d's
      plan.
    - This is the only way to gain complete independence of
      created things.
        > He became obedient to the point of death, even death on
        > a cross
     - He trod under foot all that was purely of the senses. He
       dominated it all completely, and thus entered into his
       glory. Everything human was crushed so that G-d might be
       exalted on its ruins.
        -  (don't like "crushing" all humanity... think Jeshua
           stayed really human till the end)

- We must not be surprised if, while the cross lasts, we
  experience no feeling of elevation or consolation; on the
  contrary, we may often be incapable of raising up our spirits
  or of thinking of the good effects which will follow later.

- More often than not, all we can do at the moment is to accept
  and offer up our _distress_ to Our Lord.
