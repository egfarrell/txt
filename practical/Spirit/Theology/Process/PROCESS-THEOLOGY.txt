
### Picturing Quantum Processes

    - https://www.amazon.co.uk/Picturing-Quantum-Processes-Diagrammatic-Reasoning/dp/110710422X
    - Misleading title, but great book on `process theory`
    - Not a good first book on QM: the gentle introduction in
      Chapter 1 drops off sharply.
    - An excellent intro to *process theories*, a useful branch
      of category theory. A QM background helps, but isn't
      essential. The “pictures” are abstract diagrams, not
      visualisations of subatomic phenomena.
