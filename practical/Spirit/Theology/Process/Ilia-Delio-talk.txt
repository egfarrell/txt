- God in Matter, Evolution, and AI with Ilia Delio
    - https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sOVSKWP5CF8

### Speaker:
    - Ilia Delio (Franciscan sister; former neuropharmacologist)
      integrates science, theology, and evolution into
      a “process” view of God.

### Core thesis:
    - “God” is not a static being but an evolving, relational,
      emergent reality (“not yet God”) arising within cosmic
      process .

### Paradigm shift claim:

  - Religion undergoes major shifts ~every 500 years.
  - Current shift: decline of “sky God” (transcendent, static
    deity) → emergence of God-from-matter paradigm .

### Scientific driver of theological change:

  - Quantum physics + consciousness studies undermine classical
    metaphysics.
  - Reality = energy/process; no fixed “substance” or “essence.”
  - Traditional theism (unchanging, separate being) conflicts
    with Big Bang cosmology and evolutionary emergence .

### Ontology:

  - Dual-aspect monism: mind and matter are inseparable aspects
    of reality.
  - Every entity (quark, lepton) has “inside”
    (proto-experience/consciousness) and “outside” (physical
    expression) .
  - Rejects strict physicalism and classical dualism.

### Teilhard de Chardin influence:

  - Evolution = rise of complexity + consciousness.
  - Three laws:
    - `Recurrence`: 
        - micro ↔ macro structural repetition (fractal-like).
    - `Creative union`: 
        - entities evolve via increasing relational unity.
    - `Complexity-consciousness`: 
        - higher relational complexity → higher consciousness .
  - Omega principle: 
        - “love energy” as unitive, non-entropic driver of
          evolution.
  - Matter has intrinsic mystical/depth dimension.

### Whitehead / process alignment:

  - Reality = relational PROCESSES, not substances.
  - God = dynamic field of possibility rather than fixed entity.
  - Critique: “fallacy of misplaced concreteness” in traditional
    theology.

### Jung integration:

  - God as archetypal/depth reality within psyche.
  - Emphasis on unconscious and experiential access to “divine.”
  - God-language = symbolic pointer, not literal description.

### Epistemic stance:

  - All God-talk = metaphor; ultimate reality unknowable.
  - Science cannot resolve ultimate mystery because observer is
    part of system (Max Planck quote) .

### Reinterpretation of God:

  - God = field of possibility / call / relational lure toward
    love.
  - No actualisation of God without relational response
    (“lover–beloved” dynamic).
  - “Unchanging” = persistence of call, not static being.
  - God evolves with creation (process theism variant).

### Anthropology:

  - Humans = universe become self-conscious.
  - No fixed human essence; identity is emergent, evolutionary.
  - Individuality = irreducible uniqueness (“thisness”) within
    relational whole.

### Critique of traditional Christianity:

  - Overly Platonic (spirit > matter).
  - Doctrinal rigidity (“belief = salvation”) is “unhealthy.”
  - Disconnection from science → conceptual stagnation.
  - “Old religion” contributes to ecological/technological
    disorientation.

### Evolutionary continuity:

  - Religion and human evolution are inseparable (“hominisation
    = religionisation”).
  - Movement: biosphere → noosphere (global mind).

### AI / technology thesis:

  - AI = continuation of evolutionary trajectory (extension of
    noosphere).
  - Technology + climate change = primary drivers of current
    human evolution.
  - Humans will not remain Homo sapiens indefinitely .

### AI implications:

  - Not inherently existential threat; fear is misplaced.
  - Real issue: lack of ethical framework and purpose.
  - Need “near-term eschatology” (vision of desired future
    50–1000 years).
  - Current discourse asks wrong questions (e.g., control,
    compassion limits).

### Sociological observation:

  - Generational divide:
    - Older: fear AI.
    - Younger: see opportunity and integration.

- Key unresolved / contested points:
  - Role of consciousness in fundamental physics 
        - (explicitly “not settled”).
  - Validity of panpsychist/dual-aspect interpretations.
  - Whether God truly evolves vs. only human understanding
    evolves.
  - Scientific legitimacy of Teilhard’s “love energy” and
    teleology.

### Unusual / non-standard claims:

  - God emerges from matter rather than precedes it.
  - Consciousness is fundamental to all matter.
  - Evolution driven by “love” as real energetic principle.
  - AI as theological/evolutionary advancement, not threat.
  - “God is not the mystery; we are the mystery.”

### Overall conclusion:

  - Theology must be rebuilt on evolutionary, relational, and
    process-based foundations aligned with modern science;
    failure to do so renders religion obsolete in the age of AI
    and cosmological understanding.
