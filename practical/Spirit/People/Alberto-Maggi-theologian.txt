### Italian priest: Alberto Maggi
    - Movie: 'Heretic in the Ward'

### - Main Views / Ideas:
    - Focus on the historical Jesus:
        - Emphasizes understanding Jesus in his original Jewish
          context, often challenging traditional dogmatic
          interpretations.
    - Inclusive Gospel message:
        - Argues Jesus preached universal love and inclusion,
          especially of the marginalised, sinners, and outcasts.
    - Critique of clericalism:
        - Critical of hierarchical, power-based structures in the
          Church; promotes a more horizontal, community-based
          Church.
    - Mercy over judgment:
        - Stresses God's unconditional love and forgiveness,
          minimizing fear-based religiosity (hell, punishment).
    - Accessible theology:
        - Writes and speaks in accessible language, aiming to
          bring Scripture to laypeople in modern, human terms.

### Books
    - "Dangerous Verses"
        - Versetti pericolosi (2011)
        - Explores the scandal of mercy through the censored
          story of the adulterous woman in Luke’s Gospel.
        - Maggi highlights how the early Church suppressed this
          narrative, yet shows how it powerfully subverts
          religious and social norms.

    - "The Last Beatitude: Death as the Fullness of Life"
        - L'ultima beatitudine: La morte come pienezza di vita
          (2017)
        - Written after his own serious illness, Maggi reframes
          death not as an enemy but as a companion in life’s
          journey—transforming fear into acceptance and hope.

    - "Our Lady of the Heretics: Story of Mary of Nazareth"
        - Nostra Signora degli eretici: Storia di Maria di
          Nazaret (2016)
        - Maggi demythologizes the figure of Mary, inviting
          readers to see her not as an untouchable icon but as
          a sister in faith whose real humanity deepens our
          connection.

    - "In These Times: A Hymn to Life to Rediscover the Joy of
      Every Day"
        - Di questi tempi: Un inno alla vita per riscoprire la
          gioia di ogni giorno (2018)

    - "Bernadette"
        - Bernadette (2022)

    - "The Madness of God: The Christ of John"
        - A loucura de Deus: O Cristo de João (2013)
        - Discusses the radical image of Jesus in John’s Gospel
          as understood through Divine "madness"—a wisdom beyond
          human logic.

    - "Jesus and Women"
        - Gesù e le donne (2006)

    - "Jesus and Beelzebub: Satan and Demons in the Gospel of
      Mark"
        - Gesù e Belzebù. Satana e demòni nel vangelo di Marco
          (2000)

    - "Those Who Don’t Die Meet Again"
        - Chi non muore si rivede (2013)
        - A personal account of Maggi's own illness and recovery,
          marked by faith and even joy amidst suffering.

    - "Blows and Answers"
        - Botte e risposte (2021)

    - "The Beatitudes"
        - Le Beatitudini (2014)

    - "The Truth Will Set Us Free: Conversations with Paolo Rodari"
        - La verità ci rende liberi (2020)

    - "Double Trouble: My Life as a Street Kid and Servant of
      Mary"
        - Due in condotta. La mia vita da ragazzo di strada
          e servo di Maria (2019)
