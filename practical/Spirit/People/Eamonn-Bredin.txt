### Rediscovering Jesus: Challenge of Discipleship
    - Christmas 2024, Glendalough

    - In the Gospels we see Jesus go off to lonely places to pray
      to Abba, a God who 'hugs humanity into his very person'
      (George Herbert), and to discover Abba's will for him.
    - These lonely places have communion with Abba. He found the
      strength _to fail by human standards_, knowing that Abba
      would be with him come what may. 
    - John 16:32 has caught this superbly. 
        - "Yet I am not alone. The Father is always with me." 
    - Key: the strength to fail by human standards.
