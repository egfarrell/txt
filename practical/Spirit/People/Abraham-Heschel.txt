

### The Prophets
    - In it Heschel puts forward what would become a central idea
      in his theology: that the prophetic (and, ultimately,
      Jewish) view of God is best understood not as
      anthropomorphic (that God takes human form) but rather as
      anthropopathic—that God has human feelings.
