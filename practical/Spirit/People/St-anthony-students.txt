
### Mam, before Warsaw PhD

 - Glorious St Anthony, divinely filled with the science of the
   saints,
 - I place my studies under your powerful protection.
 - Following your example I desire my knowledge to be grounded in
   the hearts of Jeshua & Mary.
 - With the aid of your prayers, I wish to perform my studies as
   a matter of duty, in the spirit of penance and with a pure
   intention of wonder and awe.
 - Implore the Father of Light to grant me a quick understanding,
   a safe judgment and a faithful memory.
 - Obtain for me the grace to work with patience, method and
   constancy, to develop the gifts I have received from the God,
   and to employ them for God's greater glory.
 - Pray God to bless my efforts, granting me success in my tasks,
   and keeping me humble in the midst of success.
 - Amen.
