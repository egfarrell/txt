### Hints / Wisdom 
    - Good for pain: MST: oral morphine

    - Always believe your patients... don't analyse them.

    - Every one of us has a Hitler inside us...

    - Healthcare professionals / psychiatrists have very high
      rates of suicide. Elisabeth: if we had 'screaming rooms' in
      our hospitals, sound-proofed where they could let out their
      anguish and rage, impotence, frustration, sadness...

    - You're always given what you need... and that includes
      energy...(if you use it to help...)

    - We have to deal with our own unfinished business... someone
      can push our buttons... There's no such thing as burnout...
      we won't be drained if we have dealt with our business and
      no-one is pushing our buttons then ...

    - Especially people who make us mad... we should bless
      them... they cross our path to make us whole...

    - If they push your button about something, that means it's
      someething in you that you don't like... or something that
      you epxerienced in your childhood that you reacted to...
        - anytime you react negatively it helps you diagnose your
          own unfinished business (if we pay attention to it)

    - There's only one G-d... I'm convinced about that... and
      we're all brothers and sisters

    - unconditional love is our goal
