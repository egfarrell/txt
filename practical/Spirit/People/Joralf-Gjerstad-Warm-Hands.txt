### Quotes
    - I never promised healing. I only promised that I would try.
      And people respected that. But there were also those who
      mocked. There were doctors who said it was nonsense. And
      priests who said it was dangerous. But I continued anyway.
      I _never asked for money_ for it. People tried to pay, but
      I refused. This was not work. This was a calling. And
      because of that, some people honored me. And some people
      despised me. That is simply how the world is.

    - And in those days, that meant you were expected to stay
      quiet. So I kept my feelings to myself. But I felt
      everything very deeply. I carried it all inside, all those
      years. Maybe that is why I could feel other people’s pain
      so clearly. It was like their pain echoed inside me. And
      when I put my hands on someone, I felt where the pain was.
      Not because I knew it with my mind, but because I felt it
      through them. Some said it was a gift. Others said it was
      sensitivity. I do not know what it was. I only know that it
      was there. And that I had to use it.

    - But it was not always easy. Because when you feel someone
      else’s pain, you also suffer with them. And that can wear
      you down. There were nights when I could not sleep. Because
      the pain of another person was still inside me. I had to
      learn how to release it, to let it go. Otherwise, it would
      have destroyed me. But it also taught me compassion.
      I understood what it means to suffer — not just to see it.
      And that is why I always treated people gently. Never with
      judgment. Never with pride. Only with respect for the human
      being sitting before me. Because everyone carries something
      heavy. Everyone has a burden. And sometimes, just being
      seen is healing in itself. People often said that I “took
      the pain.” But I don’t think I took it. I think I shared
      it. And in sharing, it became lighter. Not gone — just
      lighter. And sometimes, that was enough for a person to
      continue.

    - I believe that everything that is beautiful, everything
      that gives joy, everything that changes life for the better
      — behind that stands a great and loving God.
        - Some people talk about miracles. But I think miracles
          happen in silence. They happen in the small things
          — a look, a touch, a moment of peace. People think
          miracles are loud. But they are not. Miracles are soft.
          They happen quietly, inside a person. And they are
          different for every person. But people often want
          miracles to be big and dramatic. But the truth is that
          faith is what shapes how we understand what happens.




### Interview
    - https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CSAGDBmRGNw
    - Interview with Joralf Gjerstad in Røros, also known as the
      Snåsa Man. Countless people have received help from Joralf
      and his psychic and healing abilities. Joralf is convinced
      that God helps him and gives him these powers. It cannot be
      denied that there has been skepticism. Joralf has lived
      a long life, labeled both a miracle worker and a fraud.
      Tirelessly, he has helped thousands of people. Now he wants
      to go out and speak with as many people as he can.
    - After many years, Joralf Gjerstad is going on tour to meet
      the people, as he says. With him are two of his dearest
      collaborators and friends, Therese Ulvan and Øyvind Løvrød.
      They are embarking on a journey from church to church to
      talk about the big and important questions in life, along
      with warmth and humor. Joralf hopes these will be evenings
      that give the visitors value, reflection, and a good
      feeling to take home. The proceeds go to the Gjerstad
      Foundation.
    - The Snåsa Man is a cultural figure in Norway, surrounded by
      stories, belief, doubt, and a sort of quiet folk mysticism.
      It raises interesting questions about where personal
      meaning and communal belief intersect.

- Hello and welcome to Wisdom From North. My name is Jannecke and
I'm here with Joralf Gjerstad, also called "Snåsamannen" (the man
from Snåsa). It is an honour for me to be with him today. I´ve
dreamt of an interview with him for a long time. He is currently
on a journey with Øyvind Løvrød and Therese Ulvan to talk about
the big questions in different churches.

- Hello Joralf, welcome!
- Thank you very much.

- What will you be talking about in the church today?
- I´m going to talk about the questions that people ask me.
  I don´t have access to them now. The questions will come when
  we are in the church.

- You don´t know what you will be answering?
- I know a bit, it will be about religion and life. The questions
  will come.

- You have healed thousands of people. Can you tell us about how
  these abilities work?
- When sick people come to me and want me to heal them. I place
  my hands on them and say a prayer inside. I have done that with
  the thousands of people that has seen me. Some become well, and
  some don´t. I never ask why the person doesn't become well.
  It's just the way it is. Some are healed and others aren't.
  Luckily, there are few that don't heal.

- Is it true that you can feel other peoples pain? If they
  experience pain in a knee, can you feel that in your own body?
- I received a lady who had back pain. I told her; you have back
  pain. She said no. I told her to go to the doctor to find out
  about it. She so did, and it turned out she had cancer in one
  of her kidneys. They took pictures and told her to come back
  after 14 days to see the development.

- In between this she came back to see me. I did what I was
  supposed to do. I placed my hands on her and said a small
  prayer. People don't know that I prayed. No one knows that, but
  now it's out there. When I wanted to heal someone I had to pray
  to receive strength to heal people.

- The lady came to me and went back to the hospital after 14
  days. They gave her an x-ray and they discovered that the
  cancer was gone. How do I know that? Because the senior
  physician at Levanger hospital came to me and told me and Signe
  what happened. That's why I can share this.

- A woman with cancer on her kidney, with my healing abilities
  she became well. The senior physician at Levanger hospital
  admits to it.

- It is amazing the number of people you have healed and how many
  you have helped. Do you understand the power you have got? Do
  you know where it comes from?
- In the beginning I always wondered. But I understood that I was
  involved in spiritual deeds. That I had to believe in the power
  of the spirit that Christianity had. Every time I was about to
  heal someone I said a small prayer for them. Everyone that has
  come to me for healing has received a small prayer in their
  lives. Some have become well and others have not, but they are
  few.

- You discovered your gift at the age of seven? How was it to be
  seven years old and have abilities that not everyone else had?
  Was it lonely?
- I never thought about it. I was eight years old. I understood
  later that I was given the ability. I believed that I should
  utilize the power of the spirit to help. I was bound to the
  power of prayers and to faith.

- Even though I have been the man I need to be to have such
  a faith, I faced a difficult time ahead in certain areas.
  I received a lot of criticism. They said I thought myself better
  than other people. That was the worst thing I heard. I became
  lonely, I had faith and I prayed.

- Most people do not know about this. This is the first time I'm
  talking about this on TV. That I received spiritual strength to
  heal people with the power of prayer. Nothing can convince me
  that a human being can receive a power like that without it
  being spiritual deeds involved.

- That is the huge, good thing. That leads to believing and that
  others start to believe as well. It was a great joy to have
  that ability.

- I read in your book that you sometimes can heal by thoughts?
  Can you tell us how that works?
- The power of thought. Yes, but it is the same thing. The power
  of thought is connected to the power of the spirit. It gets its
  nurture from the spirit because the thought can't heal. It is
  the power of the spirit that can heal.

- I sat at home in Snåsa when someone from Canada called. They
  asked if I could heal a native American. The wife of a native
  American. A medicine man... I told him I can't speak English,
  and she could not speak Norwegian. What were we to do? He said
  she only wanted to say hallo to me.

- I told her to come to the phone. She came and I said; "hello"
  to her. She replied; "hello". She then said "hello, hello" and
  I said "hello, hello." It ended there.

- I then called the woman from Norway, from Grong. It turned out
  that she had made a complete recovery. That is what I heard.
  She knows the person that was on the phone. She became well.

- When I count that event distance doesn't matter. Distance
  doesn't matter. Whether you're one meter away from me or
  a thousand kilometres away, it doesn't matter. It is the spirit
  of the thought and faith. You have to have faith.

- It is quite magical?
- I never speculate in that, I just give thanks that I am able to
  do it.

- Can you elaborate on what you think happens when we die? you
  are going to talk about death tonight. The speak is called;
  "Life, death and hope". Do you believe that we move on after
  death?
- I believe what is written in the New Testament is correct. I do
  not have to explain more than that.

- Can you tell us about the importance of being in what is good
  and about love? Life is difficult for many people. How can we
  obtain happy lives? I know you've been talking about focusing
  on what is good. Can you tell us more about that?
- It is like the radio. If you wish to have a better life and
  a different shape to your life, _you can't do that by
  yourself_.
    - You can, in peaceful quietness, be in solitude and pray.
    - Pray to receive strength to give you a better life. I feel
      that that is the right thing.

- You can't create the clothes yourself without having someone to
  weave it for you. It is the power of the spirit. Many people
  don't believe in this, but this is how it is. It is also
  written in the bible. "Knock, and the door will be opened to
  you".

- _People need to avoid criticizing_. It is apparent in the
  testament; you aren't supposed to make fun of people.
    - Not separate them away from the normal life. Everyone have
      their own life to tend to. Everyone has a life to live from
      cradle to grave. In-between those two you have to live
      a life of good deeds. Live normally.

- Live a normal life?
- Hmm.

- I'm going to ask a difficult question. I don't know if it is
  difficult or not but, If you aren't a Christian, or a religious
  person, but one still believes in something, do you think one
  can still pray?
- Yes, I believe so. I think everyone does that. I think that
  every single one of us, in quiet moments, prays without telling
  anybody about it. When there is grief, they pray. When they
  experience joy, they give thanks.

- I believe it is like this; all people have good deeds in them.
  It is not only some people who can pray. The whole population

  occasions.

- I wish that everyone that watches this video must not regard me
  as the greatest in the world. I have always felt small,
  unimportant and never big. I have gone to work with my will.
    - And I have gone to work as the small man I am.
    - I am not as big as people says that I am.
    - Some believe that I am a miracle man. That is wrong, I am

- Thank you very much for what you are doing for others.
