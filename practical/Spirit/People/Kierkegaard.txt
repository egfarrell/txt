### The Cross
- He sees Christ not as a concept but as an offense — something
  that wounds reason before it enlightens it.
- For him:
    - objective systems evade decision
    - speculative theology avoids discipleship
    - the Cross confronts the individual alone
    - faith exists only under risk

- Kierkegaard’s theology survives Gethsemane because it refuses
  safety. Nothing is neutral, nothing is purely theoretical.
