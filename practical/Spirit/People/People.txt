### PERHAPS THE DEEPEST THREAD

Most people seek until they find an answer.

These people often continued seeking
even after they had found one.

They remained vulnerable to wonder,
surprise, correction, and mystery.

Not because they lacked convictions,
but because reality itself remained
larger than their convictions.
