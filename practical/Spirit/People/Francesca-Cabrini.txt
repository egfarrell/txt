
- I had an elderly friend whose husband met Mother Cabrini when
  he was a child in the early 1900s.  He suffered a ruptured
  appendix and the accompanying peritonitis.  This happened
  decades before antibiotics.  As he lay dying in the hospital,
  Mother Cabrini came into his room.  She told him that he would
  not die, but would grow up to save many lives.  He became
  a cardiologist and did just that.  I'm so happy that I can
  count this woman and her husband as my friends.  I miss both of
  them terribly, but I know they are doing good from heaven!
