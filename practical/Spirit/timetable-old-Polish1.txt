### typical Benedictine
    - 7:00 a.m.        VIGILS, the first communal prayer of the day
    - 7:30- 8:30       Lectio Divina
    - 8:30             LAUDS, (Morning Prayer)
    - 9:00             Silent Breakfast
    - 9:30- 12:30      Class and study for the formation group; assigned work for others
    - 13:00            CONVENTUAL MASS
    - 14:00 p.m.       Lunch with the guests
    - 14:30- 17:00     Assigned work
    - 17:00- 18:30     Study, rest, or exercise
    - 18:30            Enda change: mass (Lectio Divina)
    - 19:00            VESPERS, (Evening Prayer)
    - 19:30            Dinner in Silence
    - 21:00- 21:30     Community recreation
    - 21:30            COMPLINE (Night Prayer)
