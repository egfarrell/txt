### Apr 2021:
    - Reading about this verse I found: Loses his life" refers to
      a Semitic phrase "_to trifle away one's life_". 
    - It means the loss is caused by foolish actions, not
      accident or happenstance. "Life" is from the Greek root
      word psuche. It means breath of life, but can also refer to
      one's _soul_. (see paragraph at very top of this file)
