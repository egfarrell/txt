
### Poster at Franciscan Friary, Multyfarnahm
    - John Bradburne
    - Secular Franciscan Order
    - Beloved friend of the Lepers
    - John Bradburne was born in England in 1921. He served with
      the Gurkhas in Malaya and Burma during the Second World
      War. After the War he tried his vocation in various
      religious Orders. His continued search for God's will led
      him through Europe and to the Holy Land, mostly on foot.
      Inspired by St Francis he became a member of the Secular
      Franciscan Order (Third Order). In 1962 he went to Zimbabwe
      to "seek a cave", as he said, so as to devote his life to
      contemplation. There he befriended the Irish Franciscans.
    - In 1969 he made his way to a leper settlement at Mutemwa.
      There for ten years he cared for the lepers with great
      love, seeking respect and justice for them. On the night of
      Sunday the 2nd September 1979 he was abducted and
      assassinated. Since his death there have been many signs of
      his sanctity.
    - Many have turned to God through his extraordinary example.
      He wrote thousands of poems. One of these poems, entitled
      Strange Vagabond, has these words;
        - God's love within you is your native land,
        - So search none other, never more depart,
        - For you are homeless save God keeps your heart

    - (Painting by Martin Krist Yarabek -- Slovenia and Waterford, July 2015).
