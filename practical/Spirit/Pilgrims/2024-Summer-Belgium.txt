### CAESTRE
    - Our lady
    - PRAYER TO OUR LADY OF GRACE
    - O Mary, who has been invoked for so many centuries at
      CAESTRE, under the title of Our Lady of Grace, cast a look
      of kindness on the pilgrims who kneel before you with so
      much confidence, listen to their prayers.
    - Heaven salutes you under this title “full of grace”, we
      unite in these homages because you are the first of
      creatures and the most pleasant, O Mother of Jesus.
    - The earth never ceases to invoke you because divine graces
      descend through you: Through these three Virgin martyrs
      whom you wanted to honor in Caestre, make us understand the
      value of suffering; through the blind Knight whom you have
      healed, make us understand your power; through these little
      children whom you have called back to life so that baptism
      may be given to them, make us understand the value of
      supernatural life.
    - O Our Lady of Grace, watch over us, intercede for us. So be
      it.
    - (300 days of indulgence)
    - The Sovereign Pontiff Gregory XVI has granted a plenary
      indulgence to all the faithful who will make the
      pilgrimage, recite 5 Our Fathers and 5 Hail Marys in front
      of the statue and take communion in a church of their
      choice during the week of the Visitation
    - + Achille Card. LIENART, Bishop of Lille

### Meditation in Caestres church
    - Meditation around the paintings in the chapel
    - Table 1
        - 3 virgin and pious sisters, natives of England, of
          princely origin together decided in the year 819 in
          Rome to leave. Landing at Mardyck, Cassel saw them
          pass.
            - Where is my life path taking me?
            - Where does my path of faith take me?
            - Am I alone on my journey of faith?
    - Table 2.
        - Their hearts seized with fear, wandering with _timid
          steps_ in these unknown places, a lumberjack guides
          them, when from a distance, 3 bandits with sinister
          plans insist on following them in the middle of the
          forests.
            - What are my fears?
            - What are the places that scare me or, on the
              contrary, reassure me?
    - Table 3
        - In the shade of these woods, the innocent virgins
          holyly revived their failing strength. Suddenly, the
          murderers, with death in their eyes, thrust the
          homicidal dagger into their breasts.
            - ➤ Have I ever felt a threat to myself or a loved
              one so great that it made me foresee death?
            - ➤ In these circumstances who came to my aid, to my
              rescue?
                - (Who? JESHUA)
    - Table 4.
        - In a neighboring castle, lived in opulence a pious
          knight blind from birth. Struck by the cries of birds,
          he sends a valet who recoils in horror at the terrible
          crime.
            - What are the elements that prevent me from seeing,
              in particular from seeing the miseries of the
              world?
            - Do I ever back away from them?
            - (Yes... all the time)
    - Table 5
        - The pious knight who is dismayed by this crime suddenly
          has himself taken there. He cries, he prostrates
          himself, he touches each wound and his fingers full of
          blood wet his two eyes which open at the moment.
            - In these circumstances who opened my eyes?
            - The Jesuits did, Ana etc
    - Table 6
        - The happy old man, wanting celestial favor, to preserve
          the memory for ages to come, in this place dedicated
          this humble and modest temple to the Virgin who knows
          how to console and bless.
            - What are the places that remember the presence of
              action, of the Virgin Mary?
                - (The pilgrimage sites... Lourdes etc)
            - When I go there, how do I feel?
            - (I feel PEACE)

### Moorslede
    - (End of the pilgrimage in 2024) July 12, 2024
    - Was a leaflet in the Church
    - 'The fruit of silence is prayer, the fruit of prayer is
      faith, the fruit of faith is love and the fruit of love is
      service.' Today I decided to speak about silence and
      loneliness, mutual love and prayer. In all this, as Mother
      Teresa asked me, I would pray with the sisters and reflect
      on the bread of life with which we must nourish ourselves.
    - In their constitutions I read about silence and being alone
      with God:
        - 'As contemplatives we are sent among people simply to
          be a sign of God's presence. Because we as
          contemplatives are called in a special way to lead
          a life of prayer and contemplation, of penance and of
          proclamation, silence and solitude occupy an extremely
          important place in our lives. Solitude is the home of
          contemplatives and silence is their language.'
    - Above the garden gate of the St. Sixtus Trappist Abbey in
      Flanders' Westhoek you can read the words: Beata Solitudo,
      Sola Beatitudo (O blissful loneliness, only bliss).
      Solitude is necessary to find and experience happiness.
      This happiness is nothing other than a life in unity with
      God and therefore with man. This presupposes silence. Only
      a small number of spiritual pilgrims begin their journey
      from the silence of God. Most begin their spiritual
      wanderings a road full of obstacles to reach the sea of
      silence after much searching.
    - It is in this sea that we experience the depth of our
      limitations and sinfulness, but also the presence of love.
      We experience Christ who overcame the hopeless loneliness,
      isolation and rejection of the world. Only silence also
      makes it possible for us to understand the deep meaning of
      words. Beyond the silence, even the word of God can be
      swallowed up in the cacophony of the modern world. In order
      to listen to the word of God in Sacred Scripture and to
      feel the breath of his presence in nature and in the words
      of people gathered together in love in his name, we must
      make it quiet inside and outside ourselves.
    - The Madonna House in Canada now has 22 homes in North
      America, Canada, the Caribbean, Brazil, Liberia, Ghana,
      England and France. Half of these houses are 'listening
      houses'. Lay people who are linked to Jesus and the Church
      for life through the three evangelical councils want to
      live contemplatively in the world of the poor. Homes where
      they listen to the suffering Jesus who comes to express his
      thirst for peace and happiness from man's inner poverty and
      misery. But to listen it must be silent in our hearts, or
      we only hear our own voice and the noise that closes our
      heart to others and to God. Seclusion and silence go hand
      in hand. This inner
    - ... loneliness is not so much a fact or a desire to be
      alone. Rather, it is the loneliness of the total detachment
      that sets our hearts free
