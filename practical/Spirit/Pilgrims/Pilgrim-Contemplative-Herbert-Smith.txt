
### Warsaw July 2024
    - Feeling down... for the 3rd time I abandoned
      a 'Labre-style' pilgrimage.

    - Found audio note tonight:
        - When opportunities are equal in other respects, that
          work should be chosen, which promises more LASTING
          RESULTS.

    - Audio note:
        - Everything can be equal. The pilgrim should choose
          a ministry in which his WHOLE BEING and personality are
          more fully developed.
        - The Vatican II says work should give the opportunity to
          develop their talents and their personalities in the
          very exercise of their work.
        - The kingdom too, with the will of God and the need of
          others, does not call the pilgrim to a work `WHICH IS
          HIS OWN CRUCIFIXION`.
        - He should engage in work which humanistically develops
          God image in him and so gives _glory to God_.
        - Christ `did not crucify himself` and neither should we
          crucify ourselves.
        - Rather, we should match our best personal resources to
          the most critical apostolic needs and thus we will grow
          even as we serve.
        - And then another one, the pilgrim _ought not to dread_
          that his ministry will lead him to the fate in which
          Christ's ministry led him to be treated as a criminal,
          despised and murdered without giving any cause for it
          but his goodness and good works.
        - Do not dread to share this fate which Jesus aspired to
          it.

    - Audio note:
        - Divine inspiration is a PRIVILEGED way of determining
          one's ministry.
        - God speaks to us not only by the must of our
          conscience, but by _interiorly attracting_ us to
          special service by way of INVITATION.
        - Invitation is key, such as normally the case of
          a pilgrim who is drawn to the religious life or the
          priesthood.
        - France 2002 was definitely an 'invitation' ... but
          Physics or Labre??



### More Feb 2023 prayer
    - Page 142... subtle temptation: false humility
    - Trial can cloud over memories of God so darkly that they
      fail to light the way out of present difficulties. And yet
      G-d does not repeat his gifts every time they are needed.
      He requires the Pilgrim to keep them alive, by making
      recurrent pilgrimages OF MEMORY back to the springs of his
      _first graces_.
    - These graces are given to be stored in the soul, and
      returned to often and drunk deeply, to ward off temptations
      even before they strike.

    - Asked for more help and insight: p. 136
        - 'untempered zeal with its imprudent activism and
          consequent exhaustion has often provoked him to act and
          speak uncharitably'

    - 'The pilgrim is PATIENT in the knowledge that he is FAR
      FROM BEING ABLE TO ENDURE SUFFERING in the way it was
      endured by Jeshua, the fullness of the Godhead bodily (Col.
      2.9).
    - He knows nonetheless that by contemplating the Sufferer who
      saved him, he can suffer with the suffering that saves.'
    - It is Jeshua in his Death-Resurrection who continues to
      build up his hope - hope not only in Jeshua, but hope in
      his own cooperation with G-d's purposes, and hope for the
      growth of others.
    - At the very moment Jeshua is about to go down to death in
      complete defeat he says "It is finished (acomplished)".
      What looked to men like total failure to reach the goal and
      complete the plan of growth is revealed to be total victory
      in G-d's plan. Jeshua's mortal work is done. G-d will do
      the rest."

### Future eyes:
    - The pilgrim begins to see how the theological virtures of
      faith, hope and love, though they are trained on God, help
      the pilgrim to penetrate into the depths of the created
      domain. Christian commpassion is teaching him to see othes
      more the way they are to become than the way they are.
    - He is learning to see others through hope, and to love them
      in hope. Where others see only what is, he see what will
      be. ```
    - Only when the broken and depraved look into his eyes and
      see mirrored there their own future glory, does he feel he
      has served them well. ```
    - His faith is a power that reveals to them their own
      IDENTITY.
    - Otherwise, his faith, hope and love fail them.
    - But he is escaping such failures; his faith has its
      farseeing vision; it is alive with the hope it has for
      others.
    - G-d's love for humanity leads to the resurrection and the
      deification of all people which has already germinated in
      the risen body of Jesus.
