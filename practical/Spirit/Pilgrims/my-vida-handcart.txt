The problem described is vibration loosening of the nut, not rotation of the bolt in the hole. The standard engineering fixes are methods that increase resistance to self-loosening.

### 1. Use a nylon-insert lock nut (nyloc)
    - Replace the existing nut with an M6 nyloc nut.
    - Mechanism:
    - a nylon ring inside the nut grips the threads → friction prevents vibration loosening.
    - Configuration:
    - bolt head → washer → handle tube → frame bracket → washer → nyloc nut
    - This is the most common solution for carts, bicycles, trailers, and similar equipment.

### 2. Use thread-locking compound
    - Apply a medium-strength threadlocker such as Loctite 243 (blue).
    - Procedure:
    - remove nut
    - apply a drop to the bolt threads
    - reinstall and tighten
    - Result: the adhesive fills thread gaps and prevents vibration loosening but remains removable with tools.

### 3. Use two nuts (jam nut method)
    - If a nyloc nut is unavailable:
    - tighten the first nut normally
    - tighten a second nut against it
    - The two nuts lock together and resist rotation.
    - Arrangement:
    - bolt → washer → frame → nut → second nut

### 4. Add serrated or spring washers
    - Useful as an additional measure.
    - Types:
    - serrated flange nut
    - split (spring) washer
    - toothed washer
    - These increase friction at the joint.
