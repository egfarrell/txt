To find a list of all Roman Catholic monasteries and convents in Belgium, you
can use the following methods and resources:

1. Official Church Websites:
   - The Catholic Church in Belgium (Église Catholique en Belgique): Their
     official website often has directories of religious houses, monasteries,
     and convents.
   - Diocesan Websites: Each diocese in Belgium may have a list of
     monasteries and convents within their jurisdiction. Examples include the
     Archdiocese of Mechelen-Brussels, the Diocese of Liège, and others.

2. Online Directories:
   - Cistercian Abbeys (Order of Cistercians): This site lists Cistercian
     monasteries worldwide, including Belgium.
   - Benedictine Confederation (OSB): For Benedictine monasteries, you can
     check their directory.
   - Franciscan Order: The Order of Friars Minor often lists their
     monasteries and convents on their official sites.

3. Pilgrimage Networks and Websites:
   - Santiago de Compostela Pilgrimage Routes (Camino de Santiago): Some
     routes pass through Belgium and their websites sometimes list
     accommodations, including monasteries and convents.
   - Belgian Pilgrimage Network: Websites dedicated to Belgian pilgrimage
     routes may also have lists of religious accommodations.

4. Guidebooks and Maps:
   - Pilgrimage Guidebooks: Specialized guidebooks for pilgrims often
     include information on monasteries and convents that welcome pilgrims.
   - Walking Maps and Apps: Apps and maps designed for pilgrims often
     include points of interest, including religious houses.

5. Contact Religious Orders Directly:
   - Reach out to the major religious orders directly via email or phone to ask
     for a list of their houses in Belgium.

Here are some specific websites to start with:
- [Église Catholique en Belgique](https://www.cathobel.be/)
- [Archdiocese of Mechelen-Brussels](https://www.catho-bruxelles.be/)
- [Order of Cistercians](https://www.ocist.org/)
- [Benedictine Confederation](https://www.osb.org/)

By using these resources, you should be able to compile a comprehensive list of
Roman Catholic monasteries and convents in Belgium to assist you on your
pilgrimage.
