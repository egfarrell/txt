
After five years struggling, a few significant things swing my
decision to try a 1.5 year pilgrimage now.

2020 Feb: The first appearance of:
### Mark 8:35: 
    - "For whoever wants to save their life will lose it, but
      whoever loses their life for me and for the gospel will
      save it."

That night on retreat in Sligo I promised Jeshua not to refuse
him anything. (A bit like mother teresa call within a call)

Then over the next 5 years, in some very unexpected places, the
words 'Benedict Labre' appeared out of nowhere when I was
specifically asking God was this my vocation. Appeared in
a Sermon, a book, a shrine, an unexpected conversation. Amazing.

And two other times, when I specifically asked for guidance, the
passage about Fools for Christ from St Paul (Corinthians) came up. Totally bizarre.

Also April 16 (Labre Feast day) also came up unexpectedly...
included the relics of St Bernadette in Longford... (who is also
April 16)

So, very tentatively, with the knowledge that I might be totally
mistaken, and also the possibility of mental illness or
personality disorder, I'm setting off.

But the core core resolution is this: I trust God totally with my
life. So even if this pilgrimage turns out to be incorrect and it
becomes appartent it was not a valid Inspiration, that is no
problem. I'll simply return and continue praying and continue
trying to be closer to God.
