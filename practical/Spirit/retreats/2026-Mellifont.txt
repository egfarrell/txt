### 10-Jan 2026 Sat
    - Reading 'Tramp for the Lord'
        - $MY_NOTES/practical/Spirit/People/Corrie-ten-Boom.txt
    - But then morph into a chat with GPT about VOCATIONS:
        - Some calls do not compete; they coexist and therefore
          `exhaust`.
        - The `strain` is not from division, but from truth
          refusing simplification.
        - When two grammars are true, neither permits `rest`
          through `absorption`.
        - A single vocation `offers peace` by exclusion; a double
          one demands `fidelity` without shelter.
        - Relief would be easy — but it would require forgetting
          something real.
        - Fixed identity is 'rest'; obedience often is not.
    - And when thoughts of pilgrimage come:
        - Treat `fidelity`, not absorption, as the success
          criterion
        - Don't ask at the end of the session:
            - “Did I feel unified? absorbed? peaceful?”
        - Ask instead:
            - “Did I stay?”
        - STAYING, UNDER NON-ABSORPTION, IS THE WORK.
