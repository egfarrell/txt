### Ruth Burrows: Contrition
    - "Through Him, With Him, In Him" Meditations on the
      Liturgical Cycle
    - p. 76 "Do Not Judge"
    - (I judge my other family members :( )
    - The hidden sin of judging, so common, so little regarded,
      is a tremendous obstacle to G-d's Love.
    - Because it is secret, because it seems such a natural thing
      to do, because we feel we see things right, we have no
      contrition; our consciences remain untroubled.
    - Whereas a thing of less moral significance, obvious to
      ourselves and others can rack us with guilt feelings which
      we mistake for real sorrow.
    - Contrition means 'never again'. With all my heart
      I renounce this habit. I will examine myself on it daily,
      _honestly, ruthlessly_...
