### Retreat Dec 2021
    - Ignation Retreat: Discernment in the Cave
        - https://spark.adobe.com/page/HYFrtgRBpJtcv/
    - Make a personal retreat.
    - Commit to progressing through each module over the course
      of four days. Each day, visit the module
        - when you wake up,
        - when you break for lunch,
        - and when you finish dinner.
        - See what resonates with you at these different times of
          day. See what sticks with you.

    - Day 1:
        - $MY_IMAGES/Spirit/Ignatius-in-Cave.jpg
        - What about this image of St. Ignatius in the cave
          strikes you? How does it resonate with your current
          experiences?
            - How he is oblivious to the Cave and the figure at
              the entrance to the cave. He is just writing,
              writing, creating his Spiritual Exercises.

        - A reading from the book of Lamentations
            - Scripture invites us to cry out to God, even in
              times of pain, struggle and confusion. A collection
              of poems, Lamentations records the cries of the
              people of Jerusalem in the aftermath of the city's
              destruction at the hands of the Babylonians. Read
              excerpts from Chapter 1 from Lamentations. Do you
              see yourself in this cry?
                > - The roads to Zion mourn, empty of pilgrims to
                >   her feasts.
                > - Jerusalem remembers in days of wretched
                >   homelessness, All the precious things she
                >   once had in days gone by.
                > - Look, O LORD, at the anguish I suffer! My
                >   stomach churns
                -   My Reaction to the lament:
                    - Gosh I don't think I have real faith or
                      don't know how I'd react if there was real
                      tradegy like that. My faith in G-d is still
                      surface after all these years... 
                    - I never fully committed to anything:
                      marriage, job, being a pilgrim, joining an
                      order, becoming a hermit... no real
                      commitment anywhere...

        - Contemplative Activity
            - Reflect on your experience from the last several
              days. Write your own cry of lamentation to God.
              Think about how you've been feeling these days and
              tell God about it. How does God respond?
