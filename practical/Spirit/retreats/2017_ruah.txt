# Ruah 2017

  had a few days in Ruah)

- Am I being cagey? YES

- The hour is specified in v.41: 
    - it is the hour of the delivering up of the Son of man 
    - ... the divinely appointed hour. 

- "Cup" is an Old Testament metaphor for punishment and divine
  retribution. It stands for the burden of human sin that Yeshua
  has to bear in undergoing his redemptive death.
    - (Its not a punishment no - read another quote by Ruth
      Burrows that says this understanding is wrong. 
    - There's not a vengeful God) 

- His ABBA, an intimate word for "father", indicates his
  awareness of an especial, unique relationship to God. 
    - It is so dramatically right that, for the first time in the
      gospel, that name is uttered. The utterance of this
      trustful ABBA already contains "thy will be done"...


- Peter had seen himself as an exception - he at least, will not
  be "scattered". Now he is told bluntly that, in fact, he is
  being singled out for failure. 
    - Of course, true to character, and true to disciple
      hard-heartedness, he is totally deaf to the word of the
      Lord. 
    - He is quite sure that he would die rather than deny Yeshua.
      The thorough `humanness` of Peter is his supreme redeeming
      quality. 
    - And one feels that this is precisely why the human Yeshua
      set such store by him.
    - (Remeber reading this in the sitting room beside Ania, and
      remember being really struck by it)
