### The Mind of Jesus
    - William Barclay
    - The temptations that came to Jesuha were temptations
      towards the selfish use of power, the temptation to set
      material benefits to the forefront of his programme, the
      temptation to seek quick resuts by sensational means, the
      temptation to win popularity by compromise and perhaps more
      than anything else: _the temptation to distrust himself and
      doubt his call_. 
    - To accept traditional expectations rather than listen to
      the voice of G-d. 
    - And his method of dealing with temptation was to submit
      every desire and every inclination to the word and will of
      G-d and to obey the verdict he found there.
