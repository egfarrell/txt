### The key 'markers'
    - How to find our 'markers' of our own unqiue Spirituality?

    1. Consider _early childhood / youth_ in terms of dreams,
       fantasies, desires, 'reachings out' natural to us...
        - Sneak out of the house at night, sit in the forest, sit
          in the castle... but afraid and turn back.
        - Reading the 'Mind Alive' encylopedias.
        - Tapping the computer keyboard on a magazine.

    2. What is the _gift_ we have? Of knowing, relating, feeling,
       leading 
        - Autism. Maths, Physics, Computers.
        - Short-term warmth.

    3. What is Our _Gospel_? What event or teaching? What gospel
       emphasis or theology? What manifestation of Jesus draws us
       and brings us back and back? Where do we rest settle find
       ourselves at home in the gospel? We can ask ourselves...
       What is The Gospel According to [Enda]?
        - Healing the bleeding woman, the little girl, the sick,
          the demionic. Gethsemene. The Cry from the Cross. The
          baptism at the Jordan. The temptation in the desert.

    4. A fourth marker of our spirituality is found in a _turning
       point_ in our lives—one that we did not bring about, but
       which we received. 
        - For Ignatius, this was being placed with the Son. For
          Burrows, it was the revelation that intimacy with God
          was possible. For me, it was God making known to me to
          choose my life as truly as I could, and He would bless
          it.
        - It might be something very different: surviving a loss,
          falling in love, a tragedy, a big disruption, receiving
          an illumination or calling, a life-threatening disease,
          becoming a parent, or being changed by the experience
          of the poor.
        - Finding oneself in a new place in life because of these
          turns—anything that resets our lives.
        - My turnings:
            - 1998: The Nullabour: living on the road, Nature, Physics
            - 2000: JVC year
            - 2001: Saddle Hill: Cancel Wedding 
            - 2006: L'Arche: dismembered by Ana 
            - 2008: Zambia flight cancelled
            - 2008: Autism diagnosis
            - 2008: The 'Physics Years'... hope
            - 2013/14: Health / stress during PhD
            - 2018: Disaster teaching, but cling to Jeshua
            - 2020: Blood during the PhD
            - 2020: Relationship breaks
            - 2024: Freeze, shame, defeated, driving car in Goldap

        5. the fifth and and final marker to look for is what is
           one's life symbol? To what symbol do we find ourselves
           repeatedly being drawn? Something that seems to grasp
           the whole of us more than thought or words could.
                - Something less that we grasp and more that
                  grasps us. Something like Ignatius' "Knight in
                  the service of a king", or Ruth's boat floating
                  on the dark Ocean or my river at one time in
                  its whole reality. A life symbol has the power
                  to grasp the whole of us on all levels of
                  ourselves: mind, heart, feeling, Body, Soul,
                  faith and the whole of life's experiences.
                  Because of this power, a life symbol comes
                  closest to enabling us to know and express
                  one's lived relationship to Mystery. 
            - Yes: for me, the symbol is a `PILGRIM`!
