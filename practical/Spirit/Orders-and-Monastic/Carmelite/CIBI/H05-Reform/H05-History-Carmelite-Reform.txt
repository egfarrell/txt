### Reading list
    - 1976  The Collected Works of St Teresa of Avila (3 volumes)                 Kavanaugh and Rodriguez
    - 1984  Carmelite Studies 3: Centenary of St Teresa                           Sullivan, OCD, John.
    - 1988  The Carmelites: History of Brothers of Our Lady of Mount Carmel       Vol 1, 11, 111 Smet, Joachim
    - 1989  `The Avila of St Teresa: Religious Reform in 16th-century City`         Bilinkoff, Jodi.
    - 2000  `Teresa of Avila`                                                       Williams, Rowan
    - 2002  John of the Cross and Teresa of Avila: Mystical Knowing and Selfhood  Howells, Edward.
    - 2005  Entering Teresa of Avila’s Interior Castle                            Ahlgren, Gillian

    - You might read St Teresa’s letters, which give insights
      into her mind and the reality of daily life:
        - 1951  The Letters of St Teresa of Jesus. Two Vols.                          Peers, E. Alison. (Older translation)
        - 2001  The Collected `Letters of St. Teresa` of Avila. Vols I and II.          Kavanaugh, Kieran (Modern translation)
