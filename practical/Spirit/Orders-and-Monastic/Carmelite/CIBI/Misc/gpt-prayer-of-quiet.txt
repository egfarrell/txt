Let me rephrase Teresa of Avila's words in modern
English and explain it.

### Rephrased:

- "We can tell whether the peaceful, quiet state in prayer comes
  from God's spirit or if we are trying to create it on our own.
  Here’s how: When God gives us a sense of devotion and we try to
  move ourselves into a peaceful, quiet state using our own
  effort, the quiet we create doesn't have lasting power. It
  fades away quickly, and when it leaves, it often leaves us
  feeling dry and disconnected."

### Explanation:

In this passage, Teresa is helping us understand the difference
between quiet in prayer that is given by God and quiet that we
try to produce ourselves.

1. God's quiet: When God gives us a sense of peace in prayer,
   it’s a deep, lasting experience. It's not something we control
   or create. It comes as a gift and leaves us feeling
   spiritually nourished.

2. Our own quiet: When we try to create quiet through our own
   efforts, it lacks the depth and lasting power of God's gift.
   We might try to make ourselves feel peaceful by focusing on
   techniques or forcing ourselves to be still, but it doesn’t
   produce a lasting effect. Instead, after the quiet fades, we
   might feel spiritually dry or disconnected, as if the effort
   didn’t really lead to a deep or genuine experience.

Teresa is distinguishing between the "quiet" that is a true gift
from God and the "quiet" we might try to manufacture ourselves.
The latter doesn’t lead to lasting spiritual growth and may leave
us feeling dry or arid afterward. She encourages discernment to
help recognize which kind of quiet we're experiencing.
