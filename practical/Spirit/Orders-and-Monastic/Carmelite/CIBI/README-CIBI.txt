### Referencing
    - Referencing the module itself: Many students/learners like
      to make reference to the module or modules they have
      studied in their questionnaires. If you quote the text of
      the module you have studied in a questionnaire, you should
      acknowledge this by indicating the source and giving the
      unit number in a footnote, e.g., 'H02, The Origins of the
      Carmelite Family, unit 4.' Even if you do not quote the
      module directly in your answers to the questionnaire, when
      your answer depends significantly on the text of the module
      you have studied, you should indicate this in a footnote,
      but you may also indicate this by mentioning the relevant
      module unit number at the beginning a sentence: 'As unit
      4 of module S07 indicated, ...,' or 'Unit 3 of the H02
      module makes it clear that ... .'

### Teresa dates
    - `1515`  Born
    - `1537`  Makes religious profession at the Incarnation
    - `1554`  Profound conversion
    - `1562`  Founds monastery of the Reform, St. Joseph’s Avila
    - `1567`  Meets John of the Cross
    - `1582`  Dies at Alba de Tormes at age sixty-seven.
