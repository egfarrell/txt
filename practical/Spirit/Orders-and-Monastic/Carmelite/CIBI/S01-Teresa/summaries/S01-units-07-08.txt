### Unit 7: The Aqueduct (3)

- Mystical Way: Entered with the third image of the Aqueduct,
  relates to the `fifth mansions` in The Interior Castle.

- Aqueducts: Passages for water, historically used in Rome.

- Third Water of Irrigation: Uses water from a river or spring.
  Minimal effort needed, God aids the gardener.

- Faculties: Intellect, will, and memory play a role; prayer
  involves a "sleep" of these faculties.

- Intellect and Will: Less active, God is more active; experience
  described as "sleep."

- Difference from Second Water: More consolation and joy; greater
  detachment from earthly things.

- Mystical Experience: Reflects second aim of Carmelite life,
  reducing relevance of earthly attachments.

- God's Teaching: Teresa learned through God's guidance how
  faculties unite with God.

- Explanations of Mystical Graces: Teresa grapples with
  expressions of her mystical experiences.

- Writing Style: Mixes detailed descriptions with exclamations of
  thanksgiving.

- Faculties' Role: Quiet, allow God's action; involves
  a "delicate and heavy cross."

- Mystical Literature Themes: Includes love, longing for God,
  desire to show devotion through suffering.

### Unit 8: The Rain (4)

- Fourth Water (Rain): Person is entirely passive, experiences
  God’s grace; related to `Sixth Mansions` in The Interior
  Castle.

- Higher States: Beyond Teresa's early understanding, expands in
  later works.

- Prayer and Union: Experiences of high prayer, characterized by
  ecstasy and union with God.

- Rapture and Levitation: Physical experiences described with
  spiritual intensity.

- Sense of Desolation: Follows graces, a return to normal life
  with new spiritual insights.

- Tears and Ecstasy: As signs of joy, grace, and spiritual
  evolution.

- Perspective on Worldly Things: Detachment from material
  desires, sense of smallness in worldly matters.

- Mystical Teaching: Emphasizes graces over self-indulgence or
  techniques.

- Role of Imagination: Often uncontrolled, but poses no harm.

### Discussion: Rapture 

- Rapture Description: Physical stillness, intense experience of
  divine presence.

- Challenges with Understanding: Requires divine enlightenment to
  explain; a new view on worldly things.

- Acknowledgement of Past Faults: Spiritual insights reveal minor
  faults in a bright light.

- Lessons from Rapture: Experience renews energy, strengthens
  courage for virtuous living.

- Final Reflections: Experiences suggest there are even higher
  levels of spiritual ascent.
