# Module S01 Questionnaire 1 (wordcount: 200)
    1.  Give two examples of the way the `Inquisition` affected
        Teresa’s life.
    2.  Give three significant `events` in the life of Teresa
        with their dates.  Why did you choose these? (Note there
        is no wrong or right answer to his question).
    3.  Give a few points about the physical `health` of Teresa.
    4.  What circumstances `prompted` Teresa to undertake the
        process of reforming her Order?
    5.  What were the main features of the `reform`?
    6.  Give three aspects of Teresa’s `personality` that came to
        the fore in her reform work.
    7.  Note two problems that Teresa encountered as a foundress
        precisely arising from being a `woman`, rather than
        a cleric or a bishop.
    8.  What was the significance of Teresa’s first meeting with
        `John of the Cross`?
    9.  ‘`Contemplative` prayer (oracion mental) in my opinion
        is nothing else than a close sharing between friends; it
        means taking time frequently to be alone with him whom we
        know loves us.’ Life8:5 and quoted in Catechism of the
        Catholic Church n. 2709. Give three significant words
        from this, giving reasons for your choice.
    10.  What aspects of `Spain` and its cultural history at the
         time of Teresa have particularly struck you?


### Handy
    - In the book of her `Life` (chs 32-36) Teresa had already
      written how she had embarked on reform.
