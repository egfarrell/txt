### Mark 1
    - written for _existing_ disciples, already some experience
    - this Gospel all about going deeper into the mystery 
    - Mary Magdalene: the first person to receive and proclaim
      the good news that Jeshua has risen from the dead, felt
      called, felt compelled spiritually, to into _silence_.
      According to ancient Christian tradition she went into the
      desert for 30 years.
    - Perhaps it suggests how we could understand the very end of
      Marks gospel. The very first witness, felt called to go
      into 30 years of silence. A long period of profound
      silence. And that was a greater contribution than anything
      else she could have done.
    - this is a gospel that bring us face-to-face wtih the
      mystery. It leads us into silence.
    - Marks gospel requires silence, it is a very profoundly
      written document. 
    - And the silence about the resurrection is perhaps the
      strongest indication this is the right way to approach
      Mark. Taken into silence, be silent with this document.
    - To finish with a quote from John of the Cross: 
        > God the father spoke one word,  
        > that word was his Son Jeshua Christus,
        > and that word he spoke in the eternal silence.
