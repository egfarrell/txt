### Mental health needed
    - Third, don’t blame the nuns. The qualifications of
      sufficient physical and mental health are not arbitrary or
      cold or "mean".
    - Cloistered life is demanding, both physically and
      psychologically. It’s meant to be a challenge.
    - Someone who has significant struggles with either her body
      or _her mind_ would be overwhelmed by this challenge.
    - If this is you, the most loving thing the nuns can do is to
      say their way of life is not for you.
