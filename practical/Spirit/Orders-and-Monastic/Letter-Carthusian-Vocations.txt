Dear Vocations Director,

I am a 50-year-old Irishman very interested in making a vocations
enquiry to the Carthusians. This is something that has been
building for many years but maybe I should sketch a few details.

In my external life, I've worked mainly in Computers and Science,
but also spent some years as a care worker in homeless services
and disabilities. (A hybrid career). At the moment I'm
a part-time Researcher for a Physics institute (remote). I'm in
very good health - swim, cycle and jog. I had two long-term
relationships when I was younger but did not feel called to
marriage. I've been single for four years and financially
independent and during that time a sense of call has been getting
stronger and stronger. But I suppose my 'inner' life has taken
a 'parallel' journey over the past 30 years.

In 1998 I took a break from IT & Banking and cycled across
a desert in Australia for four months. That changed everything
- those months of silence, 'desert pilgrimage', sleeping outside,
had a huge effect on me. Later I saw a line from Hosea that
helped me understand it: Therefore, behold, I will allure her and
bring her into the wilderness, and speak tenderly to her heart.
(Hosea 2:14)

After Australia, I stopped Banking and spent 1 year full-time in
a Jesuit volunteer Community. I was introduced to Ignatian
Spirituality and working for social justice.

After the Jesuits, aged 30, I then went on a 7 month pilgrimage
to Santiago de Compostella. During those months again I had some
deep experiences of 'Peace' and I 'call' - but I can't really put
it in words. Later I read the phrase "Deep calls to Deep" (Psalm
42) and that's what I felt during the pilgrimage.

Many years went by (I switched to Homelessness work, and did
a Science degree) but all the time I found myself spending more
time in silence in front of the cross. One big moment that has
changed me was when I joined a L'Arche community to live with
people with disabilites. I found the social interaction very
overwhelming and left after 2 months - but it really opened my
eyes to my weakness and need for God's Love and help.

Since 2020, whenever I spend time in silent prayer in front of
the cross, I have a very strong sense of "Follow Me" and one
particular gospel passage keeps cropping up again and again:

"For whoever wants to save their life will lose it, but whoever
loses their life for me and for the gospel will save it." (Mark
8:35)

I feel a deep tug, call to give myself to Jesus, to live a life
of hidden prayer, getting to know God that I met in the desert
more and more.

These are a few details. Sometimes it's hard to explain the
attraction of the Carthusians (I have read many of the books 'The
Call of Silent Love', 'Way of Silent Love', 'When Silence
Speaks.." and even more modern ones like 'An infinity of litte
hours'). Part of me feels nervous even making this enquiry! But
part of me feels a call of Silent Love.

In any case, thank you for reading my enquiry. Feel free to email
anytime if you would like any other details or if you had any
recommendations or advice.

Best regards, Enda Farrell

ps: I still go on walking pilgrimages, but only short ones
nowadays! This is a recent one to Częstochowa, Poland
