From the beginning of Christianity spread the idea that true
Christian life and earthly pleasures can not go together.
Everyone was convinced that the desert was the ideal place
not only to escape the temptations but also to the
lukewarmness of the world. Therefore a monk lived alone. But
they were rarely as alone as they wished. As the fame of
their sanctity attracted many students. Soon resulted in
a community of monks who lived together in monasteries. The
monks who have dedicated their lives to God thank us in
advance. that we respect the solitude in which they can
pray. And that we respect the silence. Therefore we will not
be allowed to enter.
