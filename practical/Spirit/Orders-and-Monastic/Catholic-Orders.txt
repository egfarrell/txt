### Franciscans
    - Actually consists of three orders.
    - The First Order
        - comprises of priests and lay brothers who have sworn to
          lead a life of prayer, preaching, and penance.
        - 3 branches:
            - the Friars Minor             (O.F.M.)
            - the Friars Minor Conventual  (O.F.M. Conv.)
            - the Friars Minor Capuchin    (O.F.M. Cap.)
