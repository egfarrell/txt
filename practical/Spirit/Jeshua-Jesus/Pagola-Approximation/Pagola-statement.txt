ecleSALia January 7 , 2008

- 'A WORD OF ENCOURAGEMENT'
- Statement by José Antonio Pagola about the writings against his book “Jesús.
  Historical approach”
- JOSE ANTONIO PAGOLA
- SAN SEBASTIAN (GUIPUZCOA).

- ECLESALIA , 01/07/08.-In the first days of this new year of
  2008, various writings against my book «Jesus. Historical
  approximation»and against me. There are five different firms,
  but they repeat the same condemnation in unison, even using the
  same phrases and expressions. 
    - Naturally, there are many who ask me how I am, how I am
      living it and what is happening. To all those who, at this
      time, are interested in me and suffer with me, I want to
      say a word of encouragement.

1. The first thing I want to express to you is that, deep down,
   all this is doing me good. It purifies me, it forces me to
   cling to Jesus and it is leading me to identify myself
   a little more with him. 
    - If I manage to live it from Jesus, this can be a great
      grace for me, the great opportunity to give myself totally
      to him and follow him faithfully as far as necessary.

2. I am trying to live this whole process from within. Few times
   had she prayed certain psalms with such truth. He had rarely
   celebrated the Eucharist so identified with that Jesus "given
   up" by others. 
    - I am repeating Psalm 86 a lot: at this moment it seems
      written especially for me. 
    - During the day, I repeat those short invocations that
      appear in the Gospels and that help me to live united with
      Jesus. "Lord, you know everything. You know I love you". "I
      will follow you wherever you go." "Father, into your hands
      I commend my spirit". «Father, if it is possible, pass this
      chalice from me. But do not do what I want, but what you
      want»… 
    - In short, I do what I can. In Jesus I find strength and
      peace. What does it matter if they consider me a heretic
      and an Arian? Only God, that God incarnate in Jesus, knows
      what is in my heart.

3. Something that I am working on a lot within myself these days
   are the feelings towards those who condemn me. 
    - I am listening from within the words of Jesus to his
      followers: «Do not judge anyone... Do not condemn anyone.
      Excuse me». I know well the feelings of Jesus. That's why
      I pray for those who reject me.
    - I do it with names and surnames. I really think, deep down,
      they don't know what they're doing. I do not want to let
      into my heart reactions, suspicions, prejudices or feelings
      that would never come out of the heart of Jesus. 
    - I ask him to help me shed some light, human sanity and
      evangelical good sense on this type of conflict that, to
      a large extent, arises because of the profound crisis that
      we are all suffering, without knowing exactly how to move
      towards it. a future more faithful to the Gospel.

4. I want to live all this from an attitude of conversion to God.
   Although very important, I believe that our main problem is
   not the theological precision in the formulation of the
   Church's doctrine. The first thing is to awaken and strengthen
   our conversion to God, closely following in the footsteps,
   attitudes and spirit of that dear Jesus in whom he has
   incarnated and revealed himself. 
    - I believe that by fostering mutual disqualification and
      condemnation among ourselves, we are not moving towards the
      conversion that we need in the Church.

5. Naturally, I am suffering. It is normal. I suffer, above all,
   seeing the sisters I live with, my loved ones, my friends and
   friends suffer. 
    - Sometimes I don't know what to tell them, or how to explain
      what is happening. I see some of them bewildered and
      embarrassed. My soul breaks. 
    - I also think about what the Bishop of Tarazona and those
      who condemn me may soon suffer, at least if they read and
      listen to what is being said against them. 
    - Is this kind of suffering necessary? Is the suffering
      required by our conversion to Jesus? I do not know. I want
      to live it trying to humanize it and direct it towards the
      search for a Church that is more faithful to the gospel.

6. I suffer by living at this moment a strange experience that
   I had never known before. 
    - I feel that some sectors of the Church want to silence my
      voice and turn it off. 
    - According to them, it hurts the Church. 
    - I sincerely want to listen to them to see if they help me
      better adjust my message to the spirit of the gospel of
      Jesus. 
    - For now, all this does not discourage me but stimulates me.
      Jesus is leading me to love the Church more and more. For
      this reason, I am not satisfied with any Church. I want to
      see her more faithful to Jesus every day, more filled with
      his Spirit. 
    - For this reason, I will continue committed to this task,
      listening to those who help me live in a more evangelical
      way and contributing, for my part, my little "mustard
      seed". 
    - Finally, Jesus is giving me strength to love the Church
      more than _my own peace of mind_, my image or my personal
      prestige.

7. Meanwhile, these days I am preparing my spirit to respond to
   so much attack and condemnation. I want to find the right
   evangelical tone and good, clear, constructive and hopeful
   words. 
    - The important thing for me is not to defend my book. 
    - It is the much-loved fruit of many years of reflection and
      meditation, but it is still a modest contribution to the
      enormous task of conversion that awaits the Church in the
      coming years, if it wants to survive among us. 
    - What I'm looking for is that theologians and bishops are
      not the ones who close the doors to simple people to meet
      Jesus Christ, the only one who can save our Church. 
    - I will strive to show my human, Christian and theological
      truth with my life, more than with my writings. 
    - (Eclesalia Informativo authorizes and recommends the
      dissemination of its articles.

- Jose Antonio Pagola
