### A Good G-d
    - When we understand G-d as an absolute power who rules and
      imposes himself by the force of his law, we have a religion
      based on harshness, merits and punishment.
    - When we experience G-d as goodness and mercy, we have
      a religion based on trust. G-d does not terrify us with his
      power and majesty; he entices us with goodness and
      closeness.
    - We can trust him. Yeshua said this in a thousand ways to
      the sick, suffering, undesirables and sinners: G-d is for
      those who need him to be good.
