LISTEN TO THE RIVER SPEAK
Translation by Diarmuid Johnson.

We went walking by night                              We went out walking in the night,
A woman and three men                                     a woman and three men,
And the river spoke to itself incessantly             the river babbling away to itself,
And though there was much I could not grasp               and though I didnt not understand the half of it
I knew it was utterly sincere                         I knew it was wholly in earnest-
And I knew not a drop of what it said was conceit         I knew there was no false note
But the lucid dialogue proper to water.               in all that flow of talk,
                                                          those waterwords clean and clear.


But again and again                                   I was spouting my foolishness
I set incoherence spinning through the night,             into the night without cease
And with empty words                                  wearing a flippant mask,
Like a bright mask worn falsely                           of words without worth,
Over the soul's face                                  false on the face of my soul;
I deceived two of my companions                           dazzling the two beside me
Hiding raw wounds from them                           so they would not see my gaping wounds,
Keeping the ache to myself.                               would not hear me groaning.


But now that I am alone                               But now that I walk alone,
Let my soul be bare and unclothed                         let my soul go bare and uncovered;
And I will speak true to myself                       I will speak to myself
As the unconceited river spoke                            as the river spoke, undeceiving,
When it raised a poem to the mountains                when it rose a poem to the mountains
With its great flood                                      in its own weight of water,
When it resisted musical heresy                       spurning false music
And sang nothing but the essence of water.                with its own clear voice.
