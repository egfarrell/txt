Examination of Conscience: “Have I Died to Self and Centred My Life on Christ?”

1. Relationship with God
2. Love and Service of Others
3. Detachment and Simplicity
4. Humility and Surrender
5. Witness and Mission
6. Use of Gifts and Time
7. Openness to the Holy Spirit


### 1. Relationship with God
    - Is Christ truly at the centre of my thoughts, desires, and decisions?
    - Do I seek intimacy with God daily—prayer, Scripture, Sacraments?
    - Do I surrender my will to God, or do I insist on my own plans?
    - Have I let go of self-reliance, trusting in God’s providence and mercy?
    - Is my love for God evident in how I use my time and resources?

### 2. Love and Service of Others
    - Do I love and forgive others as Christ forgives me?
    - Do I serve others sacrificially, or only when convenient?
    - Am I present and attentive to the needs of family, friends, colleagues, strangers?
    - Do I put the needs and dignity of others before my own comfort and pride?
    - Have I harboured resentment, indifference, or superiority over others?

### 3. Detachment and Simplicity
    - Am I attached to possessions, status, comfort, or pleasure more than to Christ?
    - Do I seek fulfilment and security in material things, achievements, or others’ approval?
    - Am I willing to let go of my preferences, comforts, and reputation for the sake of the Gospel?
    - Have I chosen simplicity and generosity over self-indulgence and excess?

### 4. Humility and Surrender
    - Am I open to correction, or do I defend my ego and reputation?
    - Do I recognise my dependence on God, or act as if I am self-sufficient?
    - Do I take credit for good, or do I give glory to God?
    - Do I accept suffering, inconvenience, or criticism for Christ’s sake?

### 5. Witness and Mission
    - Does my life point others to Christ—by words, actions, choices?
    - Am I willing to be misunderstood, rejected, or unpopular for being faithful to Christ?
    - Have I avoided opportunities to witness to my faith out of fear or comfort?
    - Do I make sacrifices for the growth of God’s kingdom, or only for my own success?

### 6. Use of Gifts and Time
    - Do I use my talents and time for God’s glory, or for my own advancement?
    - Have I withheld my gifts, energy, or resources from the service of God or neighbour?
    - Do I ask God to guide how I use what He has entrusted to me?

### 7. Openness to the Holy Spirit
    - Do I listen for the promptings of the Holy Spirit, or only follow my own ideas?
    - Am I attentive to how God might be calling me deeper, beyond my comfort zone?
    - Do I resist God’s invitations to conversion, generosity, or deeper trust?


“It is no longer I who live, but Christ who lives in me.” (Galatians 2:20)
