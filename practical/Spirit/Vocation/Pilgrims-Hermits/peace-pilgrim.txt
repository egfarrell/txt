### Preparation
    - Throughout her thirties, Mildred searched for her mission.
      She worked with senior citizens and the
      emotionally-disturbed. She volunteered with peace
      organizations, including the Quaker American Friends
      Service Committee and the Women’s International League for
      Peace and Freedom.

    - She became a vegetarian and gave up caffeine. She stopped
      eating white flour and white sugar (decades before their
      dangers made the news).
    - She relinquished “self-will,” which she defined as her
      lower impulses. “If you are motivated to do or say a mean
      thing, you can always think of a good thing.”

### Descending into the Valley
    - (College talk 41:58)
    - When I descended into the valley, it was because I was
      thinking in self-centred terms.
    - Earlier the valleys and peaks were different, the valleys
      never went deeper than my previous life. So when I was in
      a valley I was used to that.
    - But later on the plateau, when I was in harmony most of the
      time and then slipped out... that's when I missed it the
      most.
    - And it was always when I started again to think of some
      self-centred wish, that I descended into the valley.
    - Q: Was it ever related to others behaviours towards you...
      if they were disappointed in you or treated you badly?
    - A: I cant really say it was due to the behaviour of others
      when I slipped out of harmony. It was more I had let the
      self-centred nature govern my life.
        - You see, some people have difficulty relating to
          others. But I was always very forgiving to others.
        - I rather had to learn to have a more lenient attitute
          towards myself. Because there was a time when I really
          expected too much of myself (smiles). So I had to put
          myself into my proper place.
        - Since I was 16 I took the "inner way". And so for the
          spiritual things, I always looked to the inside rather
          than the outside.
        - It's not that I don't relate well to people, and it's
          not that I don't love and appreciate people because
          I certainly do... in fact, I often see people as more
          beautiful than they see themselves. Because what I see
          when I look at people, is not their self-centred
          nature, I see their higher nature.
        - People to me look beautiful... they look like shining
          lights... whether they are acting that way or not...
          they still are capable of acting that way...
        - And what I see within the person is the reality of the
          person because really you are this nature centred in
          the good of the whole (points to higher
          nature diagram) .... and "wearing" the body, and the
          body wears the shirt...

### How Pilgrimage began
    - I had slipped out of harmony for the last time (as it
      turned out) and as I went to bed that night I thought it if
      I could just remain in harmony, I could be of much greater
      _usefulness_, because every time I slipped out of harmony,
      it impairs my usefulness.
    - And when I woke up in the morning, I was back again on the
      mountaintop, with a wonderful inner-knowning that I would
      never again need to descend into the valley.
    - And with that wonderful feeling, I went out for my time
      appart, my inspirational time, and I was back with my
      friends in less than an hour, and while I was out, you
      might say a thought just struck my mind, I felt this strong
      inner-motivation towards this "next way" of witnessing for
      peace.
    - Now there was a little more than that to it, I saw in my
      minds eye, myself walking along and wearing the garb of my
      mission... I just needed to duplicate it.
    - I saw in my minds eye a map of the USA with the large
      cities marked in on it, and it looked as if someone had
      taken a colored crayon and marked a route across from LA to
      NYC coast-to-coast and border-to-border which was my first
      pilgrimage route.
    - Now this was of course something that no-one could dissuade
      me from doing. Everyone tried. They thought I'd probably
      kill myself the first year walking across the country. But
      you see, these higher receivings... either the thing you
      touch from the outside and get the confirmation on, or the
      direct perception (and this of course was a direct
      perception)... these things get to a very deep level, where
      there is no doubt, there is complete clarity and
      understanding, you can explain these things, you can
      discuss these things...
        - Your own higher nature is actually a drop from the
          ocean of truth around you... and it has access to the
          ocean... from that source came all inspired writing in
          the first place and somebody wrote it down... and you
          yourself can reach out directly into that source.
    - Now one more thing... I might mention of course that you
      keep on learning lessons after you have found inner peace.
    - The first lesson I learnt was the lesson of receiving...
