### Food
    STAPLES (daily base)
    - Oats
    - Couscous
    - Bread (local bakeries)
    - Pasta (occasional, if fuel allows)

    CALORIE DENSE ADDITIONS
    - Sunflower/rapseed oil (small bottle)
    - Mayonnaise
    - Peanut butter
    - Nuts (mixed, peanuts, almonds)
    - Seeds (sunflower, pumpkin)

    PROTEIN (rotate when possible)
    - Tinned fish (sardines, mackerel, tuna)
    - Chickpeas / lentils (tinned or pouch)
    - Eggs (buy fresh, eat same day or next)
    - Yogurt (when available)
    - Powdered milk

    QUICK SUGARS / ENERGY
    - honey (good)
    - Raisins
    - Dried fruit (apricots, dates)
    - Bananas / local fruit

    FLAVOUR / MORALE
    - Tea / coffee
    - Salt
    - Stock cubes or spices (optional, very light)

    OPTIONAL SUPPORT
    - Multivitamin

### DAILY TARGET: ~3500 kcal
    - BREAKFAST (~950 kcal)
    - Oats: ~100 g (~¾ mug)
    - Powdered milk: ~30 g
    - Nuts: ~40 g (small handful)
    - 1 banana
    - Coffee

    - LUNCH (~1150 kcal)
    - Couscous: ~100 g (~½ mug)
    - Raisins: ~30 g
    - Olive/rapeseed oil: ~20 g (~1.5 tbsp)
    - Mayonnaise: ~30 g (~1 tbsp)
    - Seeds: ~20 g
    - Tea

    - DINNER (~950 kcal)
    - Couscous: ~100 g (~½ mug)
    - Protein: 1 tin fish OR chickpeas (~200–250 kcal)
    - Oil: ~20 g (~1.5 tbsp)
    - Bread: ~50–70 g

    - EXTRAS (~500 kcal)
    - Nuts: ~50 g
    - Extra dried fruit or bread as needed

    - NOTES
    - Add +20–30 g oil if low energy
    - Add more protein if recovery poor
    - Total dry weight ≈ 500–700 g/day
    - Oil = key calorie booster (9 kcal/g)
