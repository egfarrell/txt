# My Question

    - I am very fit, middle age, have walked thousands of miles over Europe on
      Christian pilgrimages and have spent time in various monasteries.

    - Over the past 4 years, again and again in prayer I sense a call /
      invitation to take a longer 1.5 year pilgrimage to the major Marian
      shrines of Europe. The longest pilgrimage I ever did is 7 months.

    - My 7 month pilgrimage I had €8,000 in savings so although I did camp out
      in fields etc sometimes... it was comfortable overall.

    - Again and again I have prayed to Our Lord to see if this inspiration is
      genuine. At this stage in my spiritual journey I know well that many
      things can be delusions and false inspirations.

    - I have been hugely influenced by Benedict Joseph Labre and also a modern
      day pilgrim who made pilgrimages over Europe with little money (George
      Walter - he wrote 2 books)

    - What does pilgrimage mean to me? Silent prayer, silent praise to God for
      creation, silent awe, and perhaps a silent sign to people that drive by.
      Also a deep sense of peace comes over me. But deep down I just know I'm an
      ordinary person even if these pilgrimages seem unusual.

    - But on various retreats my professional religious guides have
      understandable been very cautious and don't really approve of a 1.5 year
      pilgrimage (understandably in this day and age)

    - I have about €3000 saved, can play a flute, am strong enough to work.

    - What can I make of all this? I feel drawn gently to go... but can someone
      really discount the lack of encouragement from professed religious people?
