CLEANING REGULATIONS FOR PEOPLE AND TEAMS USING THE FACILITY
NOWA WAVE SWIMMING POOL
1. The swimming pool is a facility of the Sports and Recreation Center of the Capital City of Warsaw in the Wola district, with headquarters in Warsaw
at ul. Esperanto 5 (postal code 01-049).
2. The swimming pool is open in the following hours:
- Monday - Friday 7:00 am - 10:00 pm
- Saturday and Sunday 8:00 am - 10:00 pm
With breaks for disinfection of the facility from 1 p.m. to 2 p.m.
3. People using its services may stay in the swimming pool hall, including:
a. that can swim;
b. learning to swim or participating in other activities (under the supervision of an instructor);
c. teachers with pupils.
4. Children up to 13 years of age may stay on the premises of the facility only under the supervision of an adult.
5. People under the influence of intoxicants, alcohol, and with external signs of disease will not be
let into the swimming pool.
6. The participants of the classes are obliged to react to sound signals ruthlessly and immediately
driver's warning (whistle).
7. Group classes (swimming lessons, aqua fitnes, etc.) take place according to the agreed schedule of classes.
8. Entry to the swimming pool is allowed after prior purchase of a ticket, pass, reservation of a lane / swimming pool or on the basis of a valid agreement in writing.
9. A large 25m x 12.5m swimming pool can accommodate no more than 50 people at a time.
10. A small basin with dimensions of 12.5 mx 6.25 may accommodate up to 30 people at a time
11. There cannot be more for one instructor - lifeguard conducting swimming lessons
than 15 people.
12. Swimming lessons may be conducted by coaches and instructors employed by OSiR Wola
or by external entities on the basis of an appropriate agreement concluded with OSiR Wola. These persons must have appropriate qualifications and are responsible for the program of activities and the safety of the swimmers.
13. The following swimwear must be worn in the swimming pool:
a. women - bathing suits and caps;
b. men - swimming trunks (short leg) and caps.
14. People conducting the classes should wear sports clothes and shoes used only in the swimming pool.
15. Swimming pool customers are obliged to:
a. Thoroughly wash the whole body before entering the pool deck;
b. the foot crossing washes (paddling pool).
c. hand disinfection at the entrance and exit from the facility
16. Participants of group classes (organized for schools and kindergartens) enter the swimming pool at the same time. People conducting group classes are obliged to check the number of people in the group before and after the classes.
17. The tutor of an organized group (schools and kindergartens) is obliged to come to classes 10 minutes before their start and enter with the group. An unaccompanied group will not be allowed to enter the swimming pool.
18. In the swimming pool, it is obligatory to maintain order, peace and quiet, as well as to follow the instructions of the lifeguard and the person conducting the classes (in the above-mentioned order).
19. It is forbidden to bring into the swimming pool all kinds of dangerous items, firearms, edged weapons, explosives, pyrotechnic products, fire-endangering materials.
20. People using the swimming pool are prohibited from:
a. swim in jewelry and watches;
b. smoking, using drugs, bringing and drinking alcohol, and eating in the paid zone;
c. performing jumps from the posts and the edge of the pool without the permission of the lifeguard;
d. swim in fins and masks without the permission of the lifeguard.
21. The lifeguard and the person conducting the classes are responsible for the safety of participants in the swimming pool.
22. People using the sports equipment owned by the swimming pool (boards, floats, etc.) should put them away in a designated place.
23. The institutions or persons causing the damage are fully responsible for damage to the equipment or devices by the users of the swimming pool.
24. Participants in group activities (schools, kindergartens), programs and campaigns are entitled to use the changing rooms and showers 10 minutes before the start and 10 minutes after the end of the classes. Each participant may use the cloakroom for the duration of the classes, after this date the cloakroom must be vacated.
25. Due to sanitary and hygienic requirements, parents and other people not participating in the classes are not allowed in the swimming pool hall.
26. The cloakroom employee is not responsible for valuables left in the cloakroom.
27. Valuables should be deposited in dedicated lockers.
28. At the signal indicating the end of the class, participants should immediately leave the pool.
29. People staying on the premises of the sports facility must disinfect their hands and their own sports equipment. 30. People using the sports facility are obliged to inform the staff of any
circumstances affecting epidemic safety.
31. All persons on the premises of the sports facility (including participants and coaches), except for
persons performing professional activities not related to direct customer service, are required to cover their mouth and nose (in particular with a mask, mask, helmet or clothing or parts thereof). This obligation does not apply to people staying in the shower area, in the swimming pools and on the beach of the swimming pool, people who are in open areas and keep a distance of at least 2 m from other people.
32. The facility manager does not provide masks, masks or other personal protective equipment for the needs of persons staying on the premises of the facility necessary to fulfill the obligation indicated in the above point.
33. After using the facility, the users are obliged to immediately leave the facility
sports activities to avoid the accumulation of people.
34. Payment with payment cards is recommended.
35. Participants who do not comply with the rules set out in this document, the regulations for the use of a given
the sports facility, guidelines of the Chief Sanitary Inspector or the Minister of Health are subject to immediate expulsion from the sports facility. In the event of non-compliance with the order referred to in the previous sentence - the staff is obliged to immediately report the incident to the police.
36. Failure to comply with the safety rules may be grounds for refusing to make the sports facility available again to a person who does not comply with the rules.
37. It is forbidden to bring any items to the facility which are not normally bound
with the use of the facility.
38. People using the sports facility are required to read these regulations
and compliance with the provisions contained therein.
39. Persons violating public order and the provisions of the regulations will be removed from the swimming pool area.
40. Complaints, comments and suggestions should be reported to the management of the center.
