Searching for accommodation


### 1. Facebook

- Use Facebook - some of our students and postdocs indicate it is
  the easiest and most efficient way to find an apartment or
  room. Look at facebook marketplace for public groups, like
  “Accommodation in Warsaw” or “Mieszkania pokoje Toruń”:
  https://www.facebook.com/groups/485933348146385/(Warszawa)
  https://www.facebook.com/groups/414472168651883(Toruń) Posts in
  English are not that rare on Facebook.

    - https://gratka.pl/nieruchomosci
    - https://www.gumtree.pl/s-nieruchomosci/v1c2p1

    - Some of these websites have English versions but the
      majority are in Polish. Also most of the announcements are
      in Polish. Using google translate is an option.

    - Note: the total price (to be paid) is not usually the one
      reported: often the administration costs (such as water,
      electricity, etc.) are not included.

### Useful Phrases:
    - domy                         houses
    - mieszkania                   apartments, flats
    - pokoje                       rooms
    - wynajem / sprzedaż           rent / sell
    - mieszkania do wynajęcia      apartments for rent
    - mieszkania na sprzedaż       apartments for sale
    - mieszkania od pośredników    apartments from brokers
    - mieszkania bezpośrednio      direct from owner (no agency fees! (can easily be half or 1 month's rent)
    - kaucja                       deposit
    - cena w zł                    price in PLN
    - pokoje / liczba pokoi        rooms / number of rooms
    - łazienka / kuchnia           bathroom / kitchen
    - powierzchnia                 area
    - piętro                       floor
    - najnowsze                    the latest
    - najtańsze                    cheapest
