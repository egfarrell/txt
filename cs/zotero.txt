### ZOTERO attachment rename string
    - `extensions.zotero.attachmentRenameFormatString`
    - https://www.zotero.org/support/preferences/hidden_preferences#translator_preferences
    - In "Edit - Preferences - Advanced - config editor"
    - Orig: {%c - }{%y - }{%t{50}} _(title %t limit 50 chars)_
    - Mine: `{%y-}{%t{50}}`
    - NOTE: 
        - If there's a single comma in the PDF title (which will
          mess-up vim) then: 
        - rename the _parent item_. 
        - Then rename the attachement.
