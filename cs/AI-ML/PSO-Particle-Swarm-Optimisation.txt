
### Particle swarm optimization algorithm: an overview
    - https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00500-016-2474-6
    - The first theoretical analysis of simplified PSO algorithm
      was proposed by Ozcan and Mohan (1998) who indicated that
      in a simplified one-dimensional PSO system, a particle
      moved along a path _defined by a sinusoidal wave_, and
      determined its amplitude and frequency randomly. 
    - However, their analysis was merely limited to the simple
      PSO model without the inertia weight, and assumed that Pid
      and Pgd kept unchanged. 
    - Actually, Pid and Pgd changed frequently, so the particle’
      trajectory was a sine wave composed of many different
      amplitudes and frequencies.
    - Nickabadi et al. (2011) pro- posed a anti-noise PSO algorithm
