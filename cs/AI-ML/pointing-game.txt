### Pointing game: key paper:
    - "Top-Down Neural Attention via Excitation Backprop"
    - We propose a new method, Excitation Backprop, for modeling
      a CNN classifier's top-down attention, creating
      task-specific maps. This mimics human visual attention,
      using a probabilistic process to signal downwards in the
      network.
    - We introduce _contrastive attention_ for more distinct
      attention maps, linking it theoretically to Class
      Activation Map calculations.
    - We efficiently implement Excitation Backprop for common
      neural layers.
    - Our experiments visualize model decision evidence through
      these maps and assess accuracy in weakly supervised
      localization tasks on MS COCO, PASCAL VOC07, and ImageNet.
    - We validate our method's utility in text-to-region tasks,
      showing promising phrase localization on Flickr30k Entities
      using a CNN trained on weakly labeled web images.
    - Finally, we apply our method to model interpretation and
      data annotation in facial expression analysis and medical
      imaging.
    - https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11263-017-1059-x


### Key ideas
    - "Top-Down Attention" in CNNs:
        - The paper focuses on how top-down attention works in
          CNNs, a type of neural network commonly used for image
          recognition tasks. Top-down attention helps the network
          concentrate on the most relevant parts of an image when
          making a decision.

    - Biological Inspiration:
        - The authors took inspiration from the Selective Tuning
          model, a biologically plausible way of visual
          processing. This model uses a combination of bottom-up
          (data-driven) and top-down (goal-driven) processes.

    - New Formulation: `Excitation Backprop`:
        - Introduces novel method called Excitation Backprop.
          This method blends top-down and bottom-up information
          to efficiently calculate which parts of the neural
          network are most relevant for a given task.

    - Probabilistic Winner-Take-All Process:
        - Unlike previous deterministic models, Excitation
          Backprop uses a probabilistic approach to decide which
          neurons in the network should be focused on. This
          allows for more nuanced and interpretable attention
          maps, which are critical for understanding how the
          network is making its decisions.

    - Contrastive Top-Down Attention:
        - The authors propose a new concept called contrastive
          top-down attention. This helps in differentiating
          between similar top-down signals, making the generated
          attention maps more distinct and useful for specific
          tasks.

    - Connection to Class Activation Maps:
        - The paper establishes a theoretical link between its
          contrastive attention formulation and Class Activation
          Maps, which are used to visualize areas of interest in
          CNNs.

    - Empirical Validation:
        - The method is tested on various challenging datasets
          and demonstrated superior performance in tasks like
          weakly supervised localization (identifying objects in
          images without detailed training data).

    - Practical Applications:
        - The paper suggests practical uses of their method, such
          as in facial emotion analysis and medical image
          analysis, where understanding the focus of a neural
          network can be crucial.

    - General Contributions:
        - The paper's main contributions are the new attention
          model for CNNs, the enhancement of attention maps'
          distinctiveness, establishing a connection with Class
          Activation Map computation, and exploring
          text-to-region associations using this model.

    - Applications in Model Interpretation and Annotation:
        - The authors highlight how this new method can be used
          for interpreting neural network models and assisting in
          data annotation, particularly in complex fields like
          _MEDICAL IMAGING_.
