### On-Policy / Off-Policy Difference?
    - Main difference is the update rule:
    - on-policy
        - updates `Q-value` assuming the current policy is taken
          at the next time step,
    - off-policy
        - updates its `Q-value` by assuming only the optimal
          policy is taken by the next time step.
    - But when taking actions, both of the agents behave under
      the epsilon-greedy policy
