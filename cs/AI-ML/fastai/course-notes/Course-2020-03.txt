### Recognise digits: IDENTIFY "3" AND "7"
    - MNist package
    - `Path.BASE_PATH = path` 

- tensor: is the pyTorch version of a numpy array. 
- But a pyTorch tensor can be computed on a GPU, not just a CPU

- Basic baseline idea: pixel similarity
    - to define the "ideal" digit 3, take the pixel _AVERAGE_ of
      all the '3's
    - mistake: fail to create the baseline 
    - e.g. 
        - fancy model:      80% accuracy!
        - predict the mean: 85% accuracy! 

### Tradition: 
    - in the 'computer vision' field, when working with floats,
      we expect them to be between `0` and `1`. (so for RGB
      values, divide by 255)

### Broadcasting:
