### Lesson-23 Super-resolution
    - Video https://youtu.be/z1In7QaG0fg
    - Chat
      https://forums.fast.ai/t/lesson-23-official-topic/103965
    - Developed super-resolution U-Net model using Tiny Imagenet,
      focusing on dataset creation, preprocessing, and
      augmentation. Aim was to upscale low-resolution images.
    - Used AdamW optimizer and mixed precision for training,
      achieving around 60% accuracy. Compared our model's
      performance with others on Tiny Imagenet from 'Papers with
      Code'.
    - Addressed limitations of CNNs for image super-resolution.
    - Introduced `U-net`, a more effective architecture, and
      implemented perceptual loss. This involved comparing
      intermediate layer features of the output and target images
      using a pre-trained classifier. Resulting U-net images were
      sharper and closer to targets.
    - Explored challenges in comparing different models.
      Highlighted perceptual loss's significant improvement,
      despite lack of a clear comparison metric. 
    - Applied fast.ai's technique of gradually unfreezing
      pre-trained networks. Copied weights into our model,
      trained with initially frozen weights, leading to notable
      loss reduction.

### Lesson-25 Latent diffusion
    - Video https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8AgZ9jcQ9v8
    - Chat
      https://forums.fast.ai/t/lesson-25-official-topic/104573
    - In the series finale, Johno demonstrates converting sounds
      to images, then using course knowledge to generate audio.
      He showcases an effective bird-song generator using this
      method.
    - Jeremy concludes "Stable diffusion from scratch,"
      illustrating the use of variational encoder latents as
      "pixels" in a diffusion model. He suggests an innovative
      concept: employing latents for tasks like classification,
      potentially leading to new methods like latents-FID,
      latents-perceptual-loss, and advanced diffusion guidance
      techniques.
