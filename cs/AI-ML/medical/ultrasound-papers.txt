
1. "Classification of Ultrasound-Based Breast Cancer Images with
   Deep Learning Approaches"
    - has summary tables that compare different methods
   https://www.mdpi.com/2673-4591/31/1/8

2. **Margarida R. Ferreira et al.  2023 **: The paper explores
   the use of deep neural networks, particularly EfficientNet,
   for classifying breast lesions in ultrasound images. It
   achieves a high accuracy of 97.65% and an AUC of 96.30%. The
   study demonstrates the effectiveness of deep learning in
   clinical breast lesion classification. It underscores the
   potential of EfficientNet in medical imaging. This research
   contributes to the field of automated and accurate breast
   cancer detection  [Ferreira et al.]
   https://dx.doi.org/10.1109/EMBC40787.2023.10340293  .

3. **Bashir Zeimarani et al.**: This research focuses on deep
   CNNs for classifying breast lesions from ultrasound images.
   A custom-built CNN with regularization techniques is
   developed, achieving 92.05% accuracy and an AUC of 0.97. The
   study shows that even with limited training data, this CNN
   outperforms traditional algorithms. It highlights the
   effectiveness of deep learning over manual feature selection.
   The paper contributes to advancements in breast cancer
   detection using AI  [Zeimarani et al.]
   https://dx.doi.org/10.1109/ACCESS.2020.3010863  .

4. **H. Alrubaie et al.  2023 **: The study presents a CNN model
   for classifying breast ultrasound imaging. It categorizes
   images into benign, malignant, and normal classes with 96%
   accuracy. For benign and malignant classifications, it
   achieves 100% accuracy. This work demonstrates the high
   effectiveness of CNNs in medical image analysis. The model's
   high accuracy showcases the potential for improved breast
   cancer diagnostics  [Alrubaie et al.]
   https://dx.doi.org/10.29194/njes.26020057  .

5. **Jarin Tasnim and Md Kamrul Hasan**: The paper introduces
   a fully automated breast ultrasound diagnosis system using
   deep learning. It achieves high accuracy, surpassing previous
   techniques. The system automates the classification of breast
   lesions in ultrasound images. This advancement represents
   a significant step in AI-driven medical diagnostics. The study
   shows how deep learning can revolutionize breast cancer
   detection  [Tasnim and Hasan]
   https://dx.doi.org/10.1088/1361-6560/ad1319  .

6. **A. AlZoubi et al.  2023 **: This research evaluates transfer
   learning and automatically designed networks for ultrasound
   image classification. A CNN network optimized by Bayesian
   methods shows superior accuracy and performance. The study
   emphasizes the efficiency of optimized deep learning models in
   medical imaging. It highlights the advantages of transfer
   learning and Bayesian optimization in AI applications. This
   approach leads to improved accuracy and generalization in
   breast lesion classification  [AlZoubi et al.]
   https://dx.doi.org/10.1007/s11517-023-02922-y  .

7. **Jennie Karlsson et al.  2023 **: The study explores deep
   learning for classifying breast images from point-of-care
   ultrasound  POCUS . It combines POCUS images with artificially
   generated images for improved accuracy. The approach achieves
   a 95% confidence interval for AUC between 93.5% - 96.6%. This
   method showcases the potential of deep learning in enhancing
   POCUS image analysis. The research is significant for its
   application in real-time clinical environments  [Karlsson et
   al.] https://dx.doi.org/10.1117/12.2654251  .

8. **Zhemin Zhuang et al.  2020 **: This paper uses image
   decomposition and transfer learning for breast ultrasound
   lesion classification. It significantly improves accuracy,
   sensitivity, specificity, F1 score, and AUC. The method
   outperforms traditional transfer learning techniques. The
   study demonstrates the potential of combining advanced AI
   techniques for medical imaging. It marks a notable advancement
   in the accurate and efficient classification of breast lesions
   [Zhuang et al.] https://dx.doi.org/10.1002/mp.14510  .

9. **Rajesh Singh and Ragini Mishra  2022 **: The research
   develops a deep learning approach for classifying tissues in
   mammograms using ultrasound methods. It utilizes advanced
   segmentation maps for accurate results. The system is capable
   of differentiating between normal and abnormal tissues. The
   paper highlights the integration of multiple imaging
   techniques. This study contributes to the field by improving
   mammogram analysis through deep learning  [Singh and Mishra]
   https://dx.doi.org/10.1109/WPMC55625.2022.10014847  .

10. **Mohammed Tarek GadAllah et al.  2023 **: The study conducts
    a comparative analysis using ten pre-trained CNN models on
    breast ultrasound images. The ResNet50 model achieves the
    highest accuracy at 97.25%. It highlights the effectiveness
    of different CNN architectures in medical imaging. The
    research offers insights into selecting optimal models for
    breast lesion classification. This comparative study aids in
    understanding the best practices for applying CNNs in breast
    cancer detection  [GadAllah et al.]
    https://dx.doi.org/10.1109/IMSA58542.2023.10217585  .
