# Policy Based Methods

- Homepage https://classroom.udacity.com/nanodegrees/nd893/parts/286e7d2c-e00c-4146-a5f2-a490e0f23eda


### 00: Project
    - Train a robotic arm to reach target location, or train
      a four-legged virtual creature to walk.

### 01: Intro to Policy-Based Methods
    - Learn the theory behind evolutionary algorithms, stochastic
      policy search, and the REINFORCE algorithm.
    - Learn how to apply the algorithms to solve a classical
      control problem

### 02: Improving Policy Gradient Methods
    - Learn about techniques such as Generalized Advantage
      Estimation (GAE) for lowering the variance of policy
      gradient methods.
    - Explore policy optimization methods such as Trust Region
      Policy Optimization (TRPO) and Proximal Policy Optimization
      (PPO)

### 03: Actro-Critic Methods
    - Study cutting-edge algorithms such as Deep Deterministic
      Policy Gradients (DDPG)

### 04: Deep RL for Financial Trading
    - Learn from Nvidia how to use actor-critic methods to
      generate optimal financial trading strategies.
