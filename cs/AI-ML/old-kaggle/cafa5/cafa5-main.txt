


### Basic info
    - The problem: There are many proteins in the databases for
      which the sequence is known, but the function is not. 
    - The gap between what we know and what we do not know is
      growing. A major challenge in bioinformatics is to predict
      the function of a protein from its sequence or structure.
    - At the same time, how can we judge how well these function
      prediction algorithms are performing?

    - A good place to start is "A (not so) quick introduction to
      protein function prediction" Authored by @predragradivojac
      (attached). It is actually a pretty quick introduction for
      computational people who have zero domain knowledge and
      want only the relevant stuff for tacking the problems in
      this competition. This will explain the motivation for the
      problem, how it is cast, etc. The third chapter, on CAFA is
      a bit dated, and while historically correct, the rules that
      we had for previous CAFA competition do not necessarily
      apply to the Kaggle challenge. The only source for Rules
      for CAFA 5 is on this site. Also, we use slightly different
      assessment metrics than in section 2.3 (again, Rules for
      CAFA 5).
