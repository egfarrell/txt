- TRAINING LOOP
    - for all batches
    - note: pytorch accumulates gradients... so we must 'zero
      out' the gradient for EACH batch
        - $MY_IMAGES/cs/AI-ML/deep-lizard-single-batch-zero-out-gradient.jpg
    - interesting: never thought of it this way:
        - the `batch size` affects HOW MANY TIMES we're going to
          update the weights
        - we call `optimzer.step()` once per batch
        - if you change the `batch size`
        - in deep lizard example: 
            - he had 600 'batches', or 600 iterations of the
              training loop. (60,000 MNIST images, batch_size was
              100)
            - so he took 600 steps towards the loss function's
              minimum
            - so when u change the batch size, you change the
              number of times we update the weights and step
              towards the loss function's minimum
