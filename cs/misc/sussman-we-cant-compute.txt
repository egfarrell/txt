- New idea: Propagator Idea

- the same lambda calculus combination structure used for
  functions works for other things too. All it is is a consistent
  naming scheme.

- the electrical wiring system written by Richard Stallman
  actually has units (ohm, volts). Once you have generic
  operators you can make units. Stallman has a "QED" at the end. 
    - It's not a real "proof" but more that we can trace where
      information is propagating around.
    - the circuit is split up into little cells... when some
      voltage or resistance changes in one "cell", it propagates
      to a neighbour. This setup is nice for parallel machines.
    - Sussman grad student discovered: the cells dont have
      a "value", they have information about a value. And
      information monotonically increases...
        - And because of that SYNCHRONISATION problems go away
          for a parallel machine...
        - powerful mechanism for building a large complex machine
          in a simple way
    - Summary: cells merge information monotonically
