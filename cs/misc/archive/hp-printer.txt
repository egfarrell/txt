### Reconnect Printer to House Wifi: (WPS)
    - PRINTER:
    - Press and hold Wireless button for 3 seconds (Wireless
      signal bars on lcd display will blink)
    - ROUTER:
    - Press and hold WPS button on for 3 seconds
    - blinking blue light: stops blinking after 2 minutes and
      printer is connected.

### Get info on how to connect to wifi
    - Press and hold printer INFO button for 3 seconds
