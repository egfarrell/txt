### CONFIGS
    - ACTIVE config:                $HOME/.config/mpv/mpv.conf
    - ACTIVE Keybindings:           $HOME/config/mpv/input.conf

    - Dotfiles config:              $HOME/dotfiles/ini/mpv/mpv.conf
    - Dotfiles keybindings default: $HOME/dotfiles/ini/mpv/input.conf.default
    - Dotfiles keybindings mine:    $HOME/dotfiles/ini/mpv/input.conf

### always show time progress
    osd-level=3

### Listen to livestream
    - HN tip https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=26805702
    mpv --no-video --vo=null --ytdl-format='bestaudio/best'

### Handy Keys
    - "P": show progress
    - "O": show progress keey on-screen
    - ">": next in playlist
    - "<": prev in playlist
    - "s": screenshot

### Show key names
    mpv --input-keylist
