### interesting paper:
    - Linked Edit Rules:
    - A Web Friendly Way of Checking Quality of RDF Data Cubes
    - Meroño-Peñuela, Guéret, Schlobach

    - Data editing is “the activity aimed at detecting and
      correcting errors (logical inconsistencies) in data” [16].
      Traditionally, edits have been considered at two levels:
        - micro-edits, aimed at finding inconsistencies in
          individual records;
        - macro-edits, “based upon analysis of an aggregate
          rather than an individual record” [3].
        - More recently, automatic processing of these edits has
          gained importance [9].

    - For example, the R packages _editrules_ (validation through
      micro-edits) and validate (validation through cross-record
      and cross-dataset macro-edits) are useful tools to
      automatically validate locally stored statistical datasets.
    - However, these rule formats are not Web standard
      ([WebStructured]) nor suitable for efficient Web
      distribution ([WebDistrib]).

    - Eurostat proposes the eDAMIS Validation Engine (EVE) to
      validate statistical datasets defining intra (micro) and
      inter (macro) record rules, but these are kept internally
      in the system.

    - Finally, SDMX proposes Validation and Transformation
      Language (VTL). However, VTL:
        - strongly emphasises transformation instead of
          validation ([CubeCheck]);
        - doesnt define a mechanism to publish or link edit rules
          on the Web ([WebDistrib], [WebStructured], [UniqID]).
        - No existing approach tackles requirements explicitly
          [Scope],
        - does not generate interoperable validation reporting
          ([Prov], [Annotation]).
