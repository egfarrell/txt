jpeg filetype

formats

	jpeg/JFIF,
		most common format for storing and
		transmitting photographic images on the Web.

	jpeg/EXIF
		most common image format used by DIGITAL CAMERAS
		and other photographic devices;


	These format variations are
	often not distinguished,
	and both are simply called "JPEG".


	The term "JPEG" is an acronym
		Joint Photographic Experts Group,
		which created the standard.


MIME
	The MIME media type for JPEG is
		image/jpeg

	except in Internet Explorer,
		image/pjpeg
