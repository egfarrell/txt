install nvidia DELL laptop

Here is my fix:

1.Download the right driver for linux from nVidia and put it into your
home folder. I used
http://www.nvidia.com/object/linux-display-ia32-304.60-driver.html

2.Open terminal and enter 
    sudo apt-get --purge remove nvidia-* 
after that 
    echo options nouveau modeset=0 | sudo tee -a /etc/modprobe.d/nouveau-kms.conf
and
    sudo update-initramfs -u

3.Reboot, previous steps are taken from the guide here:
http://www.overclock.net/a/install-nvidia-drivers-in-ubuntu-11-04-11-10

4.Boot into recovery mode, from there choose 'resume normal boot'.
A few warnings - continue.

5.In login screen press crtl+alt+F1 and log in.

6.Enter 'sudo service lightdm stop'. If you don't get right back into
virtual terminal press crtl+alt+F1 again.

7.Enter 'ls'. You should see all things in your home folder listed,
nvidia driver being one of them.

8.Enter 'chmod +x nvidia-304-60'. nvidia-304-60 being the file you
downloaded, this may be different.

9.Enter 'sudo sh nvidia-304-60'. See above ^

10.The installer will start, ask you to agree to the terms and
conditions and continue to compile.

11.After that it will ask if it should register for automatic
compiling (Yes) and if it should run nvidia-xconfig (Yes).

12.Enter 'sudo reboot' to reboot normally. Done!
