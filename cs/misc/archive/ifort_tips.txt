ifort tips

optimisation
    -O0     no optimise, better for debugging
    -O2     default - e.g. inline small functions
    -O3     max. longer to compile

-xHost
    take advanage of any special instructions
    on the CPU that you're compiling on

-fp-model fast
-fp-model precise
    floating point operations
    'precise' will match whatever bracket ordering you specify
