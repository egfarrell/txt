iAnnotate Export Annotations

A 'Notes Summary' is a plain-text, sequential list of your
markups. It can be:
* sent in the body of any email sent from iAnnotate, 
* or it can be copied to the iPad clipboard.

# Copying it to the Clipboard
    From the _document tab_, you can also copy the Notes
    Summary to the iPad clipboard for pasting into other
    fields and/or apps. This may be useful for pasting your
    summary into the iPad Notes or Mail apps.


# Sending it via Email

	Navigation Panel. 
    * Slide open the Navigation Panel at the left, 
	* select Action -> More Actions -> Note Summary. 

    If you are currently viewing the document, then the
    Notes Summary for that document is inserted into the
    body of an email (the document itself is not attached).

    If you perform this action from the library instead,
    then you must select the document via its circle icon
    prior to tapping Notes Summary in the accordion.
