
What is CI / CD?

1. **Continuous Integration (CI):**
   - This is like having a sous-chef who constantly checks your
     recipe and cooking techniques.
   - Every time you make a change (add a new ingredient or tweak
     a step in the recipe), the sous-chef (CI system)
     automatically takes your updated recipe and prepares a small
     portion of the dish.
   - The sous-chef then tastes it (runs automated tests) to make
     sure it still tastes good (your code works correctly) and
     hasn't gone bad (introduced errors).
   - If it's not perfect, the sous-chef tells you immediately, so
     you can fix any issues right away.

2. **Continuous Delivery (CD):**
   - Once your sous-chef (CI system) is happy with your recipe
     (code), it's time to send the dish (software) out to the
     dining room.
   - The dish is plated and presented beautifully (built and
     packaged), ready for your customers (users) to enjoy.
   - But, it's not served to everyone right away. Instead, it's
     placed on a special serving tray (staging environment) for
     a final quality check.
   - Testers and quality assurance (QA) folks come by to taste it
     one last time to ensure it's truly perfect and won't make
     anyone sick (thorough testing).

3. **Continuous Deployment (CD) (Optional):**
   - If you're feeling daring, you can skip the final quality
     check and send the dish straight to the dining room as soon
     as it's ready. This is like continuously deploying your code
     to production without manual intervention.
   - However, this approach is riskier because if there's
     a problem with the dish (bugs in the code), it goes directly
     to the customers (users).

- So, CI/CD pipelines are like having a kitchen crew (automation
  tools and scripts) that ensures your recipe (code) is always
  tasty, consistent, and safe to serve. It allows you to update
  your menu (software) frequently, knowing that each dish
  (software release) will meet high-quality standards and be
  ready for your customers to enjoy without causing any "food
  poisoning" (software issues).
