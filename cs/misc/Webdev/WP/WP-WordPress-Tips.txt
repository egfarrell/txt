### Basic tips
    - https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6bsYrFoYTzI
    - _Timezone_: chose actual city name e.g. 'New York'
        - If you use the city instead of time zone then daylight
          saving +/- 1 hour will be automatically done. While
          using UTC setting will not make this
    - _New page_/post:
        - Don't click 'Publish' twice!
        - click '3 dots', then untick 'pre-publish checks'
            - $MY_IMAGES/cs/wp/pre-publish-checks.jpg
    - Create multiple pages at once via menu section
        - Goto website - `customise`
            - $MY_IMAGES/cs/wp/create-new-menu-1.jpg
        - make it a primary menu
            - $MY_IMAGES/cs/wp/create-new-menu-2.jpg
        - Add as many new pages as you want
            - $MY_IMAGES/cs/wp/create-new-menu-3.jpg
        - Other approach:
            - We focus on building the home page and then
              x ammout of example content pages and then
              duplicate the the content pages for extra pages.


### New rivals to WP
    - WP is very famous so clients often require it.
    - But newer crop of CMSes:
        - `Craft, Ghost, Kirby, Twill, October, Grav...`
        - They don't have legacy baggage and are much better for
          fully custom-made sites. You basically get a light web
          framework with super customisable admin.
