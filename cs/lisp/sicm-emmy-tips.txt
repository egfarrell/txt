### Emmy Internals
    - https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=37391161
    - During the 2 year period I've been working on Emmy (on top
      of work by Colin Smith), I was keen to make the
      implementation more accessible and well-documented than the
      original.  
    - There's still not a great map of the project (from
      primitives to general relativity), but many of the
      namespaces are written as literate programming
      explorations: https://emmy.mentat.org/#explore-the-project
    - Here's the automatic differentiation implementation/essay,
      for example:
      https://sritchie.github.io/emmy/src/emmy/differential.html
    - A rough sketch of the tower is:  
        - `emmy.value` and `emmy.generic` 
            - implement the extensible generic operations
        - `emmy.ratio`, `emmy.complex` and `emmy.numbers` 
            - fleshes out the numeric tower  
        - `emmy.expression` and `emmy.abstract.number` 
            - add support for symbolic literals
        - Next we need an algebraic simplifier...
        - `emmy.pattern.{match,rule,syntax}` 
            - give us a pattern matching language  
        - `emmy.simplify.rules` 
            - adds a ton of simplification rules, out of which  
        - `emmy.simplify` 
            - builds a simplification engine Actually the
              simplifier has three parts... 
                - the first two start in `emmy.rational-function`
                  and `emmy.polynomial` and involve converting an
                  expression into either a polynomial or
                  a rational function and then back out, putting
                  them into "canonical form" in the process. 
        - That will send you down the rabbit hole of polynomial
          GCD etc...  And on and on! I'm happy to facilitate any
          code reading journey you go on or chat about Emmy or
          the original scmutils, feel free to write at sam [at]
          mentat.org, or else visit the Discord I run for the
          project at https://discord.gg/hsRBqGEeQ4

### Double Double Pendulum
    - With this activity you can explore the double double
      pendulum.  Two double pendula are simulated with nearly
      identical initial conditions.
    - The initial conditions of the first pendulum (the red one)
      are input. For the second pendulum (the blue one) the
      position of the lower pendulum bob is displaced by one
      micron.  The left panel shows the evolution of both double
      pendula.
    - Initially the blue pendulum is hidden by the red pendulum.
      The panel on the right shows the common logarithm of the
      difference between the angles of the lower bob for the two
      pendula.
    - http://groups.csail.mit.edu/mac/users/gjs/6946/Activities/dp6.html
    - $MY_CODE/scheme/SICM/pendulum-html/dp6.html

### Surfaces of section
    - With this activity you can explore surfaces of section for
      the driven pendulum.  Input the parameters you wish to
      explore, then click "start".
    - The evolution of the driven pendulum is shown in the left
      panel. The surface of section is shown in the right panel.
    - Click on the right panel to choose initial conditions, and
      the simulation will begin.  To try a different initial
      condition click the "restart" button.
    - http://groups.csail.mit.edu/mac/users/gjs/6946/Activities/driven-pendulum.html
    - $MY_CODE/Scheme/SICM/pendulum-html/driven-pendulum.html

    - There are 3 ways:
    1. mit-scheme -band mechanics.com
