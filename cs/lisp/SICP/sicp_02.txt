### Task:
    - Peano arithmetic - add 2 numbers
    - Both methods are recursive definitions but lead to different SHAPED processes
    - TIME  - is down the blackboard
    - SPACE  - (or memory) is width across blackboard

### method1 - ITERATION:
    - transfer from one pile to another
    - TIME O(x) - ie if x is one bigger, then its one more line down the board
    - SPACE O(1) - its always only one expression wide
    - comment: has all its state in explicit variables
    - comment: If the computation got whacked you can resume easier

### method2 - linear RECURSION:
    TIME O(x)
    SPACE O(x)
    transfer to a pile in your hand and then add them(?!)
    like a mountain turned on its side
    - comment: The computer has extra information under the table
    - comment: If computation whacked you dont know how many were supposed to be incremented

### Fibonacci
    - exponential recursion
    - time complexity O(fib n)
    - space complexity O(n) --->
        have to remember how far down the tree youve come
    - keep having to recalculate subtrees that you already calculated

### Towers of Hannoi
    - produces an exponential tree just like Fibonacci
    - theres No algorithm thats not going to take exponential time
