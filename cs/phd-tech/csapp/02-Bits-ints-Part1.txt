### Bit level representation of numbers
    - first computer actually used base 10. Had 10 vacuum tubes.
    - binary is nice because you can have 2 _voltages_, for '0'
      and for '1'
    - Really nice: each voltage has a bit of wiggle room: '1'
      could be any voltage between 0.9v and 1.1v
        - this allows a bit of 'noise' on the wires

### Binary representation of FRACTIONS
    - 1.5213 * 10^4 = 1.1101101101101 * 2^13
    - 0.1 in binary is 1/2
    - 0.01 in binary is 1/4

### Hex
    - annoying to be writing big strings of 1's and 0's

### Subtleties in C about signed/unsigned
