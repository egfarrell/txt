sage compile from source

### Parallel compile
    - /proc/cpuinfo
        - Read through that file to determine the number of cores
          on your system. 
    - Say your system has two cores and you want to use two
      threads for compiling Sage in parallel. Then use:
    - `$ SAGE_PARALLEL_SPKG_BUILD="yes"; export SAGE_PARALLEL_SPKG_BUILD`
    - `$ MAKE="make -j2"; export MAKE`
    - `$ make`

### sage installation
    export SAGE_INSTALL_GCC=yes 
    make
