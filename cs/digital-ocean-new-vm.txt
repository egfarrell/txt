### gecos
    - The GECOS field is the 5th entry in Unix `/etc/passwd`
      records, storing user account details like real name and
      contact.
    - (Originally, GECOS stood for "General Electric Comprehensive
      Operating Supervisor," which was an operating system
      developed by General Electric. However, in the context of
      Unix and Linux systems, the term has lost its original
      association and simply refers to this informational field.)

        "image":"ubuntu-22-10-x64"}' \
