### Tweaks for conversion
    - Reduce image size:
        - Look and Feel -> Styling -> Extra CSS:
        - img {max-height:50%; max-width:50%;}
    - Structure and Detection
        - ```
        - //*[name()='h1' or name()='h2' or name() = 'h3']
        - ```

### deDRM - Kindle 
    - General Help https://github.com/apprenticeharper/DeDRM_tools/blob/master/FAQs.md
    - Help about KFX format: https://www.mobileread.com/forums/showthread.php?t=291290

    - Key point: 
    - Need old `Kindle for PC` (1.17) for DeDRM to work!
    - Even `Kindle for PC` 1.24 won't work... 
      - (and no chance either for versions > 1.25)
    - (I have this exe on my SD Card and box.com)  
    - KindleForPC-installer-1.17.44170.exe:
    - MD-5: 53F793B562F4823721AA47D7DE099869
    - SHA-1: 73C404D719F0DD8D4AE1C2C96612B095D6C86255

      2C120144163200
