rsync tips

### incremental synchronise 2 folders
    - NOTE: 
    - put a final FORWARD SLASH after the "source/" and "target/"
      folders! (Oct 2022)
    - ```
    - rsync -rtvu   source/          target/
    - rupdry        ~/study/books/   /media/ef/EF_128GB/Study/books/
    - rupdry        ~/study/images/  /media/ef/EF_128GB/Study/images/

    - rupdel        ~/study/books/   /media/ef/EF_128GB/Study/books/
    - rupdel        ~/study/images/  /media/ef/EF_128GB/Study/images/
    - ```



### keep folders in sync 
    - see 'rupdel' alias: $HOME/.bash_aliases

### Keep image folders in sync
    rsync -av --ignore-existing /home/ef/study/images/ /media/ef/EF_128GB/Study/images/
    rsync -av --ignore-existing /media/ef/EF_128GB/Study/images/ /home/ef/study/images/

### rsync flags
    -a   archive (this flag implies recursive!)
    -c,  checksum  (Skip based on checksum, not mod-time & size)
    -h,  human readable
    -P   combines --progress and --partial.
    -r   recursive
    -t   preserve times
    -v   verbose
    -u   update. (only source files which are newer than destination will be copied)
    -z   compress


### rsync works both ways!
    ```
    rs server:/home/ef/remotefile  .
    rs ./localfile                 server:/home/ef/
    ```

### Copy entire folder AND rename
    - key point: trailing slash "/" after folder 'test'
    rs test/ cach:/home/iamb/datasets/uploads/test-rename

### throttle download speed (eg. 200kb per sec)
    (good for flaky dongle connections)
    rsync --bwlimit=200 ...

### spaces in filenames (use \ and "")
    rsync ... "host:/home/user/filename\ has\ spaces.flv" .
    other option:
        use -s and ""

### specify SSH port
    rsync -rvz -e 'ssh -p xxxxx' --progress  ./testit  ef@xxx.xxx.xxx.xxx:/home/ef/

### use ssh config - "evo"
    rsync -rvz  --progress ./testit evo:/home/ef/

### clean up after transfer
    --remove-sent-files
