### MP4 export
    - export profile: H.264
    - (to reduce the size, choose lower "frame rate")

### Can't see any filters
    - When you click FILTERS you should see a "+" button
      somewhere on the black window. 
    - When you click that button you will see a bunch of
      different filters. 
    - Keep in mind that you must have a video/audio clip selected
      to apply filters. 

### Add things to timeline:
    - Down in the bottom left hand corner, click button with
      3 horizontal lines
    - click "Add video track"
    - click open file
    - select a video file
    - drag down to timeline
    - Press <HOME> otherwise the audio track will start after the
      video track
    - click "Add Audio track"
    - click open file
    - select an audio file
    - drag down to timeline

### Remove a clip
    - START of clip: press "s" to define LEFT  boundary
    - END   of clip: press "s" to define RIGHT boundary
    - highlight clip
    - press "x"
