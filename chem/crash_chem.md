
### protons
    - the number of protons defines what element it is.
    - 79 protons - always gold!
    - 47 protons - always silver!
    - Its the defining trait of an element.
    - Depending on what its electrons are doing it might be
      shiny, or a different colour, or a cure for disease but its
      always a silver atom.
    - (Aside: Silver = Argentum = shiny gray stuff!)

### Nucleus
    - Nearly always very stable. Comes out of reactions unscathed,
      while electrons are always jumping around.

### Neutrons
    - Dont have any charge so don't really change the properties
      of an atom. 
    - All the protons couldn't crush into the nucleus together
      without the padding of the neutrons.
