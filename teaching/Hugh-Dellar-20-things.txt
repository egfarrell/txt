### Hugh Dellar - Twenty Things in Twenty Years

    https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=755gblfOjks

1. He fell into a 'ME' shaped hole. Teaching offered him freedom,
   creativity...

2. Conflict resolution mechanisms
    - (eg. an Italian walks into the class and says 'ah everyone
      from Japan'). 
    - His strategy: firm, friendly, slightly sarcastic, slightly
      funny. Failure to tackle this disruption leads to classes
      slipping out of control. ("trouble trouble, before trouble
      troubles you")

3. Kicking the grammar habit:
    - He had 'grammar anxiety', and he infected his students with
      'grammar anxiety'. We wasted hours debating slight
      grammatical differences... Eventually I vowed: never teach
      anything I would never say, or never hear said.

4. The way I was taught to teach grammar crippled my
   understanding of grammar.
    - Its a Lie that: 'Grammar (discrete structures) is at the
      heart of what language is'. 
    - This results in poor teaching and poor learning. 
    - Remember: accuracy emerges slowly, through exposure to
      repeated *patterns*...

5. There is no real need for 'needs analysis'.
    - Most students don't know what they need! They all think
      they need more grammar!
    - Remember: students need repeated exposure to the most
      *frequent* words in context... 
    - They need a better understanding of how those words work
      with other words. They need advice on how to go about
      learning this stuff. They need to listen/read a wide range
      of texts.

6. Resistance is futile!
    - Students believe English should be like their own language.
    - They show resistance. We should be patient and smooth the
      way for them by smiling, and agreeing that not everything
      makes sense!

7. INPUT is more important than output. (YES!!!!)
    - In the past he wasted hours with 'student talking time'.
      Their language knowledge didnt increase while talking in
      pairs. Students need guided graded input from the TEACHER!

8. Too much METHODOLOGY.
    - Theres actually too much innovation and creativity in ELT.
    - It has come to a stage where 'method' is valued over
      language knowledge and language awareness.

9. Most student errors are not grammar errors.
    - Their errors are more subtle and not always easily
      corrected! 
    - He noticed that the tasks he chose (grammar tasks) were
      often the reason students were producing simple grammatical
      errors. Broader, freer tasks are better.

    - If you really look at student output, the problem is more
      complex (than grammar): eg meanings have been
      half-understood, but the students dont understand the
      context. Meanings have been expressed, but have been
      expressed clumsily etc

10. Pronunciation is a neglected area.
    - Sometimes its hard to spend time on it, because it reaps
      little reward in the classroom. 
    - But pronouncing a foreign language is a _motor skill_ and
      needs drilling into the muscle memory (like learning
      a guitar). 
    - And final point: the main goal of pronunciation is to
      improve LISTENING!! (The 'jungle' of spoken language)

11. Brain theories and neuro-science about learning are just
    gobbledegook!

12. Course materials need to be woven together. 100 one-hour
    lessons dont make a 100 hour course.

13. The group is more important than the individual. In the
    classroom, the dynamic of the group is more important than
    just individual learners.

14. Phrasal verbs are badly taught! Just say 'these words go
    together, in this order. Learn them!'

15. 'Skills based' lessons are a total waste of time.
    - He thought he was teaching skills but he was actually
      teaching nothing.
    - Better talkers/readers dont have better 'skills', they have
      better LANGUAGE! So just teach the students more language!
      Use any text as a vehicle for NEW LANGUAGE.

16. Remember, digital lessons arent everything!
    - 50% of the world doesnt have access to the internet. In the
      3rd world, teachers teach and learners learn. We should
      remember this!

17. All this new technology means more WORK for teachers!
    - eg '17 apps your students must use... 20 ways to use
      a YouTube clip...'. 
    - If teachers are going to use all this new technology, then
      this preparation time should be paid time! We shouldnt be
      doing it all over the weekend. We should filter all the
      'stuff' on the internet.

18. Make time to read BOOKS.
    - He was influenced by the four 'Michaels'. Dont just read
      blogs and tweets and facebook!

19. Teaching is not an 'art' or a 'science'. Its a CRAFT!
    - not 'art': eg:  creativity, art, drama, poetry.
    - not 'science': eg: 100 words by 10 hours = success.
    - its a craft: eg: like the Japanese idea of a 'master'. 
    - Its a level of skill and wisdom acquired by repeating the
      same tasks over and over again, and reflecting on how you
      perform those tasks.

20. Times are getting tougher. Harder to publish ELT books now.
    Theres threats to teachers all around. But at the same time,
    he thinks: 'teaching is one of the best jobs in the world'
