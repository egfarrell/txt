### Access Your Potential (AYP):
    - These codes must be for the level they are working towards.
    - i.e. if they have an E3 qualification, they will be working
      towards L1.
    - The hour bands are for the hours they could achieve from
      their start date to the end of the course.
    - i.e. if a learner starts in October, and the course runs to
      May then they could do say 56 hours, so they would fit into
      this band:
        - Level 1, Foundations for Learning and Life, 45 to 68
          hrs, Learning Aim ref: Z0003487
