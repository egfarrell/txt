
L1 Topics
---------

Topic
Target
Language of Maths
Place value
Read, write, order and compare numbers up to one million
Recognise and use positive and negative numbers
Write out figures in words
Read temperatures on a thermometer
4 rules of whole numbers
Add and subtract using efficient written and mental methods
Multiply and divide using efficient written and mental methods
Use simple formulae expressed in words for one or two step calculations
Calculate the squares of one and two digit numbers
Follow the order of precedence of operators
Rounding and estimating
Round numbers to nearest 10, 100, 1000 to make approximate calculations
Estimate to check that answers are reasonable
Estimate answers to calculations using fractions and decimals
Multiply and divide
Multiply and divide whole numbers by 10, 100, 1000
Use multiplication facts and make connections with division facts
Fractions
Read, write, order and compare common fractions and mixed numbers
Correctly match equivalent fractions to each other
Find fractions of whole number quantities or measurements
Decimals
Read, write, order and compare decimals up to three decimal places
Add, subtract, multiply and divide decimals up to two decimal places
Approximate decimals by rounding to a whole number or to one or two decimal places
Equivalencies between decimals and fractions
Recognise and calculate equivalences between common fractions, percentages and decimals
Percentages
Read, write, order and compare simple percentages
Calculate percentages of quantities, including simple percentage increases and decreases by 5% and multiples of 5
Fractions, decimals, percentage equivalencies
Match common fractions, decimals and percentages
Ratio and direct proportion
Dilute liquids to a given ratio
Change quantities in a given recipe
Sharing an amount in a given ratio.
Length
Write down at least 2 units of measure for length
Estimate and accurately measure lengths of objects
Convert between metric measurements for length
Weights and capacities
Write down at least 2 units of measure for weights & capacity
Estimate and accurately measure weight and capacity of objects
Convert between metric measurements for weights and capacity
Time
Write dates in common formats
Write the time in the 12-hour and 24-hour clock
Area and perimeter
Calculate the area and perimeter of simple shapes including those that are made up of a combination of rectangles
Volume
Accurately calculate the volume of cubes & cuboids.
Graphs, charts, tables and diagrams
Correctly extract and interpret information from lists, tables, charts and graphs
Collect , organise and represent discrete data in tables, diagrams and charts including pie charts, bar charts and line graph
Group discrete data and represent grouped data graphically
Mean(average) and range
Calculate mean for the a set of numbers
Calculate the range for a set of numbers
Probability
Understand probability on a scale from 0 (impossible) to 1 (certain) and use probabilities to compare the likelihood of events
Use equally likely outcomes to find the probabilities of simple events and express them as fractions
Problem Solving – Measure, shape and space
Solve simple problems involving ratio,
Convert between units of length, weight, capacity, money and time, in the same system
Recognise and make use of simple scales on maps and drawings
Measure, shape and space
Draw 2-D shapes and demonstrate an understanding of line symmetry and knowledge of the relative size of angles
Interpret plans, elevations and nets of simple 3-D shapes
Angles
Use angles when describing position and direction, and measure angles in degrees
Money
Calculate simple interest in multiples of 5% on amounts of money
Calculate discounts in multiples of 5% on amounts of money
