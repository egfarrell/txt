
L2 Topics
---------

Number and place value
Read, write, order and compare positive and negative numbers of any size
Carry out calculations with numbers up to one million including strategies to check answers including estimation and approximation
Relationship between multiples, factors and prime numbers
Use mental and written methods of calculation to generate results when solving problems using whole numbers of any size
Complete calculations using the words multiple, prime number and factor and relate them to multiplication and division facts
Ratio and Proportion
Understand and calculate using ratios, direct proportion and inverse proportion Solve problems involving the number of parts in a given ratio, and the value of one part
Discuss examples of inverse proportion
Simple Algebra
Representing variables and constant factors)
Use formula to calculate cost of gas bills
Evaluate expressions and make substitutions in given formulae in words and symbols to produce results.
Follow the order of precedence of operators, including indices
Evaluation of a number as fraction of another
Order, add, subtract and compare amounts or quantities using proper and improper fractions and mixed numbers 
Use fraction to compare two numbers
Express one number as a fraction of another
Equivalent fractions
Match equivalent fractions
Decimals
Order, approximate and compare decimals 
Add, subtract, multiply and divide decimals up to three places
Use exchange rate to calculate the amount of foreign currency required
Percentage of quantities
Solve problems involving percentages
Calculate percentage change (any size increase and decrease), and original value after percentage change 
Calculate amounts of money, compound interest, percentage increases, decreases and discounts including tax and simple budgeting
Evaluation of one number as a percentage of another
Work out percentages of amounts and express one amount as a percentage of another 
Know and use strategies to check answers obtained with a calculator
Equivalent fractions, decimals and percentages
Complete a table of equivalent fractions, decimals and percentages 
Convert percentage savings and profit to fraction and decimals
Fractions, decimals and percentages
Apply fraction, decimal and percentage in problem solving situations
Identify and know the equivalence between fractions, decimals and percentages
Metric  & Imperial measurement
Categorise metric and imperial units of length, distance, weight, capacity 
Read scales to different levels of accuracy, including reading between marked divisions
Convert between metric and imperial units of length, weight and capacity using a) a conversion factor and b) a conversion graph
Compound measures
Calculate using compound measures including speed, density and rates of pay
Area, perimeter and volume
Use given formulae to find areas of regular and composite shapes  including triangles and circles and non-rectangular shapes (formulae given except for triangles and circles)
Use formulae to find volumes and surface areas of 3-D shapes including cylinders (formulae to be given for 3-D shapes other than cylinders)
Scale Drawing
Calculate actual dimensions from scale drawings 
create a scale diagram given actual measurements
Use coordinates in 2-D, positive and negative, to specify the positions of points
2D and 3D shapes 
Understand and use common 2-D representations of 3-D objects 
Draw 3-D shapes to include plans and elevations 
Calculate values of angles and/or coordinates with 2-D and 3-D shapes 
Solve problems involving 2-D shapes and parallel lines
Extracting Data
Extract and interpret information from lists, tables, charts and graphs
Organising and representing data.
Measure and present information as a chart / graph.
Collect and record data over a period of time. Display the data on a chart or graph
Draw and interpret scatter diagrams and recognise positive and negative correlation
Present findings in a suitable way
Present outcome of investigation using a pie chart and bar chart.
Collecting and presenting data
Carry out an investigation and present the outcome in a suitable form
Averages & range
Calculate mean, median and mode.
Estimate the mean of a grouped frequency distribution from discrete data
Use the mean, median, mode and range to compare two sets of data
Discuss the distinctions that each average is useful for different purposes.
Find the range and use it to describe the spread within sets of data
Probability
Work out the probability of combined events including the use of diagrams and tables, including two-way tables
Express probabilities as fractions, decimals and percentages
