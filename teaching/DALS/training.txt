- Home working with DSE – As we move forward and continue working from home we
  need to revisit the DSE assessments.
- All staff need to complete the following:
- Complete the attached DSE assessment and send back to your line manager.
- Complete the working at home assessment on iDerby, this is the link:
  https://iderby.derby.gov.uk/apps/departmentforms/HWHSSA.aspx
- Managers
- Discuss the DSE assessment with your individual team members and refer staff
  to advice/guidance on iDerby
- Emailing to groups of people – We've had a data breach this week with emails
  going out to groups of learners where they can see each other's personal email
  addresses. As a reminder, when you have to send an email to a group of
  external recipients (e.g. learners) you must use the 'BCC' (blind copy)
  option. This is to ensure we are protecting personal data and we do not breach
  our Data Protection/GDPR policies. All data breaches have to be reported to
  the DCC Information Governance team and possibly the ICO.
- Mandatory CPD training – All staff are required to complete the updated
  GDPR/Cyber security training by Friday 29th May and send evidence of this
  having been completed to enquiries@derby.gov.uk. Any access issues need to be
  logged via a ticket on iDerby, or speak to your line manager if you can't do
  this yourself.
