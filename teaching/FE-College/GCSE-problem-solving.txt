    Sum and Difference
    (a) Find two three-digit numbers that add to make 204
            ................................... and ...................................

    (b) Find two numbers, each less than 200, that differ by 150
            ................................... and ...................................


    Answers:
        101, 103
        (b) Two numbers less than 200 that differ by 150.
        eg, 199 & 49, 198 & 48, 197& 47, 196 & 46 etc,
        down to 151 & 1



    ### Put the numbers in
        - (a) Either of:
        - (60 – 20) – (80 – 50) = 10
        - (50 – 20) – (80 – 60) = 10

        - (b) Any answer which works
        - eg, (50 – 5) – (30 – 20) = 35
