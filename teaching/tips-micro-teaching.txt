### micro teaching
    - https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FpoegE1L25Y
    - prepare, teach, evaluate
    - decide what you want to teach, aims & objectives, what's
      practical
    - how are you going to involve everyone? what are they going
      to do?
    - devise a 'sparkling' starter activity
    - what resources will you need? Pictures, realia, powerpoint
      (remember, have a BACKUP PLAN)
    - how will you assess each stage: a quiz, Monitoring, Q&A
    - summarise your session.
    - have a rationale: explain and justify the reasons for your
      choices
    - complete a self-evaluation form
    - outcomes should be "SMART" - specific and measurable


### Preparing for micro teaching
    - https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=le_yAVd0CNg
    - Select topic you are passionate about! (and have knowlege
      about so you can answer questions)
    - select something neither too simple or two difficult
      (stretch your learners)
    - ensure the session isnt a lecture/presentation:
        - have learner activity/exploration
    - 1/3 to 2/3 rule. Teacher talking only 1/3 time.
    - Plan using a sensible, coherent structure.
        - intro (warmup) 5 mins
        - main
        - conclusion: learning is assesed
    - STATE what you want your learners to learn in 2 or
      3 clearly defined outcomes
    - outcome DIFFERENTIATION: core outcomes and a set of extended
      outcomes to stretch learners.
    - make sure learners have access to different sorts of
      learning in the lesson.
        - analyse
        - interpret
        - compare
        - evaluate
        - create

### Micro Teach
    - https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hFNkBsJca0c
    - Who - dyslexia, diabetic
    - What - Subject - something you're confident about
    - When
    - Where - what equipment and resources
    - Why - pass assement / get qualification
    - How - lesson plan / session plan (timing)

    - Structure!
        - BEGINNING:
            - say name,
            - find out if learners know something already
        - MIDDLE:
            - main section where learning takes place
            - keep it ACTIVE
            - paired activity, discussions, role-play, group activities
            - or teacher demos something, then learners try
            - teacher must keep involved when learners are doing
              something!
        - END
            - Recap aim
            - Say Thank you

    - Tip: Fill time: PPP a question:
        - Pose,  (for everyone! not just one learner)
        - Pause,
        - Pounce/Pick
