### Lesson Structure
    1. presentation of the purpose of the lesson
    2. repetition of material / homework check
    3. warm-up
    4. presentation of the material
    5. exercises
    6. linguistic production - work in subgroups
    7. lesson summary

    - Remember!
    - The teacher is a supportive and not dominant person in the classroom.
    - A key element of the lesson is the stage of free linguistic production.
    - Not every mistake should be corrected.
    - A lesson is an interaction between students, not a lecture.
    - The lesson should start with a presentation goal and end with a summary.
