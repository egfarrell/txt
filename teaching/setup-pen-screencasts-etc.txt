### Record iPad Screen (ie Notability App for Screencasts)
    - UPDATE: I use DOCERI now! Can record and export from the
      ipad, with wrist guard
    - Use ZOOM Windows Program
        - Start a meeting
        - Share Screen
        - Connect iPad using airplay
        - Press alt+R to start recording
        - Press alt+R to stop recording
        - Video is place in "Documents\Zoom"
        - https://support.zoom.us/hc/en-us/articles/201379235-Screen-Share-iPhone-iPad-Apps

### Recording "real" screencast using camera
    - A suggestion. Please use a cd marker on a white sheet of
      paper or whiteboard with white light (6500 k) from left
      side. It will be much more legible.
