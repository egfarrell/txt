    - open-question-mindset (OQM)

      journey to get there


### Developing open question approaches
    - thinking time (30 secs)
    - time out for thinking and talking
    - no hands questioning (can put pressure on other students)
    - phone a friend
    - preview questions
    - high challenge evaluative or creative questions
    - sequencing
    - big questions (setting thoughtful/substantial questions)
    - Fat questions (dont accept answers less than 15 words)
    - Skinny questions (force a shorter answer)
