# EXAMPLE

total word count: 2000

Would a behaviour intervention help to reduce the amount of low
level disruption.


### Rationale (750 words)
    - why? context. Explore the "why" question and related theory e.g.
    - Maslow. Look at other people related to behaviour e.g. Rogers,
    - Vizard
    - Need sources (theory) here.
    - Secondary research - you are consuming it.


### Evidence
    - Reference list (Harvard)


### Critical Analysis (1000 words)
    - e.g. used Australian author Rogers in Rationale, then examine
    - implications of it being from an Australian context
    - Pros/Cons.
    - Need sources (theory) here.


### Methods
    - Questionnaire (e.g. to Staff / students)
    - observations
    - promonitor data / group profiles
    - your summative assessments - achievements background
    - reflective diary (beware bias)

    - NO WORD COUNT -> technically not needed! However show an
    awareness of this

### Ethics
    - need to show an awareness of potential ethical issues
    - impact of intervention (e.g. only did it in one class and
      not the other. Could be giving one class an advantage over
      another)
    - age of students
    - consent (if doing observations on a student - but will
      consent impact on their behaviour)
    - impact on colleagues


### Action plan (750 words)
    - can use an action plan table
    - if you do your surveys / observation - then I'll try out
      some interventions
    - then you need to give timescales
    - draw up survey (1 week)
    - wait for responses (10 days)


    - what you are going to do, how long it'll take and how
      you'll use it / measure it
