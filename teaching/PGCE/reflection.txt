
### What?
    - I had a very difficult group who were hard to keep engaged
      and tended to be on their phones.  I noticed the the
      classroom was setup in a way that the desks were scattered
      randomly around the room in groups of 2. 

### So what?
    - Because the desks were pointed in all directions some
      students didn't really face the board and some had their
      backs to me when I was explaining things. The room and the
      lesson appeared a bit scattered.


### What next?
    - For the next lesson I decided to arrive early and have the
      desks arranged in 5 lines all facing the board. This will
      signal that there is a real lesson going on and not some
      kind of disucssion group.
