### 9 Understand how to involve learners and others in the assessment process

(600 words minimum)

- 9.1 Explain why it is important to involve learners and others
  in the assessment process

    - The student should write a brief essay explaining the
      reasons why is it important to involve learners and others
      in the assessment process.


- 9.2 Explain the role and use of peer- and self- assessment in
  the assessment process

    - They should then explain the role and use of peer- and
      self-assessment in the assessment process.


- 9.3 Identify sources of information that should be made
  available to learners and others involved in the assessment
  process

    - Finally they should identify sources of information which
      should be made available to learners and others involved in
      the assessment process
