### 5 Plan inclusive teaching and learning

- 5.1 Devise an inclusive teaching and learning plan

    - The student submits a session plan for the MicroTeach on
      the subject of their choice.

    - (Please read the information in the assignment "MicroTeach
      Session Details" for further details regarding your
      MicroTeach session before you devise your plan)

    - You may use the blank session plan from the learning
      materials, or devise your own similar plan.



(400 words minimum).
