
### 11 Understand requirements for keeping records of assessment in education and training

(400 words minimum).

- 11.1 Explain the need to keep records of assessment of learning

    - The student should complete an essay explaining the need to
      keep records of assessment of learning.

- 11.2 Summarise the requirements for keeping records of
  assessment in an organisation

    - They should then summarise record keeping requirements
      within their OWN organisation.

- Please include reference to LEGAL requirements in regard to
  record keeping, such as the Data Protection Act 2018 and the
  Freedom of Information Act 2000.
