### Good Study habits
    - https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=23Xqu0jXlfs

    - BREAK YOUR STUDYING INTO CHUNKED SESSIONS
        - _30 mins max_, then 5 min break

    - CREATE A DEDICATED STUDY AREA
        - the "study lamp" on/off trick

    - STUDY ACTIVELY:
        - Facts or Concepts?
            - Concepts more important.
                - Put them in your own words.
        - Also highlighted words:
            - when you see highlighted words are you _recalling_
              what they mean, or just _recognising_ them?
            - Humans very good at recognising
            - So quiz yourself, to make sure you are actually
              recalling something

    - TAKE MORE EFFECTIVE NOTES:
        - rewrite/recap asap after class

    - SUMMARIZE OR _TEACH_ WHAT YOU LEARN
        - record screencast etc

    - SQ3R:
        - Survey Question,
        - Read,
        - Recite
        - Review
        - _Survey_ the chapter before reading...
        - ie go to the END and read summary

    - Facts are harder to remember than concepts. Use mnemonics:
        - acronyms
        - coin idiomatic sayings: "In 1492, Columbus sailed the ocean blue"
        - image associations

    - MY ORIGINAL NOTES
        - break study into little 30 min blocks
            - then take a 5 min break
            - start studying again...
            - $MY_IMAGES/misc/study-efficiency-half-hour-chuncks.jpg
        - Treats
            - when you reach the end of your study period,
            - give yourself a _big treat_
        - "state dependent memory":
            - some students used to study after beer... then you
              only recall/remember when intoxicated(!?)
        - Environment/Lamp:
            - we're controlled by environmental queues: studying
              in bedrooms not good
            - _turn on your study lamp_, sit and study,
                - as soon as you lose your edge...
                - stand up, switch the _study lamp off_...
