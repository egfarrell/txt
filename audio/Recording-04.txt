From the book, the same Jesus, a contemporary christiology, three main ideas constitute this soteriological understanding.
Number one, Jesus redeemed the human race by his fidelity even unto death and not by his death itself.
It's inaccurate to say without qualification that Jesus saves us because our immediate
saving contact with God is through the Holy Spirit. Number three, Jesus' achievement of divinization
constituted in human history a new possibility for human becoming, a new epitome. In this way
Jesus became central to the salvation of all humankind whether people know and reference him or not.
