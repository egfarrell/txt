Fire a light beam down a lift, as the lift is accelerated upwards.

The light beam, when it reaches the bottom of the lift, will be
doppler blue-shifted.  (Because an observer on the lift floor is being
accelerated towards the light wave which makes the wave fronts sqrunch
shorter (ie bluer))

The same happens to a light laser beam fired down a tower in Earths
gravitaitonal field. Its blue shifted.

The opposite happens when a light beam is shot up an accelerated lift
shaft or away from a gravitational field. Its red shifted. When light
leaves the surface of the sun, we can see from its spectral lines that
its slightly red-shifted when it reaches earth.

Note if the lift and light beam were both in free-fall, no shift in
the light wavelength would be detected. Equivalence priniciple.

Mass presents an obstruction to getting rid of the gravity field due
to a coordinate transformation.

Gave 2 examples of the chain rule for calculating the change in
a coordinate and for switching between "x" and "y"  coordinate
systems. Also introduced Einstein summation convention.

Tensors: Scalar is tensor of rank 0
Vectors are tensors of rank 1.

Vectors and tensors have special transformation properties. You cant
build a tensor out of 3 scalars (pressure, temp, humidity) because
when you transform them they stay at the same value in the new
coordinate system.

Contravariant vector: upstairs
Like a matrix equation with 2 column vectors and a matrix.

Covariant vectors: downstairs
Originally introduced to show how to switch between x and y coordinate
systems. Class of objects (gradients) who transform according to the
second chain rule.

Metric tensor
Curved space has an obstruction in it that prevents the use of flat coordinates.

In cartesian coordinates he gave a neat form of Pythagoras for ds,
using the kronecker delta. The kronecker is actually the metric tensor
when using cartesian coordinates!

So if you have some mad metric tensor R, and you can undo it and
transform it back to the kronecker, then you were dealing with a flat
space all along.

Sometimes you can transform PART of a space back to flat space, but
not the whole space because of some obstruction. i.e. tip of cone is
an obstruction to flatness although you can paint flat coordinates
onto a large part of the cone.

Polar coords in flat space. Theres no cross term. These means circles
of constant r and lines of constant theta are perpendicular to each
other.
