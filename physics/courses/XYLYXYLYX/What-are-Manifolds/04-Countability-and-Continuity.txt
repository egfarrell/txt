### Homeomorphisms
    - if the function `f` is continuous
    - and the inverse `f^-1` is a function (1-to-1) and
      continuous
    - then we're dealing with a Homeomorphism 
    - Homeomorphisms preserve
        - Compactness
        - Separability
        - Connectedness
