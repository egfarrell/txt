### Closed Set
    - easy to define
    - is the complement of some open set
    - ie the complement of an open ball, is the boundary of the
      open ball, and the entire rest of the plane... 
	- $MY_IMAGES/physics/what-are-manifolds/complement_of_open_set.jpg

### Limit Points
    - retreat back to the notion of a _neighbourhood_
    - aside: an open set is a neighbourhood of all it's points 
    - open Neighbourhood: 
        - is an open set that contains a point 
    - Definition of Limit Point 
        - `S` is a subset of `X` 
        - `p` is a limit point of subset `S` if every open
          neighbourhood of `p` also contains at least one point of `s`
        - $MY_IMAGES/physics/what-are-manifolds/limit_point.jpg

### counter-intuitive limit points
        - the actual limit-point itself doesnt have to be in `s` 
        - the limit point could be on the open boundary of `s` 
        -  but all it's neighbourhoods will contain at least
           1 point in `s`, therefore `p` is a limit point of `s`

### Closure of a set
    - the set plus its limit points
