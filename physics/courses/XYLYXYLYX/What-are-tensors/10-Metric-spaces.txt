### "Promotion"
    - a vector space that has an inner product, is called an
      inner product space (or a metric space)
    - I think there's a different between an "inner product
      space" and a "vector space"
    - the vector space is "promoted" to an inner product space

### How to build a metric
    - note: an inner product can only take vectors from the same
      vector space... can't mix in vectors from another space...
    - two vectors fed into an inner product... quite long when
      you write it all out
    - $MY_IMAGES/physics/what-are-tensors/inner-product-written-out.jpg
    - key: if you know what the inner-product does on all
      combinations of the basis vectors (to produce a number)
      then you know how it operates on any vector in the vector
      space.
