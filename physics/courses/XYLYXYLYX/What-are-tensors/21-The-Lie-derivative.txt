### 3 types of derivatives
    - covariant derivative
    - lie: helps you understand killing vectors
    - exterior derivative 

Interesting:
    - another way of defining a tangent (vector) space at a point
      in terms of curves
        - its the set of all tangent vectors to all possible curves
          that go through that point
        - so thats a vector space at a point 

- the coordinate basis was used as a representation for the
  vector fields at each point in spacetime

  going through every point
