### Not a vector space
    - note: the set you get when you create a cartesian product
      of a vector space with itself, is not a vector space
    - you'd have to define an addition, scalar mult etc 
    - (you could turn it into a vector space by using the _direct
      sum_) 

- The tensor product is kind of a "map" 
    - the `[β ⊗ α]` is a single map made from the tensor product
      of two maps from the dual space `β` and `α`. 
    - This tensor product operates on two vectors in the original
      vector space, and the result is a real number
    - $MY_IMAGES/physics/what-are-tensors/construction-of-a-tensor.jpg
