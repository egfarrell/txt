- new notion of a vector: a vector is an element of a _set_

- what kind of SET makes a vector space?

    - well it must have _vector addition_
    - you put a vector on the left, vector on the right, and the
      result is a vector
    - important: addition only works for vectors in the set. It's
      not a general rule that allows you add vectors from
      different vector spaces or different spaces altogether. It
      only allows you take 2 vectors and the output vector is
      also in the set. (ie closure)

    - put out of a number out of the Real number "bin"

### Dimensionality
    - if I draw a vector out of `V`, whats the minimum number of
      other vectors I need, to linerly combine them to create
      `q`?

### isomorphic
    -  if two spaces have the same field and Dimension, they are
       practically the same, apart from the name (isomorphic)
