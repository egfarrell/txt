### Differential Operator
    - a (differentiable) function of 4 variables: 
        - `f(X^0,X^1,X^2,X^3) = f(X^μ) = f(X)`
    - the 4 variables will turn out to be coordinates 
        - $MY_IMAGES/physics/what-are-tensors/intro-to-Differential-Operator.jpg
    - also heres the notation:
        - $MY_IMAGES/physics/what-are-tensors/notation-partial-differentiation.jpg

- The differential operators are linear! Like everything...
	- $MY_IMAGES/physics/what-are-tensors/differential-operators-are-linear.jpg

- The set of all Linear operators capable of operating on
  4 variables is a vector space. (It's not an inner product
  space.. just a plain vector space)

- It is a `realisation` of a 4D Vector Space.. 

- The linear operators are like a bunch of tools that work on
  functions.... but the "tools" or operators themselves have
  their own linear properties!
