### woa(!!)
    - whenever you have a force proportional to the mass you can
      count on it being a so-called fictitious force
    - and if you look at the gravitational force in Newtonian
      Physics: `F = - GMm/r^2`
    - you see it's `∝` to the mass, and could it be that gravity
      is a fictitious force of some kind???? :)

- The fictitious part of the potential grows much faster as the
  radius shrinks, and it apparent keeps the particle away from
  you... keeps it at bay.
