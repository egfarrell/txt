- nothing is _stationary_ in GR

### Preamble
    - Worldlines are a foundational concept for geodesics
    - Test particles follow worldlines
    - Put the _time_ axis in the centre of your thinking! 
        - get `X^0` out there in your mind
    - You cannot "stand still" in spacetime. Even if you are
      still in spacetime, Time is always moving...
    - How "fast" are you moving through time (proper time)
    - We measure time in terms of metres. And everything moves
      through time at the speed of light `c`
    - So you're always flying through spacetime at the speed of
      light
    - every particle has a 4-velocity on it's worldline. 
        - And that 4-velocity is `c`
        - $MY_IMAGES/physics/What-is-GR/4-velocity.jpg
        - `dτ` is proper time. Measured by the clock on the test
          particle
