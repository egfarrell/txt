- key task: take an embedded surface (a 3-sphere embedded in 4D
  space), and find the metric of the embedded surface, by
  utilising the metric of the embedding space

- the metric tells us how to take the inner product at every
  point

- The connection tells us about parallelism . 
- Also: the connection ties together tangent spaces...

- the vector actually lives in the tangent space at each point,
  although a lot of books just draw the vector directly on the
  spacetime point
	- $MY_IMAGES/physics/What-is-GR/vector-in-tangent-space.jpg

- tangent space = tensor product space 
    - but each tangent space lives in it's own little universe,
      not connected to any other tangent spaces 

- Really to do GR we need a "spacetime" made of 3 things:
    1. A manifold 
    2. A metric 
    3. A connection 
    - But wait, you can build the connection from the metric, so
      you only need 2 things! 

- wihtout a metric you have nothing 
    - even if you have coordinates "close" to each other, but
      without a metric that doesn't mean anything!
