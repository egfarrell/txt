operational approach:

Preparation P
    - P stands for list of preparation instructions
    - P gets a vector in Hilbert space.
    - Now in the operational mode, the P vector is the
      preparation instructions
    - NOT the physical state of the atom etc that comes out
      of the preparation procedure...

- Measurement M
    - Represented by Hermitian operator. It just
      represents the lists of instructions needed to carry
      out the measurement.

Discussion about pros and cons of operationalism

- Sometimes a state in Hilbert space isn't enough to describe
  a preparation.

- Why not? Because you could have a state inside a box
  randomly selected by rolling a dice.

- With a random or unknown preparation a density operator
  is used to describe the probabilities of a certain
  measurement outcome.

- Also reduced density matrix.
