### average velocity

    - `V_avg = (Rf - Ri) / (Tf - Ti)`
    -       `= ∆R / ∆T`

    - Theres really 3 equations here! for x,y,z components

    - but average velocity doesnt tell you everything!
      instantaneous velocity gives more info.


### position update equation

    - just a re-arrangement of the average velocity formula above
    - `Rf = Ri + (Vavg)(∆T)`


### average velocity note:

### momentum
    - `p = mv`
    - not quite right
    - `p = γ m v`

    - `γ = 1` for things moving slowly (e.g. 30 m/s)
    - `γ = ∞` for things moving at the speed of light
    - i.e. you cant get to the speed of light!


### high speeds
    - plot of increasing `γ`
    - (non-linear?)
    - if you double the speed, you MORE than double the momentum
    - it can a lot more force to stop something at high momentum
