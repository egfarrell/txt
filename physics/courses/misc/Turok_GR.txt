compatibility condition
	when messing around with cristofels and connections
	you need to show that going from region A to region B to region C,
	gives you the same as going from region A to C
	Apparently this "compatibility connection" important for "fibre bundles"
	Riemann found out that this condition is satisfied when he used
	his transformation law of the connection.
	So all coordinate systems when they overlap are consistent.

torsion

	and this part is the TORSION?
	And... people ASSUME the torsion is zero?
	In supergravity the torsion isnt zero, its a small number
	related to the spin density or something.
