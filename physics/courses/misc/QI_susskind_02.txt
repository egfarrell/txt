# QI susskind 02

- classical bit
- stick: up or down

### Electron between bar magnet
    - The magnetic field affects the electron, but instead of
      snapping into alignment, it precesses round and round
      first, but finally settling down.
    - ie it radiates energy

1. prepare state: put in magnetic field
2. detect state: put ANOTHER magnetic field and see how much
   energy radiated
3. Continuous radiation output: as you vary the angle of the
   prepared electron, a different amount of energy is radiated
- WRONG!!! THATS CLASSICAL!

- for a real quantum electron, you either get
    - no emission of radiation or
    - exactly on lump (quantum, photon) of radiation.

- Its like the electron only has two states, `up` and `down`.

### Electron response
    - either DOES give off a photon (of specific energy) or it
      DOESNT give off one.

### Interesting
    - You can prepare an electron state in any direction, but
      when measure it you only get UP and DOWN.

### Preparation horizontal.
    - Then put into vertical magnetic field
    - Probability `1/2` that it emits a photon.
    - ie you get a probability distribution, and the distribution
      seems to have a memory how the electron was prepared.
