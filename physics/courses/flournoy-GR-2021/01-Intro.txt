### Conclusions
    - There's no "correspondence principle" between GR and SR
      (but there is a "link" between them... see below)
    - GR is a generalisation of the theory of Newtonian Gravity.
    - There should be a limit we can take in GR, where we reduce
      to Newtonian Gravity.
    - Link(s) between GR and SR?
        - GR has solutions, and one solution will be SR.
          Basically you take out all the sources that curve
          spacetime, and solve the equation, and the solution you
          get is flat spacetime. And that's the spacetime of SR.
        - (But it's not a generalisation stemming from
          a correspondence principle, it's just a solution of GR)

### Compare GR to Relativistic EM
    - Equations 1 and 2 stem from...? 
        - if you apply the Euler-Lagrange equation to the EM
          Lagrangian, you get the first two equations...
        - ie Equations 1 and 2 are the resulting _equations of
          motion_ stemming from the EM Lagrangian
    - Equations 3 and 4 stem from the Bianchi Identity. 
        - The Bianchi Identity is a statement about the
          underlying EM _geometry_. 
        - NOT the spacetime geometry though, rather the geometry
          of the electric & magnetic fields OVER spacetime. 
        - Each point in spacetime has a "blade of grass"
          representing the electric field value, and another
          blade of grass representing the magnetic field value,
          and you take that field of grass and it has
          a volumetric geometry more so than the spacetime upon
          which it was born.
