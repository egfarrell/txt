### Principle of (Special?) Relativity
    - The laws of physics should take the same form for all
      observers in _inertial frames_.
        - This holds not just for Einstein's relativity but for
          Galilean relativity too
        - I never fully appreciated that it's just for inertial
          frames.

### Speed of light
    - it's not that `c` is important for SR, it's that the speed
      of ANYTHING massless (incl. light) cannot exceed this speed
      and must appear constant in all frames.
        - ah so maybe that's why gravitational waves travel at
          speed `c` as well? (as Lee Smolin asked somewhere)

### Difference between COVARIANCE and INVARIANCE!
    - invariance: keeps the same value before and after
      a transformation before: A=B, after A=B
    - covariance: if I do something to the lhs: M*A, then I must
      do the same thing to the rhs: M*B... afterwards, A will
      still equal B, but A and B may have different values than
      before the transformation...
