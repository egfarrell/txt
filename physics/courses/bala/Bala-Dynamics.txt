### Overview
    - A basic course in CM at the senior Undergraduate/Masters
      level. It covers the Lagrangian and Hamiltonian formalisms.

    - Places the subject in the broader context of classical
      dynamical systems. The phase space description is
      highlighted, and the distinction between integrable and
      chaotic Hamiltonian systems is shown.

    - The role of symmetries is explained, and also the
      connection between symmetry, invariance and conservation
      principles.
        - (Key Suskind diagram too)
        - $MY_BOOKS/Physics/CM/Susskind-Noethers-theorem.pdf

    - SR is covered, with an emphasis on invariance and symmetry
      as applied to classical mechanics.


High level outline:
----------------------------
    - 3 lectures
        - Mechanics of a system of particles in vector form
        - Conservation of linear momentum
        - Energy and Angular momentum


    - 6 lectures
        - Degrees of freedom
        - generalized coordinates and velocities
        - Lagrangian action and Principle of Extremal Action
        - Euler-Lagrange equations
        - Constraints
        - Applications of Lagrangian formalism


    - 6 lectures
        - Legendre transform
        - generalized momenta
        - Hamilton's equations of motion
        - Phase space
        - Phase trajectories
        - Applications to systems with small number of dof's


    - 1 lecture
        - Scattering in a central potential
        - Rutherford formula
        - scattering cross section

    - 2 lectures
        - Charged particle in an EM field

    - 2 lectures
        - Non-inertial frames of reference and pseudo-forces:
        - centrifugal
        - Coriolis and Euler forces

    - 3 lectures
        - Rotations in 3 dimensions
        - Euler angles
        - Angular momentum
        - Elements of rigid-body dynamics
        - The symmetric top

    - 3 lectures
        - Small oscillations
        - Normal mode analysis
        - Normal modes of simple molecules and harmonic chains

    - 5 lectures
        - Basic ideas on Dynamical Systems: conservative v. dissipative systems
        - Hamiltonian systems and Liouville's theorem
        - Constants of motion and symmetries in CM
        - `Noether's Theorem` and connection between symmetry, invariance and conservation

    - 4 lectures
        - Canonical transformations
        - Poisson brackets
        - Action-angle variables
        - Integrable Hamiltonians
        - Non-integrable systems and elements of chaotic motion

    - 2 lectures
        - SR: Inertial frames
        - Principle and postulate of Relativity
        - Lorentz transformations
        - Length contraction, time dilation and the Doppler effect
        - Velocity addition formula
        - Rapidity

    - 5 lectures
        - Four-vectors and tensors
        - Energy-momentum of a free particle
        - Relativistic invariance of physical laws
        - The wave equation
        - Transformation properties of EM fields
        - Homogeneous and Inhomogeneous Lorentz groups
