### Stationary States:
    - In QM the particle doesnt have _separate_ definate values
      for KE and PE. Just for the total. 
    - This is because the momentum and position operators dont
      commute. 
    - This is different to the classical hamiltonian in a well
      where you can get separate values for `KE` and `PE` and add
      them.

- The expectation value of the `KE` is always positive. 
- Expectation is linked to the norm (modulus) which is always
  positive.

### Isospectral oscillators.
    - Linear Harmonic oscillator is first member of an infinite
      family which have EQUALLY SPACED energy levels. 
    - The harmonic appears again and again, even in QFT where you
      add one more particle to a quantum field and you increase
      the energy by a constant amount (adding the particles rest
      energy or mass)

- Cannot write position and momentum in finite Matrices. Must be
  infinite matrices.

- Basically because `p` and `x` dont commute. And if you take the
  trace of both sides of the commutator equation you get
  a contradiciton.

### DIRAC ladder operators
    - He solves the harmonic operator using Diracs method which
      splits the 2nd order eigen-equation into two 1st order
      equations. 
    - Basically rewrites the operators `p` and `x` as
      dimensionless operators `A` and `A† `which commute to `1`. 
    - These ladder operators (or creation and annaliation
      operators) simplify things.
