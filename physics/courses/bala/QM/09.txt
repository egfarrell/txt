### QM Nptel 09

- Constants in infinite potential well:
    - `L`, `m`, `hbar`

- Constants not used:
    `Kb`: Working at 0K so no boltzmann constant
    `G`:  No gravity in setup
    `C`:  No speed of light - assuming non-relativistic setup


- Normalisation condition helps produce distinctive solution.

- The eigenstates of the infinite-well Hamiltonian in position
  space aren't eigenstates in momentum space.

- But the position eigenstates could be represented as
  a superposition of 2 momentum eigenstates.

- (by rewriting `sin(x)` using `e`)

- The you get two sharp peaks at `-hbar kn` and `+hbar kn`
    - ie like two delta functions.

- But! remember we said the eigenstates have to be in `L2` ie
  square integrable.

- And the dirac delta function isnt in `L2`.

- The resolution! You dont integrate from `-∞` to `∞` BUT from `0` to `L`.

- This stops the momenta from becoming delta functions. Theyre actually smooth functions instead.
