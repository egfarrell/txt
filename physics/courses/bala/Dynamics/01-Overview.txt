In mathematics, dynamical systems has a specific meaning, it is
studied under `Ergodic` theory...

- Dynamical System: 
    - First approach: Anything that changes with time...
    - A set of variables evolving in time...

- Time: two types...
    - Continuous - "flows"
    - Discrete  - "maps"
        - (maybe you're sampling a population every week, month
          etc)

- Like time, the dynamical variables can be discrete or
  continuous.

- He made a point about infinitely continuously varying
  independent variables `x`, `t` for a standing wave, and they
  would be governed by _partial differential equations_... but
  then some other type of system with discrete(?) independent
  variables would be governed by _differential equations_


- Quantum dynamics: we have equations (Schrodinger) that describe
  the evolution of _probability_ distributions...
