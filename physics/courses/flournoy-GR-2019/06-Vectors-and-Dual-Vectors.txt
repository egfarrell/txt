### Vectors and dual vectors


- How do spacetime vectors transform?
- I want a transformation rule for vectors


- The whole of SR comes from saying "I want to describe physics
  with true vectors". And creating a true vector such as
  4-momentum is hard and non-trivial because you have to get it
  to transform the correct way.

- And in GR, true vectors play an even deeper role because it
  turns out that trying to force the equations to be true vectors
  or tensors actually forces us into a lot of the non-trivial
  ways of working on curved spaces.


- The first vector we're going to examine is one created from
  _coordinate_ differentials. 
    - Because if we take a tiny displacement in spacetime we know
      it's described by a vector (it's got a magnitude and
      direction) 

- And the great thing about this vector is we know how it
  transforms because we know how the coordinates transform.
