### Stephen Earle Robbins PhD (Author) 
    - Stephen Robbins received his PhD in educational
      psychology/psychology from the University of Minnesota in
      1976, with a focus in computer simulation of thought. 
    - His doctoral thesis critiqued the computer model of mind
      then just emerging, describing a theory of consciousness
      based in Bergson's theory before the problem of
      consciousness came into vogue. 
    - It was in attempting to reconcile Bergson's concepts of
      time to relativity in this thesis that he was drawn into
      the problem of relativity's interpretation. He gravitated
      from academics into software development, working
      thirty-three years as an executive in bank data processing
      and banking software firms. With the emerging awareness of
      the weaknesses in the computer model of mind in the
      mid-1990's, particularly in the area of consciousness, he
      began publishing aspects of his theory - more than a dozen
      articles in academic journals along with several conference
      presentations. He and his wife, Susan, live on a small farm
      in Wisconsin.

### Books
    - The Mists of Special Relativity: Time, Consciousness and
      a Deep Illusion in Physics
      https://www.amazon.com/Mists-Special-Relativity-Consciousness-Illusion/dp/1492900427
    - This book is a must read for anyone interested in exploring
      a factual account of relativity. Robbins analyzes various
      components of relativity (simultaneity, reciprocal time
      dilation, Minkowski diagrams, effects from acceleration,
      etc.) and logically tests whether all of these working
      parts can possibly fit together in a self-consistent way.
    - The observations he makes and questions he poses are too
      important to ignore! 
    - He also explores relativity from a philosophical
      perspective and cites the works of Bergson, Chalmers and
      others while adding his own commentary. 
    - Another review: The mists are those of Robbins own
      misunderstandings of both SR and GR...
