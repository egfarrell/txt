### MST125 Grade
    - Final grade determined by lower of OCAS and OES score.
    - E.g. 90% OCAS, 60% OES, the grade set by the 60%.
    - You have to pass (i.e. get > 40% in both OES and OCAS).
      Explained p.23/24 of the Module Guide.
