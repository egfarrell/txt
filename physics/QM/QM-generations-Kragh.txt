### Discoveries
    - Observation is a NECESSARY, but not SUFFICIENT condition
      for a discovery...
    - It was only in 1938 that `superfluity` attained the status
      of discovery

### resistance disappears
    - What was the cause of the abrupt change in resistance?
    - Why was the phenomenon restricted to so few metals in the
      period table?
    - Theory could just not tell..

### Phase changes
    - Langevin suggested the discontinuous vanishing of
      resistance was perhaps the result of a phase change in the
      metal.
    - But Onnes lab had already tested that.

### explanation
    - in 1957 superconductivity was finally explained on
      a microscopic basis by `BCS`

### roots relativity
    - cf: $MY_NOTES/physics/EM/Fresnel.txt
    - Relativity had its roots in 19th century optics. Fresnel
      suggested in 1818 that a moving transparent body would
      partially drag along the ether.
    - (I was just reading about the Fresnel drag in a John Norton
      paper last night! - Sept 2016) Both Fresnel and Maxwells
      theories showed it was impossible to detect the earths
      movement through the ether to order v/c...

### Michelson-Morley
    - However Michelson-Morley was expected to show effects to
      the 2nd order (v/c)^2...
    - According to Loretnz's theory, effects should be observable
      to this precision

### Lorentz contractions
    - When Michelson-Morley came up null, Lorentz modified his
      theory and introduced his famous contraction which was
      assumed to be caused by changes in the molecular forces and
      in 1904 after further work on coordinate system, published
      his full "transformations"..
    - Although the same formulae had been published in 1887 by
      Voight and 1900 by Larmor!
