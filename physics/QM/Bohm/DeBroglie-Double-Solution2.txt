- De Broglie’s Double Solution Theory is a fascinating, but
  mathematically difficult, interpretation of QM.

- Is it the same as "Pilot Wave theory"?
    - No, but Pilot Wave theory is a simplified, stripped-down
      version of it.

- Is it different from Bohmian Mechanics?
    - Yes, it is fundamentally different in its philosophical
      view of reality (ontology), even though they share
      a historical lineage.

### 1. What is the "Double Solution Theory"?
    - Proposed by de Broglie in 1927, it attempted to maintain
      a purely physical, objective, and deterministic view of QM.

- "Double Solution" comes from de Broglie’s proposal that the
  quantum wave equation actually has two different solutions (two
  waves) that exist simultaneously and share the SAME PHASE:

1. The `u`-wave (or `v`-wave):
    - A highly localized wave that contains a moving singularity
      (a point of infinite density).

2. The `Ψ`-wave:
    - Continuous, smoothly spreading wave WITHOUT SINGULARITIES.
      This is the standard wave function used in Schrödinger's
      equation.

- In this theory, the particle isn't a separate object from the
  wave. Instead, the particle *is* the singularity—a tightly
  packed, localized concentration of energy—built directly into
  the fabric of the `u`-wave.
    - As the `u`-wave propagates through space, the singularity
      (the particle) is carried along with it.


### 2. What is Real vs. What is Abstract?
    - De Broglie was very explicit about separating the physical
      world from our mathematical tools.

    - What's REAL (Ontological):

        - The `u`-wave:
            - This is a physical, objective wave that propagates
              in standard, everyday 3D space.

        - The Particle:
            - This is a real, physical object, but it is defined
              mathematically as a singularity (or a non-linear
              highly concentrated peak) entirely incorporated
              WITHIN the `u`-wave.

    - What's ABSTRACT / MATHEMATICAL (Epistemological):

        - The `Ψ`-wave:
            - He viewed the standard Schrödinger wave function as
              a strictly mathematical, statistical tool.
            - Because we can't know the exact initial position of
              the singularity (the particle) within the `u`-wave,
              we use the `Ψ`-wave to calculate the *probability*
              of where the particle might be.

        - Furthermore, for multiple particles, the `Ψ`-wave
          exists in a multidimensional "configuration space".
        - For de Broglie, anything requiring such a configuration
          space can't be a real physical object; it can only be
          an abstract mathematical construction.

### 3. Is it the same as "Pilot Wave Theory"?

- No. The Double Solution theory is the "parent" of Pilot Wave
  theory.

- When de Broglie formulated the Double Solution, the mathematics
  were a nightmare. Describing a wave with a moving singularity
  requires non-linear mathematics, still relatively undeveloped
  in 1927.

- Because he couldn't get the math of the Double Solution to work
  perfectly, de Broglie presented a "watered-down" version at the
  1927 Solvay Conference.
    - He dropped the complex `u`-wave and the singularity.
      Instead, he simply postulated that the abstract `Ψ`-wave
      acts as a "pilot" that guides a separate, point-like
      particle along a trajectory.

### 4. Different from later "Bohmian Mechanics"?

- Yes, they're fundamentally different in their view of reality,
  even though they result in the same mathematical predictions
  for particle trajectories.

- In 1952, Bohm resurrected de Broglie's forgotten Pilot Wave
  theory and formalized it into "Bohmian Mechanics" (or DeBB
  theory). However, Bohm made different philosophical choices
  from de Broglie.

- Crucial differences between de Broglie and Bohm:

- The Nature of the Particle:
    - Double Solution:
        - The particle is a singularity made out of the wave
          itself. It is an artifact of the physical `u`-wave.
    - Bohmian:
        - The particle and the wave are two entirely separate,
          distinct entities. The particle is a literal
          point-mass, and the wave pushes it around.

- Reality of the `Ψ`-Wave:
    - Double Solution:
        - The `Ψ`-wave is only a statistical illusion
          / mathematical tool.
    - Bohmian:
        - The `Ψ`-wave is an actual, physically real field.
        - ??? Is that the standard view? Does modern Bohmians

- The "Space" they live in:
    - Double Solution:
        - De Broglie insisted that physical reality must happen
          in normal 3D space.
    - Bohmian:
        - Because Bohm made the `Ψ`-wave physically real, he had
          to accept that this real wave exists in
          `3N`-dimensional "configuration space".
        - De Broglie hated this idea, viewing a physical wave
          living in a mathematical space as absurd.

### Summary
    - De Broglie's Double Solution Theory was a grand attempt to
      explain QM using localized singularities living inside
      real, physical waves propagating in 3D space.
        - The physical wave and the particle are real, while the
          standard wave function (`Ψ`) is an abstract statistical
          tool.

    - Because the math of the Double Solution was too hard, he
      created the Pilot Wave theory as an approximation.

    - Years later, Bohm took that approximation and turned it
      into Bohmian mechanics, but in doing so, Bohm gave physical
      reality to the abstract `Ψ`-wave and placed it in multi-d
      configuration space - something de Broglie never agreed
      with.
