### Wrong guiding equation?
    - Former student in a Bohmian group here. 
    - There is an issue with this paper - there is no agreed-upon
      `guiding equation` for the photon. That is one of the open
      problems in Bohmian Mechanics. 
    - So I don't know what the authors of the paper used for
      photon velocity, but whatever it was it wasn't standard.
    - This does not disprove the core theory. In fact, since
      Bohmian mechanics hasn’t been fully extended to special
      relativity, it doesn’t model photons as particles at all,
      but rather as EM waves.

### Not disproven?
    - For anyone wondering, the conclusion that this
          "disproves Bohmian mechanics" is a big stretch. The
          original paper found a weird energy-speed relationship
          they said conflicts with Bohmian predictions. But
          a physicist named Nikolić immediately published
          a rebuttal showing they used the wrong formula. Bohmian
          mechanics has more general equations for complex
          systems like the one in the experiment. When you apply
          the correct math, the experimental results actually
          seem to fit. So, no, Bohmian mechanics isn't "debunked"
          by this paper. It's an ongoing scientific discussion,
          not a settled takedown.


### Reply paper: Nikolić
    - A follow-up paper by Hrvoje Nikolić: 
    - arXiv July 10, 2025: "Overcoming a challenge for Bohmian
      mechanics," argues the challenge can be addressed. 
    - He points out that Bohmian velocity is defined by the
      `continuity equation` not solely by the `phase gradient` of
      the wavefunction, as assumed in the Nature paper. 
    - By constructing an appropriate velocity model, he claims
      the experimental results can be reconciled with Bohmian
      mechanics, thus defending its validity.

### trajectories?
    - This experiment doesn’t disprove Bohmian mechanics, it
      highlights how different ontologies (particle
      `trajectories` vs. `probabilistic amplitudes`) interpret
      the same mathematical structure differently.
    - So long as Bohmian mechanics reproduces standard quantum
      predictions, discrepancies in interpretive metrics like
      “measured speed” do not constitute falsification.


### Fluid boundaries
    - When you look at Classical equivalents of Bohemian
      mechanics using fluid boundaries you see particles which
      appear when excitations of appropriate strength are applied
      to the field.  A particle pops out then rides the
      surrounding field while simultaneously creating it the
      field.  I am not exactly sure how this fully rules it out.
