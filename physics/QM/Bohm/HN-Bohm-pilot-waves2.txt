# Squeezed Discussion: Why Don't More Physicists Subscribe to Pilot Wave Theory?

### Top 10 Most Interesting Points

1.  The Interpretation Trilemma: 
    - Every QM interpretation must sacrifice one of four
      intuitive concepts: locality, realism, definiteness, or
      determinism. Pilot Wave sacrifices locality; Many Worlds
      sacrifices definiteness; Copenhagen sacrifices determinism.

2.  Parsimony vs. Scale: 
    - Many Worlds is argued to be more "parsimonious" (simpler
      rules) because it takes the math of the wave function at
      face value without adding a "collapse" mechanism, even
      though it results in an infinite number of universes.

3.  The "Go vs. Chess" Analogy: 
    - Simple rules (Go/Many Worlds) can generate infinite
      complexity, whereas complex rules (Chess/Copenhagen)
      restrict outcomes but are harder to define fundamentally.

4.  Quantum Speedup as an Illusion: 
    - In Pilot Wave theory, quantum computers aren't "faster" per
      se; rather, because the universe is deterministic, the
      entire history of the universe leading up to the
      calculation is part of the computation.

5.  The Measurement Problem: 
    - Most "standard" interpretations lack a rigorous definition
      of what constitutes a "measurement," leading to "mysticism"
      or "instrumentalism" (shut up and calculate).

6.  No-Communication Theorem: 
    - Despite the "spooky action" of entanglement, the laws of
      physics prevent sending actual information faster than the
      speed of light, preserving causality.

7.  Consciousness as Classical: 
    - The reason we don't perceive quantum superpositions may be
      that consciousness is a classical information-processing
      phenomenon (like a Turing machine) limited by the
      "no-cloning theorem."

8.  The Mathematical Stumbling Block: 
    - A primary reason Pilot Wave isn't more popular is the
      difficulty in extending it to a Lorentz-invariant Field
      Theory that practical physicists (like those at the LHC)
      can use for calculations.

9.  Non-Standard Analysis Analogy: 
    - Pilot Wave might be like Non-Standard Analysis in math:
      a different, perhaps clearer way to reach the same results,
      but not "standard" because it doesn't offer unique insights
      that current tools can't already achieve.

10. Configuration Space: 
    - In many-body QM, the "world" doesn't actually exist in 3D
      space, but in a high-dimensional configuration space; 3D
      reality is a projection or "classical illusion."
