### PETE box
    - $MY_NOTES/physics/QM/Hadamard-H-gate-PETE.txt
    - The PETE box is a Hadamard gate.
    - A Hadamard gate applied to a definite input (white = 0,
      black = 1) produces a state that, if measured, gives
      black/white with 50–50 probability — exactly what a single
      PETE box does.
    - Two Hadamards in a row give the identity transformation:
      - `H(H(|0>)) = |0>`
      - `H(H(|1>)) = |1>`.
    - So stacking two PETE boxes (Hadamards) gives a perfectly
      predictable output.
    - But if you measure in between, you collapse the
      intermediate state, and the second PETE box reverts to
      producing random outcomes — exactly what the tutorial
      describes when they shine a light between the boxes.
