### Gravitomagnetism NASA
    - Gravitomagnetism is produced by stars and planets when they
      _SPIN_. "It's similar in form to the magnetic field
      produced by a spinning ball of charge," explains Clifford
      Will. 
    - Replace charge with mass, and magnetism becomes
      gravitomagnetism.
    - 'Gravity Probe B' in Earth orbit.
    - We don't feel gravitomagnetism as we go about our everyday
      lives on Earth, but according to GR it is real.
        - When a planet (or star, or black hole, or anything
          massive) spins, it pulls space and time around with it:
          aka "frame dragging."
    - The fabric of spacetime twists like a vortex. Einstein
      tells us that all gravitational forces correspond to
      a bending of spacetime; the "twist" is gravitomagnetism.
    - What does gravitomagnetism do?
        - "It can make the orbits of satellites _precess_," says
          Will, "and it would cause a gyroscope placed in Earth
          orbit to wobble." Both effects are small and difficult
          to measure.
