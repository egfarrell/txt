### Syllabus: "PHIL202: Philosophy of Science"

- This course survyes philosophical issues surrounding modern
  science. Major areas covered:
    - Scientific reasoning
    - Scientific progress
    - Interpretations of scientific knowledge
    - The social organization of scientific practice.

- Its aim is to familiarize you with philosophical issues about
  science and to equip you critically to interpret popular
  reports about contemporary scientific research.

- Throughout this course, you'll become acquainted with some
  influential philosophers of science, including:
    - David Hume
    - Pierre Duhem
    - Carl Hempel
    - Karl Popper
    - Thomas Kuhn
    - Imre Lakatos,
    - Bas van Fraassen
    - Philip Kitcher
    - Helen Longino

- You'll read selections from scientific research too, via news
  articles and case studies, in order to connect philosophical
  views to actual scientific practice.

- You should approach this course neither in a naïve nor hostile
  attitude about science, but instead critically engaged in
  examining science as a human activity.
