Examination
The course is assessed with the grades A, B, C, D, E, Fx or F.
 
 
The grade A constitutes the highest grade on the scale and the remaining grades follow
 
in descending order where the grade E is the lowest grade on the scale that will result in
 
a pass. The grade F means that the student™s performance is assessed as fail (i.e.
 
received the grade F).
 
 
Assessment of student performance is made through written test and/or oral
 
examinations and/or presentation of mandatory assignments. Reexamination will be
 
offered within six weeks under the regular semester periods. The number of
 
examinations are limited to five times.
Course Evaluation
A course evaluation will be carried out at the end of the course in accordance with the
 
guidelines of the University. The result of the course evaluation will be filed at the
 
department.
