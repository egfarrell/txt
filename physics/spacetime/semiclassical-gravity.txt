- Hybrid framework: It treats spacetime geometry as classical (a
  smooth metric `g_ab` satisfying Einstein’s equations) while
  matter fields are quantum (described by QFT on that curved
  background).

- Modified Einstein equation: The classical stress-energy tensor
  T_ab is replaced by its quantum expectation value `<T_ab>`. So
  the field equation becomes `G_ab = 8πG * <T_ab>`.

- This means spacetime curvature responds to the average
  energy-momentum of quantum fields.

- Predictive reach: This approach explains effects like Hawking
  radiation and cosmological particle creation, where quantum
  fields on curved spacetime produce observable energy
  fluxes—without yet needing a full quantum theory of gravity.

- Limitations: It’s inconsistent when quantum fluctuations of
  T_ab are large (for instance, near singularities or in
  superpositions of different geometries). Spacetime can’t
  coherently respond to a single averaged source if the source
  itself is fundamentally probabilistic.
