- Coordinates that do not cover all of spacetime have caused
  a lot of problems in GR. When we study gravitational collapse
  and black holes we will see that the usual coordinates for the
  spacetime outside the black hole do not reach inside the black
  hole. For this reason, a particle falling into a black hole
  takes infinite coordinate time to go a finite distance.

- This is purely the fault of the coordinates: the particle falls
  in a finite proper time, into a region not covered by the
  ‘outside’ coordinates. A coordinate system that covers both
  inside and outside satisfactorily was not discovered until the
  mid-1950s.

  over from Euclidean geometry, that a vector is something whose
  _components_ transform as do the _coordinates_ under
  a coordinate transformation.
