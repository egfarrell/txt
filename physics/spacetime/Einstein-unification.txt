  with time, but also the electric field with the magnetic field,
  and energy with momentum."

- QUESTION: Could u unpack Wheeler's opinion?

- Wheeler is doing a kind of sweeping, poetic compression of
  physics history there. Let’s unpack the pieces with care:

### 1. Space and time
    - This one is clear: SR fused the 3 coordinates of space with
      the 1 coordinate of time into a single spacetime structure.
    - Minkowski: “Henceforth space by itself, and time by itself,
      are doomed to fade away into mere shadows.”
    - That’s the cleanest and most obvious "unification."

### 2. Electric field and magnetic field
    - Strictly speaking, Maxwell already tied electricity and
      magnetism together into electromagnetism.
    - But the real fusion happens in SR. In SR, what one observer
      calls a purely electric field, another moving observer may
      see as partly electric and partly magnetic.
    - In modern notation, we package the electric and magnetic
      fields into a single antisymmetric tensor (the
      electromagnetic field tensor `F_{mu nu}`).
    - So while Maxwell showed the two were connected, Einstein’s
      relativity revealed they’re not just cousins—they’re
      literally different aspects of the same geometric object,
      depending on your frame.

### 3. Energy and momentum
    - Classical mechanics treated momentum (`mv`) and energy
      (`1/2 mv^2`, or `potential` energy) as separate.
    - Relativity says they’re components of a single 4-vector:
      (`E/c, p_x, p_y, p_z`).
    - Conservation of energy and conservation of momentum are
      then not two separate principles but a single conservation
      of 4-momentum. That’s Einstein’s unification.

- So Wheeler is being slightly grandiloquent: he’s highlighting
  that Einstein’s relativity didn’t just give us curved
  spacetime, it also rewrote the logical scaffolding of physics
  in a more geometrical way.
    - Relativity tends to take pairs of things that were once
      distinct and show that they are shadows of one underlying
      entity:
    - space & time → spacetime
    - electric & magnetic → electromagnetism in SR
    - energy & momentum → 4-momentum
        - See: $MY_NOTES/physics/spacetime/4-momentum-timeline.txt

- EM: Maxwell gave us the theory where `E` and `B` talk to each
  other dynamically; Einstein gave us the transformation theory
  where they become the same thing in different inertial frames.
    - It’s like the difference between discovering two siblings
      live in the same house (Maxwell) and discovering they’re
      actually just two faces of a single Janus-headed being
      (Einstein).

- If you want to stretch Wheeler’s style even more, you can see
  this as part of a larger pattern in physics: Newton unified
  celestial and terrestrial mechanics, Maxwell unified
  electricity, magnetism, and light, Einstein unified space and
  time, then mass and energy (E=mc^2), then energy and momentum.
  Each generation finds a new symmetry or higher-dimensional
  geometric object hiding behind the everyday divisions.

- The interesting frontier is: what next? Unifying forces
  (electroweak theory, grand unification), or even information
  and physics (Wheeler’s own "It from bit").
