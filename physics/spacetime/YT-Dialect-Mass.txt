        - It is not correct to state that rest/invariant mass is
          independent of the state of motion of the observer.
        - What you rather mean to say is that, for any observer
          _co-moving_ with a body of mass, should they adopt the
          Einstein synchronization convention when setting up
          their coordinate system, then they will measure the
          "invariant" or "rest" mass for that body. 
        - This does not mean that only invariant mass is real
          however; rather that there is a coordinate system we
          can adopt which, just as with clock dilation or length
          contraction, masks the _additional relativistic mass_
          acquired by a body in a co-moving frame. 
        - Indeed all such invariant quantities in relativity are
          based on a simultaneity convention choice. We cover
          this topic more deeply in our video "Matrix Theory".
