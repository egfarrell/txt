# Preferred reference frame v preferred foliation
    - A *preferred reference frame* is not necessarily the same
      as a *preferred foliation*, although they're related
      concepts, and often arise in similar contexts in spacetime
      physics:

### Preferred Reference Frame
    - A *preferred reference frame* refers to a specific inertial
      or non-inertial frame of reference that is singled out as
      special by the laws of physics or the structure of
      spacetime.
    - In spacetime physics, this concept often arises in contexts
      where `Lorentz invariance` is violated or modified.
      - E.g: In theories like Hořava-Lifshitz gravity, there
        is a preferred global time function, and the rest frame
        associated with this time function becomes a preferred
        reference frame.
    - A preferred reference frame is typically defined locally
      (at a point or within a region) by specifying the velocity
      of an observer or fluid (e.g., the rest frame of a cosmic
      fluid in cosmology or the aether frame in historical
      theories).
    - *Key Feature*: A preferred reference frame can exist
      without necessarily invoking a foliation.
        - E.g., it might represent the rest frame of some
          physical entity (e.g., the cosmic microwave
          background).

### Preferred Foliation
    - A *preferred foliation* refers to a specific slicing of
      spacetime into spacelike hypersurfaces that are
      distinguished by some physical or geometrical property.
    - In many cases, a preferred foliation arises in spacetimes
      where there is a natural way to define a `global "time"`
      function ` t `.
        - The hypersurfaces of constant ` t ` then form the
          foliation.
    - The concept of a preferred foliation inherently applies to
      the entire spacetime (or a significant region of it), as it
      involves a GLOBAL STRUCTURE rather than a local frame at
      a point.
    - *Key Feature*: 
        - A preferred foliation can exist even if there is no
          explicit "preferred reference frame" defined at every
          point.
        - E.g., a spacetime might have a preferred foliation due
          to global symmetries, while the choice of reference
          frame _within each hypersurface_ can remain flexible.

### Relationship Between the Two
    1. Overlap:
        - In some theories, a preferred foliation and a preferred
          reference frame are closely related:
            - A preferred foliation often induces a natural
              notion of a preferred reference frame.
        - For example, if a spacetime has a preferred slicing
          defined by a global time function ` t `, the observers
          whose 4-velocity is ORTHOGONAL to the slices naturally
          define the preferred reference frame.
    - Conversely, if a preferred reference frame exists, it might
      imply a foliation into hypersurfaces orthogonal to the
      4-velocity of observers in the preferred frame.

    2. Distinction:
        - The two are not equivalent:
        - A preferred foliation is a global spacetime property
          and pertains to the geometry and slicing of spacetime.
        - A preferred reference frame is a local concept tied to
          an observer or physical system within the spacetime.

### Examples in Physics
    1. Cosmology:
    - The FLRW spacetime in cosmology has a *preferred foliation*
      of spacelike hypersurfaces of constant cosmological time
      (determined by the uniform matter distribution).
    - It also has a *preferred reference frame*: the rest frame
      of the cosmic fluid or the frame in which the cosmic
      microwave background is isotropic.

    2. Lorentz-Violating Theories:
    - In Hořava-Lifshitz gravity, the existence of a global time
      function implies both a preferred foliation and a preferred
      reference frame tied to the "aether-like" structure.

    3. GR:
    - General Relativity, due to its general covariance, admits
      neither a preferred foliation nor a preferred reference
      frame. Any foliation or reference frame is a matter of
      choice rather than physical necessity.


### Summary:
    - A *preferred reference frame* is about singling out
      a specific `observer` or velocity field.
    - A *preferred foliation* is about globally singling out
      a way to `slice spacetime` into hypersurfaces. They often
      coexist in related contexts, but they are conceptually
      distinct.
