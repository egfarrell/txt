What is the nature of __geometry__?

### A priori 
    Geometrical principles are neccessary truths independent
    of any possible experience.

### Empirical
    Geometrical principles encode practical prescriptions
    for measurement.

### Physical 
    Geometrical principles in themselves only concern
    abstract formal structures. They become descriptions of
    reality only when interpreted with the help of physical
    principles.

### Dynamical (Einstein)
    Geometry is not a fixed framework for physics, but
    a dynamical _field_ that interacts with other physical
    fields in nature.

### Mach (Die Mechanik, 1883)
    The motion of a body K can only be estimated by
    reference to other bodies... 

    When we reflect that we cannot abolish the other
    bodies..., and therefore cannot decide by experiment
    whether they play a fundamental or incidental part; 

    that hitherto they have been the sole sufficient means
    of the orientation of motions and of the description of
    mechanical facts; 

    it will be advisable to regards all motions
    provisionally as determined by these bodies.

inertia
    In general, things will behave merely in virtue of their
    inertia just as they would in a gravitational field.

    To the observer sealed in a box, there is nothing to
    distinguish the two situations.

    Gravity is indistinguishable from intertia.

    The gravitational field is "transformed away" in the
    accelerated coordinate system.

### local and global
    Local and global structure of general-relativistic
    space-time: the light-cone structure looks the same in
    the neighborhood of any point, but a local intertial
    frame cannot be extended into a global frame; 

    local inertial frames will be accelerated relative to
    each other, in which any other intertial frame is also
    inertial.

    (So local inertial coordinates cannot generally be
    _extended_ to global inertial coordinates.)

### geodesic principle
    Principle: the path of a freely-falling particle is
    a geodesic of space-time.

    Motivation: Arguments from the equivalence of gravity
    and inertia suggest that the path of a freely-falling
    particle is _locally indistinguishable_ from the path of
    a Newtonian particle "not subject to forces".

### Interpretive principles
    of space-time geometry:
    * The path of a light-ray is a null geodesic of space-time
    * The path of a freely-falling particle is a timelike
      geodesic of space-time
