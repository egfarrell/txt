- The motivation for writing this came from reading 
    - "Icons and Symmetries" by Altmann.

- He showed how using a representation with the wrong
  symmetry seriously impeded the discovery of the laws of
  magnetism.

- I realised that differential forms carried his program even
  further.  

- The EM theory given here has no _right-hand_ rules at all.

- After all, EM theory is a right-left symmetric theory

- A proper regard for the symmetry of objects leads to
  a stratification of the vrious 3-degree geometric objects into:
    - vectors, 
    - 1-forms, 
    - 2-forms, 
    - twised 1-forms 
    - twisted 2-forms

- Relations between objects in different strata require operators
  with additional geometric structre.

- I argue that mechanics is unneccessarily difficult because he
  basic symmetries aren't properly represented.
    - EG! This appears in the unintuitive distinction between
      centrifugal and centripetal force.
