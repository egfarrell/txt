### Neighbourhoods, not points
    - In the 2nd chapter I argue that while one cannot give the
      state of the matter and fields of a classical world
      '`spacetime point`' by 'spacetime point', one can give it
      `neighbourhood` by neighbourhood, where these
      neighbourhoods can be as small as one likes. 

    - I also argue that one can give the geometric structure of
      spacetime neighbourhood by neighbourhood
