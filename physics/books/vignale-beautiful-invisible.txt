### Tangent bundle(!)
    - The abstractness of velocity becomes clear when we consider
      motion restricted to a line (or surface in 3D space)
    - Eg, here's a bead, bound to move along a curved wire:
        - $MY_IMAGES/physics/vignale-tangent-bundle-01.jpg
            - At each point, the velocity of the bead can have
              any magnitude, but its direction must always line
              up with the tangent to the wire, pointing either
              ‘forwards’ or ‘backwards’.
            - Notice each point has its own private tangent,
              completely different from a neighbours tangent, no
              matter how near the neighbour is.
    - The jagged-looking ensemble of all tangents constitutes
      a _tangent bundle_ grafted on top of the wire:
        - $MY_IMAGES/physics/vignale-tangent-bundle-02.jpg
            - So the bead "lives" on the wire, but its velocity
              lives in a completely different and abstract space:
              the TANGENT BUNDLE to the wire.

### Stationary Maximum
     - The concentration of reflected light about the maximum
       angle (42 deg) is a consequence of a very important
       mathematical principle for understanding nature.
     - It's the 'principle of the STATIONARY MAXIMUM' even though
       the same is true for the minimum
     - The principle says the _rate of variation_ of any variable
       quantity vanishes when the quantity approaches its maximum
       or minimum value.
        - (Brilliant! That's why `δV = 0`. I never underestood
          that from a 'physical' point of view)
        - Although this principle is slightly different to "least
          action" which is more about nature always choosing
          a stationary quantity for her processes. This principle
          is kind of a fact about quantities that vary.
     - Thinking about it: When you throw a ball vertically up, it
       stops of an instant at the point of maximum height, before
       descending.
     - For just a fleeting moment, it is stationary. The rate of
       variation of its height vanishes. (!!!)
     - When the sun in its yearly revolution reaches the
       northermost limit of its path in the sky (on 21 June) it
       lingers there, nearly stationary, for a few days: this is
       the origin of "soltice, meaning "solar arrest"
