### PHYSICAL theories depend on these MATH theories

    - CLASSICAL MECHANICS 
        - Differential Geometry
        - Sympletic Geometry

    - SPECIAL RELATIVITY 
        - Linear Algebra
        - Differential Calculus

    - GENERAL RELATIVITY 
        - Differential Geometry

    - QM (NON-RELATIVISTIC) 
        - Linear Algebra:
            - sufficient to grasp the measurement problem,
              quantum nonlocality, and the simplest "no hidden
              variables" proofs.
        - Functional Analysis:
            - needed to deal with realistic systems 
            - (e.g. particles with position degree of freedom)

    - QFT (mainstream) 
        - QFT ISNT a theory yet, if a theory must be formulated
          in known mathematics!
        - But practitioners of the "art" of QFT use tools like:
            - group representation theory,
            - ordinary and partial differential equations
            - calculus of variations.

    - QFT (algebraic) 
        - Operator Algebras,
        - and for certain topics (e.g. superselection theory)
          Group Representation Theory and Category Theory.
        - QFT on curved spacetime also requires Differential
          Geometry.

### Dependencies of MATH theories on other math theories

    - DIFFERENTIAL GEOMETRY
        - Multivariable Calculus, and preferably Real Analysis
        - Point-Set Topology is very helpful (since topological
          and metric concepts are central in Differential
          Geometry)

    - OPERATOR ALGEBRAS 
        - Functional Analysis
        - Abstract Algebra
        - Measure Theory
        - Complex Analysis 
            - passing acquaintance...
            - for some topics (e.g. modular theory) you need
              a mastery of Complex Analysis

    - FUNCTIONAL ANALYSIS
        - Real Analysis
        - Linear Algebra
        - and Point-Set Topology.
        - Complex Analysis (passing acquaintance)

    - CATEGORY THEORY
        - The beauty & utility of category theory only becomes
          apparent against the backdrop of other fields:
        - Abstract Algebra
        - Algebraic Topology
        - Algebraic Geometry

    - Topos theory depends on category theory.


### Books by field

### GENERAL MATHS
    - Geroch: Mathematical physics.
        - $MY_BOOKS/Physics/Maths/Math-Phy-General/Geroch-mathematical-physics.pdf
        - The title is misleading. It might be called: 
            - "The minimum abstract mathematical prerequisites
              needed for contemporary mathematical physics."
        - It surveys several branches of abstract mathematics
          including Vector Spaces, Groups, Manifolds, Topological
          Spaces, etc..

    - Schechter: Handbook of analysis and its foundations.
        - Don't read from front to back. Use as a reference.
        - This book contains materials on mathematical structures
          not included in Geroch, such as topological groups,
          vector measures, convex sets, and set theory.
