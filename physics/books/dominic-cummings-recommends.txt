### Dominic_Cummings_Recommends.txt

    - Six Easy Pieces, Feynman. A classic based on his famous
      lectures. A short companion book to his famous lecture
      series.

    - Tips on Physics: a problem-solving supplement, which was
      unpublished for many years. Useful for someone doing
      A Levels or beginning an undergraduate course.

    - Physics for Future Presidents, Richard Muller (2010).
      Brilliant physics book for the interested nonspecialist
      written by a top physicist, widely praised by Nobel Prize
      winners, used in his famous Berkeley course.

    - Theoretical Minimum, Leonard Susskind (2013). Based on
      a course Susskind taught in San Francisco to give people
      a basic physics education (maybe wait until the typos are
      fixed).
        - $MY_BOOKS/Physics/Undergrad/Susskind-01-Classical-Mechanics.pdf

    - Fab: From Personal Computers to Personal Fabrication,
      Gershenfeld (MIT). On digital fabrication.

    - The Great Equations, Crease.

    - A Cavendish Quantum Mechanics Primer, Mark Warner.
      https://www.cavendish-quantum.org.uk/ This excellent short
      book introduces quantum mechanics using A Level maths.

    - The Second Creation, Crease & Mann (1996)

    - The Infinity Puzzle, Frank Close (2011).

    - A great biography of Einstein by Isaacson.

    - A great biography of Dirac, The Strangest Man, Farmelo.

    - The Dance of the Photons, Zeilinger. On the connections
      between quantum physics, computation and information
      theory.

    - The Limits of Quantum Computers, Scott Aaronson (Scientific
      American, 2008).

    - Quantum Computing Since Democritus, Scott Aaronson (2013)
      is a brilliant introduction to many ideas about
      computation, physics and the frontiers of quantum
      information science.

    - Michael Nielsen on quantum mechanics and computers: Why the
      world needs quantum mechanics… Quantum Computing for
      Everyone… Quantum Computing for the Determined.

    - Woit’s blog… Dorigo’s blog. Great for keeping up with
      developments in physics.


### More recommendations

- _The Blank Slate_ by Steven Pinker

- _Behavioral Genetics_ by Robert Plomin - A great standard
  textbook.

- _What is Thought?_ by Eric Baum

- _Consciousness_ by Christof Koch

- _Thinking, Fast and Slow_ by Daniel Kahneman - Noted for
  cognitive biases and foreign policy.

- _Expert Political Judgment_ by Philip E. Tetlock

- _Sources of Power_ by Gary Klein - On expert decisions under
  pressure.

- _Biology is Technology_ by Robert Carlson - On biological
  engineering and synthetic biology.

- _The Language of Life_ by Francis Collins - On the future of
  the genetics revolution.

- _Complexity Economics_ by Eric Beinhocker

- _The Misbehaviour of Financial Markets_ by Benoit Mandelbrot

- _Rationality in Economics_ by Vernon L. Smith

- _An Engine, Not a Camera_ by Donald MacKenzie - On financial
  models; an essay.

- _A paper by Andrew Lo_ - Describes differences between the
  treatment of uncertainty in physics and economics.

- _A memoir by Charles Ferguson_ - Gives a glimpse of how
  a Silicon Valley start-up really happens.

- _The Quark and the Jaguar_ by Murray Gell-Mann

- _Consilience_ by E.O. Wilson

- _Linked_ by Albert-László Barabási

- _Complexity: A Guided Tour_ by Melanie Mitchell (2009) - Covers
  inter-disciplinary subjects from computer science to the immune
  system.

- _The Signal and the Noise_ by Nate Silver (2012) - An excellent
  introduction to ideas about modelling and prediction.

- _The History of the Peloponnesian War_ by Thucydides

- _The Art of War_ by Sun Tzu

- _On War_ by Carl von Clausewitz

- _The Transformation of War_ by Martin van Creveld

- _Introduction to Strategic History_ by Colin S. Gray

- _Brave New War_ by John Robb - On how civilian technological
  trends are changing modern war.

- _A paper by a senior British civil servant_ - On nuclear
  strategy.

- _The Battle of Algiers_ by Gillo Pontecorvo (movie) - Essential
  viewing on terrorism and counter-terrorism.

- _Bismarck: A Life_ by Jonathan Steinberg - Pflanze’s Biography
  (for understanding political decision-making).

- _Just in Time_ by John Hoskyns - On the difficulties of
  strategic thinking in the modern British state.

- _The Audacity to Win_ by David Plouffe - From inside
  a successful modern political campaign.

- _The Victory Lab_ by Sasha Issenberg (2012) - On how science
  and technology has invaded political campaigns.

- _Influence: The Psychology of Persuasion_ by Robert Cialdini
  - Interesting on the psychology of selling and marketing.

- _Meet the People_ by James Frayne (forthcoming) - On modern
  communications.

- _Good Strategy, Bad Strategy_ by Richard Rumelt (2012) - Almost
  the only good business book on strategy and decisions.

- _The Checklist Manifesto_ by Atul Gawande - Interesting on how
  some fields have significantly improved performance while
  others have not, and the barriers to improvement.

- _The Visual Display of Quantitative Information_ by Edward
  Tufte - A classic about the presentation of information.

- _Beautiful Evidence_ by Edward Tufte - Another classic about
  the presentation of information.

### historical analysis:

    - _A Farewell to Alms_ by Gregory Clark (2008) - Discusses
      selective pressures in pre-industrial England and their
      impact on modern society.
