### foliation mystery cleared-up after 15 years
    - No, spacetime having a *fixed foliation* is not necessarily
      the same as having a *preferred foliation*, though the
      terms are related.

    ### 1. Fixed Foliation
    - A fixed foliation refers to a specific way of slicing
      spacetime into a sequence of spacelike hypersurfaces (often
      called "time slices") that is established and unchanging in
      a given context.
    - In a spacetime with a fixed foliation, the slicing is
      a mathematical or physical choice used for convenience,
      analysis, or solving equations. 
        - However, the foliation itself does not imply a deeper,
          _fundamental property_ of spacetime.
    - Example: In numerical relativity, one often uses a fixed
      foliation to solve Einstein's field equations by evolving
      quantities across a sequence of hypersurfaces.

    ### 2. Preferred Foliation
    - A preferred foliation implies that spacetime fundamentally
      "prefers" a specific slicing due to some physical or
      geometrical property.
        - This typically occurs when there is a physical reason
          to single out a specific family of spacelike
          hypersurfaces.
    - A preferred foliation is often associated with theories
      beyond General Relativity (GR), such as:
        - Lorentz-violating theories (e.g., Hořava-Lifshitz
          gravity), where a preferred foliation is tied to
          a global time or a privileged rest frame.
        - Cosmology, where the presence of a uniform matter
          distribution (e.g., in FLRW spacetimes) can lead to
          a natural foliation based on surfaces of constant
          cosmological time.
    - In GR itself, there is no *preferred foliation* since the
      theory respects general covariance (diffeomorphism
      invariance), meaning all foliations are equally valid.

    ### Key Difference
        - The main difference is that a `fixed` foliation is
          a *choice* used for practical or technical purposes,
          whereas a `preferred` foliation implies an inherent
          structure in spacetime that distinguishes one slicing
          as physically or geometrically special.
