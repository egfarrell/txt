# key:
    - TYPES of dofs
        - positional
        - momentum
        - rotational
        - vibrational
    - NUMBER of dofs: `3N` per atom


### TYPES of dof
    - molecules can have four types of degrees of freedom (dof's):

    - POSITIONAL dof:
        - 3 dof: x, y, z coordinates of the center of mass.

    - MOMENTUM dof (translational):
        - 3 dof: momentum in x, y, z directions.

    - ROTATIONAL dof:
        - `2` for linear (diatomic) molecules (no rotation around bond axis).
        - `3` for non-linear (polyatomic) molecules.

    - VIBRATIONAL dof –
        - `3N - 5` for linear molecules.
        - `3N - 6` for non-linear molecules (N = number of atoms).
        - Note: Each vibrational mode contributes `2 dof` (`potential` + `kinetic` energy parts).

- So, molecules can have these 4 types of dof’s. The total NUMBER
  of dof depends on the exact molecular structure.

### Total number of dof's
    - `3N` total degrees of freedom:
        - Any molecule with `N` atoms has `3N` total dof:
        - Each atom can move independently in 3D space → x, y, z.

### Focus on vibrational dof's
    - Firstly `3N` total degrees of freedom:
    - Now subtract the non-vibrational dof's:
        1. Translational dof’s: `3`
            - Entire molecule can move as a whole in x, y, z
            - → `3 dof.`

        2. Rotational dof’s:
            - Linear molecules (e.g. O₂, CO₂):
                - only rotate about 2 axes
                - (not around the bond axis, as it has negligible
                  moment of inertia).
                - → `2 rotational dof`

            - Non-linear molecules (e.g. H₂O, NH₃):
                - rotate about all 3 axes
                - → `3 rotational dof`

    - So, vibrational dof’s = what's left:
        - Linear:
            - Vibrational dof = 3N - 3 (translational) - 2 (rotational)
            - = `3N - 5`

        - Non-linear:
            - Vibrational dof = 3N - 3 - 3
            - = `3N - 6`

# Another explanation

* Degrees of Freedom (DOF): 
    - Independent ways a molecule can STORE ENERGY.

* Total DOF: 
    - For a molecule with N atoms, each atom has 3 spatial
      coordinates, so `3N` total degrees of freedom. 
    - These `3N` dof's are _distributed_ among translational,
      rotational, and vibrational motions.

* Translational DOF:
    * Movement of the entire molecule through space (x, y, z directions).
    * Always `3 dof`.

* Rotational DOF:
    * Rotation of the molecule around its center of mass.
    * Linear molecules (e.g., `O_2`, `CO_2`): 
        - Rotate around two axes perpendicular to the molecular
          axis. Rotation around the bond axis doesn't change the
          molecule's orientation, so it doesn't count as
          a rotational DOF.
        * `2 dof`
    * Non-linear molecules (e.g., `H_2O`, `CH_4`): 
        - Can rotate around three independent axes.
        * `3 dof`

* Vibrational DOF:
    * Internal motions where atoms within the molecule move
      relative to each other (stretching, bending bonds).
    * Calculated by subtracting translational and rotational
      dof's from the total `3N` dof's.

    * For linear molecules:
        * Total DOF: `3N`
        * Translational DOF: 3
        * Rotational DOF: 2
        * Vibrational DOF `= 3N - Translational - Rotational 
                           = 3N - 3 - 2 
                           = 3N - 5`

    * For non-linear molecules:
        * Total DOF: `3N`
        * Translational DOF: 3
        * Rotational DOF: 3
        * Vibrational DOF `= 3N - Translational - Rotational 
                           = 3N - 3 - 3 
                           = 3N - 6`

* Each vibrational mode contributes `2 dof`:
    * Unlike translational and rotational dof's (which are purely
      kinetic energy), vibrational modes involve both `kinetic`
      energy (atoms in motion) and `potential` energy (stored in
      the stretched/compressed bonds).

    * Therefore, each unique way a molecule can vibrate
      contributes two degrees of freedom for energy storage.

Answer:
    - Your calculation for a diatomic molecule is correct:
        - N = 2 (e.g., O2, HCl)
        - Total DOF: 3N=3×2=6
        - Translational DOF: 3
        - Rotational DOF: 2 (diatomic molecules are linear)
        - Vibrational DOF: 3N−3−2=6−3−2=`1`

    - Regarding your clarification:
        - Yes, that one vibrational "mode" (or "degree of
          freedom" in the `3N−X` formula sense) indeed has two
          components: 
            - Kinetic Energy (KE) and Potential Energy (PE).
        - It's often clearer to say "1 vibrational mode" rather
          than "1 vibrational dof" when referring to the outcome
          of the 3N−X formula, and then acknowledge that each
          mode contributes 2 actual degrees of freedom for energy
          storage (one for KE, one for PE).

- So, for a diatomic molecule:
        - You calculate 1 vibrational mode.
        - This single vibrational mode accounts for 2 actual
          degrees of freedom for energy partitioning
          (equipartition theorem context), one for the kinetic
          energy of the vibrating atoms and one for the potential
          energy stored in the bond.

### Question:
    - cheers... the use of the word "mode" has made it clearer...
      (for vibrational modes/dofs)
    - But does this distinction between "mode" and actual "dof"
      apply also to translational, positional, rotational dofs? 


### Answer
    - This question highlights a subtle but important distinction
      in the terminology!
    - The distinction between "mode" and "actual DOF" is
      primarily relevant and most prominent for vibrational
      motion.

    - Here's why:

    - Translational DOF:
        - For translational motion, "mode" and "DOF" are
          essentially interchangeable.
        - Moving in the x-direction is one translational mode,
          and it contributes 1 DOF (specifically, `1 kinetic
          energy term`, 1/2mvx2).
        - There are 3 such independent directions (x, y, z), so
          3 translational modes = 3 translational DOF. 
        - There's no separate potential energy associated with
          pure translation (in free space).

    - Rotational DOF:
        - Similar to translational, for rotational motion, "mode"
          and "DOF" are also largely synonymous.
        - Rotating around a specific axis (e.g., Rx, Ry, Rz) is
          a rotational mode, and it contributes 1 DOF (`1 kinetic
          energy term`, 1/2Iω2).
        - For linear molecules, there are 2 rotational modes
          = 2 rotational DOF.
        - For non-linear molecules, there are 3 rotational modes
          = 3 rotational DOF. Again, no separate potential energy
          is stored in pure rotation.

    - Vibrational DOF:
        - This is where the distinction becomes crucial.
          A "vibrational mode" refers to a specific, independent
          pattern of atomic motion within the molecule (e.g.,
          a symmetric stretch, an asymmetric stretch, a bending
          motion).
        - Each of these distinct vibrational "modes" stores
          energy in two ways:
            - Kinetic energy from the atoms moving.
            - Potential energy from the bonds being stretched or
              compressed (like a spring).
        - Therefore, each vibrational mode contributes 2 degrees
          of freedom to the total energy of the molecule
          according to the equipartition theorem (at high enough
          temperatures where these modes are fully "active").

    - In summary:
        - For translational and rotational motion, a "mode"
          directly corresponds to a single degree of freedom
          (which is associated with a `kinetic energy term`).
        - For vibrational motion, a "mode" refers to a distinct
          pattern of vibration, and each such mode contributes
          two degrees of freedom (one for kinetic energy and one
          for potential energy) to the overall energy balance of
          the molecule.

This is why the formulas for vibrational DOF (3N−5 or 3N−6) count the number of vibrational modes, and then for energy considerations, each of those modes is understood to contribute 2 DOF.
