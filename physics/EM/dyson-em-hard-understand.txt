# Freeyman Dyson: 
### Why Maxwell's Theory hard to understand?

    - Maxwell only briefly mentioned his own EM theory:(!)
        - "Another theory of electricity, which I prefer, denies
          action at a distance and attributes electric action to
          `tensions` and `pressures` in an _all-pervading
          medium_, these stresses being the same kind as those
          familiar to engineers, and the medium being identical
          with that in which light is supposed to be propagated."

    - Maxwell's theory becomes simple and intelligible only when
      you give up thinking of mechanical models. 

    - Instead of mechanical objects being primary and `EM
      stresses` being secondary consequences, you must think of
      the EM field as primary and mechanical forces as secondary.

### Two Layers
    - The modern view of the world that emerged from Maxwell's
      theory is a world with _two layers_.

    - Top Layer: 'Touch and measure'
        - This is the layer of things we can directly touch and
          measure, consisting of mechanical stresses and energies
          and forces.

    - Bottom Layer: 'Fields'
        - This layer is the fundamental constituents of the
          world, consists of `FIELDS` satisfying simple `linear`
          equations.

    - The two layers are connected, because the quantities in the
      top layer are quadratic or bilinear combinations of the
      (fundamenta?) quantities in the bottom layer.

    - To calculate energies or stresses, you take the square of
      the strength of the electric field or multiply one
      component of the `field` by another.

    - The two-layer structure of the world is the main reason why
      Maxwell's theory seemed mysterious and difficult. 
        - The objects down in the bottom layer, the objects that
          are truly fundamental, are abstractions not directly
          accessible to our senses.

    - The objects that we can feel and touch are on the top
      layer, and their behaviour is only determined indirectly by
      the equations that operate on the bottom layer. 
        - The two-layer structure of the world implies that the
          basic _processes of nature are hidden from our view_.
        - (cf d'Espagnat?)

    - We now take it for granted that electric and magnetic
      fields are abstractions not reducible to mechanical models.
      To see that this is true, we need only look at the UNITS
      the electric and magnetic fields are measured in.
        - The conventional unit of electric field-strength is:
          `sqrt(joule per cubic meter)` 
            - A joule is a unit of energy and a meter is a unit
              of length, but a square-root of a joule is not
              a unit of anything tangible.
        - There is no way we can imagine directly measuring the
          square-root of a joule. 
            - The unit of `electric field-strength` is
              a mathematical abstraction, chosen so the square of
              a field-strength is equal to an energy-density that
              can be measured with real instruments.
        - The unit of energy density is a `joule per cubic
          meter`, and therefore we say that the unit of
          field-strength is the square-root of a joule per cubic
          meter.
        - This doesn't mean that an electric field-strength can
          be measured with the square-root of a calorimeter. 
        - Instead it means an electric field-strength is an
          abstract quantity, incommensurable with any quantities
          we can measure directly.

### Similarities with QM
    - It may be helpful for understanding QM to stress the
      similarities between it and Maxwell's theory. 
    - In two ways, Maxwell's theory may provide a key to the
      mysteries of QM.

    - Way 1:
        - First, the attempts to understand QM in terms of
          a language based on _classical_ concepts are similar to
          the attempts to understand the Maxwell theory in terms
          of _mechanical_ models. 
            - The Maxwell theory became elegant and intelligible
              only after attempts to represent EM fields by
              mechanical models were abandoned.
        - Similarly, QM becomes elegant and intelligible only
          after attempts to describe it in words are abandoned.
            - To see the beauty of the Maxwell theory, it's
              necessary to move away from mechanical models and
              into the abstract world of fields.
            - To see the beauty of QM it is necessary to move
              away from verbal descriptions and into the abstract
              world of geometry(?) <--- I thought abstract
              algebra. 
        - Mathematics is the language that nature speaks. The
          language of mathematics makes the world of Maxwell
          fields and the world of quantum processes equally
          transparent.

    - Way 2:
        - The 2nd connection between Maxwell theory and QM is
          a deep similarity of structure. 
            - Like Maxwell's theory, QM divides the universe into
              two layers. 
        - The 'bottom' layer contains the wave-functions of
          Schrodinger, the matrices of Heisenberg and the
          state-vectors of Dirac.
            - Quantities in the first layer obey simple linear
              equations. Their behaviour can be accurately
              calculated. But they cannot be directly observed.
        - The 'top' layer contains probabilities of particle
          collisions and transmutations, intensities and
          polarisations of radiation, expectation-values of
          particle energies and spins. 
            - Quantities in the second layer can be directly
              observed but cannot be directly calculated. They do
              not obey simple equations. They are either squares
              of first-layer quantities or products of one
              first-layer quantity by another.

    - In QM just as in Maxwell theory, Nature lives in the
      abstract mathematical world of the bottom layer, but we
      humans live in the concrete mechanical world of the top
      layer.
    - We can describe Nature only in abstract mathematical
      language, because our verbal language is at home only in
      the top layer.
    - Just as in the case of the Maxwell theory, the abstract
      quality of the bottom-layer quantities is revealed in the
      units in which they are expressed. 
        - For example, the Schrodinger wave-function is expressed
          in a unit which is the square root of an inverse cubic
          meter.
        - This alone makes clear that the wave-function is an
          abstraction, forever hidden from our view. 
        - Nobody will ever measure directly the square root of
          a cubic meter.

    - The ultimate importance of the Maxwell theory is far
      greater than unifying electricity and magnetism. Its
      ultimate importance was as the prototype for the great
      triumphs of 20th century physics.

    - It's the prototype for Einstein's Relativity, for QM, for
      the Yang-Mills theory of generalised gauge invariance, and
      for the unified theory of fields and particles known as the
      Standard Model.

    - All these are based on the concept of dynamical fields,
      introduced by Maxwell in 1865. 
        - All have the same _two-layer structure_, separating the
          world of simple dynamical equations from the world of
          human observation.

    - All of them embody the same quality of mathematical
      abstraction that made Maxwell's theory difficult for his
      contemporaries to grasp. 
        - We may hope that a _deep understanding of Maxwell's
          theory_ will disperse the fog of misunderstanding still
          surrounding the interpretation of QM.

    - And we may hope that a deep understanding of MAXWELL'S
      THEORY will help to lead the way toward further triumphs of
      physics in the 21st century.
