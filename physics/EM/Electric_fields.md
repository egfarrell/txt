- Zen: the sound of one hand clapping. Thats the electric field.
  The electric field here _now_ at this point depends on what
  charges were doing in the _past_.

- If there's a large distance between the charges, where the time
  of travel of light is not negligble, then Coloumbs Law IS NOT
  appropriate. Coloumb is better for `electrostatics` where the
  location of the charges isnt changing.

- Now the concept of a field overcomes some of the limitations of
  Coloumb. Its easy to calculate the response of a test charge to
  the field. However it can be very complicated to calculate the
  field ITSELF. ie. you've to take into account all movements of
  charges in the past etc.

- When drawing field lines: the area density of lines reflects
  the strength of the field. ie `# lines / surface area`. A nice
  choice is to draw `1/e0` lines for `1 coloumb` of charge. Then
  the denisty will give the electric field. Think about it!
