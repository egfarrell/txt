
### Teaching
    - “One story illustrates Chandrasekhar's devotion to his
      science and his students. In the 1940s, while based at the
      University's Yerkes Observatory in Williams Bay, he drove
      more than 100 miles round-trip each week to teach a class
      of just two registered students. 
    - Any concern about the cost-effectiveness of such
      a commitment was erased in 1957, when the entire class --
      T.D. Lee and C.N. Yang -- won the Nobel Prize in physics.”
    - Source: https://chronicle.uchicago.edu/951012/chandra.shtml
