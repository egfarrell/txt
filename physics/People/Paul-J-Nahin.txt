    - 1999: Time Machines
        - Blend of physics, philosophy, and sci-fi, tracing time
          travel from early literature to contemporary theories.

    - 2001: The Science of Radio
        - Detailed examination of AM radio technology, using
          MATLAB + electronics demos to explain the fundamentals
          of radio engineering.

    - 2002: Oliver Heaviside
        - Self-taught Victorian electrical engineer who
          revolutionized electrical theory and laid the
          groundwork for modern telecomms.

    - 2002: Duelling Idiots
        - Probability puzzles for readers to think critically
          about chance, randomness, and mathematical reasoning.

    - 2007: When Least Is Best
        - Optimization probs: shows how mathematicians have found
          elegant solutions to real-world challenges through the
          `principle of least action` (!)

    - 2009: Mrs. Perkins’s Electric Quilt
        - Exploration of maths through quilting; showing how
          mathematical concepts can be applied to art and design.

    - 2010: An Imaginary Tale
        - Historical journey through the development of the
          imaginary unit `i`, uncovering its significance...

    - 2011: Dr. Euler’s Fabulous Formula
        - Examination of Euler's identity, exploring its profound
          implications and connections to various fields.

    - 2011: Time Travel
        - Comprehensive look at the scientific and philosophical
          aspects of time travel, discussing its feasibility and
          paradoxes.

    - 2011: Number-Crunching
        - `Numerical methods` and their applications, focusing on
          techniques to solve complex problems.

    - 2012: Chases and Escapes
        - Analysis of pursuit and evasion problems, applying
          concepts to scenarios involving chases, escapes, and
          strategic movement.

    - 2012: The Logician and the Engineer
        - Interplay between logic and engineering, examining how
          logical principles underpin technological advances.

    - 2013: Digital Dice
        - Randomness and probability in the digital age,
          showing how computers simulate chance and the
          implications for various applications.

    - 2013: Will You Be Alive Ten Years From Now?
        - Examination of mortality and probability; the factors
          that influence life expectancy and survival.

    - 2014 Holy Sci-Fi!
        - Intersection of sci-fi and science fact; how fiction
          has influenced scientific thought and discovery.
