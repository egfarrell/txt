### Physical Relativity
    - Physical Relativity (2005) is a subtle, revisionist work.
      He doesn’t push a literal ether; instead he argues that
      Lorentz invariance is dynamically grounded—that the
      geometry of spacetime reflects the symmetry of the
      dynamical laws governing matter and fields, not an
      independent geometric structure laid down beforehand.

    - That’s the key distinction:
        - In Einstein’s orthodox interpretation, spacetime
          geometry has explanatory primacy; it dictates how rods,
          clocks, and particles behave.
        - In Brown’s “dynamical” view, the laws governing rods,
          clocks, and fields explain why spacetime has the
          geometry it does.
