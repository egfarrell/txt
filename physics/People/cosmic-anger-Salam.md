### God
    - Now, Salam was drawn towards research in Theoretical
      Physics. He wanted to emulate Dirac, to carve out
      mathematical monuments to _Allah's work_.

### Mathematics
    - For Salam, Dirac was the greatest scientist of the 20th
      century.
    - When I asked him if this included Einstein, he was clear:
        - 'Einstein had his mathematics all done for him. Dirac
          invented his.
    - Not only that, it was Dirac who first made it clear the
      route towards real understanding lies through _abstract
      mathematics_, not through Engineering Mathematics.

### electrons
    - Electrons are constituents of our world, _cogwheels_ in an
      atomic mechanism that are invisible but make our world
      recognisable - sunlight, colour and warmth.
    - All that is familiar and comfortable is due to electrons.
      The _motive power_ for these _electronic cogwheels_ is
      provided by the electric and magnetic effects that Maxwell
      had wrought together...

### Cogs
    - When a cog in a mechanical motor turns, it emits a smooth
      hum when the motor is working well, _increasing in
      frequency as the motor turns faster_.
    - Electrons also 'hum' but their vibrations are in higher
      frequencies of the electronic register - visible light,
      X-rays, ...
    - When examined as in slow motion, this emitted radiation is
      no longer a continuous hum: instead it is a rapid staccato
      of radiation bullets, or quanta...

### Photons
    - Einstein called these radiation quanta 'photons', to _rhyme
      with electrons_ and underline the particle-like behaviour
      of electromagnetic radiation.

### Signals
    - An electron signals its presence like a _quantum radar
      beacon_, continually emitting and absorbing photons. These
      transient photons surround the electron like an EM _halo_,
      its field.
    - When another electron approaches, some photons instead of
      returning to their home electron, _become entangled_ in the
      halo of the second electron, and _transfer_ their
      allegiance.
        - This is how the EM force is transmitted, and one
          electron _feels_ the presence of another.

### Graft
    - To describe such effects, Dirac's designer equation had to
      be harnessed to the EM field. Grafting the equation onto
      the mathematics of Quantum EM without losing their elegance
      was difficult.

### Numbers
    - In this emergent QED, one feature was crucial:
        - Even the most complete theories ultimately depend on
          numbers that can only be measured by experiment.
    - Einstein's SR said that the velocity of light is the same
      everywhere, but cannot predict its actual value. This key
      parameter can only be measured.
        - (but can it really be measured? see Maudlin and the
          "speed" of anything)

### effect
    - QED had its characteristic input number, and it is very
      small. Assemble enough atoms together and their coherent
      magnetism can lift a wrecked car, but when a single photon
      messenger is absorbed or emitted, the effect is tiny, an
      effect of less that 1% in the quantum calculations.
    - Further photons contribute less than 1% of 1%, so the EM
      experience of a single electron whould be represented
      fairly accurately by a token photon, as Bhabba had done.

### Out of control
    - Extending the calculations to include additional effects
      should have given _tiny corrections_, fine tuning almost
      too small to measure, but instead, the mathematics exploded
      unexpectedly.
    - Rather than giving tiny numbers, it _spat out infinities_,
      which jammed up any chance of making calculations.
    - QED appeared to have gone completely out of control.


### electron charge
    - Dirac suspected this could be due to the definition of the
      electric charge carried by a single electron. It was this
      charge that governed the strength of any EM effect.
    - An electron is given a specific electric charge, a tiny but
      fixed fraction of the amount of electriciy carried by
      a current of 1 ampere during 1 second.
    - But any single quantum electron is shrouded by its quantum
      halo, which itself mimics electric charge, masking the
      charge on the '_bare_' electron inside.

### solution
    - Calculations should separate this bare charge from the
      extra contributions due to the electron-photon halo,
      _alleged to be infinite_.

    - The trick was to attribute the naked electron with an
      _additional infinite charge_, the latter exactly cancelling
      the infinite contribution from the calculation, giving
      a result that agreed with the _measured charge_ on the
      electron.

    - _Subtracting two infinities_ to give a precise result
      looked very suspect. There is an infinite number of
      numbers: add these up and the result is also infinite, but
      more so. How can the number of numbers equal their sum.

    - But physicists said that the infinities of QED could cancel
      each other out. Such a trick hinted that the theory was
      somehow deficient, but it worked anyway.

### Renormalisation
    - Theorists called their mysterious procedure
      '_renormalisation_' and marched on.
    - The trick could be cleverly camouflaged because its
      infinities occur only with quantities that _no theory can
      predict_ and have to be determined by _measurement_.
    - (like the charge on teh electron, or its mass)
