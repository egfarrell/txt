### you just know the motions of other bodies
    - If the body is a _test body_, (i.e. its mass and charges
      are so small the body exerts a negligible influence on
      other bodies in the universe) and if the initial positions
      and subsequent motions _of the other bodies_ are known,
      then Newton's laws, in conjunction with the laws
      determining the forces, are enough to determine the
      subsequent motion of the test body _uniquely_.

### Area Law and angular momentum
    The Earth rotates with:
    - constant _angular velocity_ 
    - with its _axis_ pointing in a fixed direction in space.

    This parallels the AREA LAW:

    - `constant [angular velocity] = [equality of area] swept out. `
        - It is obvious that for motion in a circle, equality of
          area swept out is converted into equality of angular
          velocity.

    * The fixed direction of polar axis corresponds to _fixity of
      the plane_ of motion in the area law.

### dual nature
    1. Through _Lex Prima_, absolute space and time keep
       a body moving in a straight line at a uniform speed
       and would do so for ever but for the intervention of
       other bodies in the universe. 

    2. Through _Lex Secunda_ the bodies mutually deflect
       each other from one inertial motion _to another_.

    - We note also that the law of inertia itself has two quite
      distinct parts: 
        - the rectilinearity of the motion  (absolute space)
        - and the uniformity of the motion. (absolute time)

### Law 1
    - The law of inertia has 2 distinct parts:
    1. rectilinearity of motion  <----->  absolute space
    2. uniformity of motion      <----->  absolute time

### absolute space
    - We began by asking what use is absolute space if your
      motion through it cannot be detected by any experiment?

    - The answer is that only highly special motion is
      undetected. 

    - Uniform motion remains undetectable but any acceleration
      can be detected. 

    - _Why this is so, we do not know_. 

    - It is this puzzle Mach hoped to solve with his suggestion
      that somehow the distant stars are what actually determine
      the allowed frames of reference.

### inertial systems
    - Sticking to the useful fiction that we can 'see' absolute
      space, we define an _inertial frame_ of reference (also
      called an inertial system) as one that is either:

    1. at rest in absolute space
    2. moves uniformly though absolute space in a fixed direction

### Interesting: (circular definition!!)
    - One can alternatively _ignore all references_ to 'absolute
      space' and simply say an inertial frame of reference is one
      in which Newton's laws hold in the form he stated them.

    Important:
    - the operational definition _Lange_ eventually found for an
      inertial frame of reference.

### 4 forces!
    The most distinctive feature of Newton's dynamics is the
    concept of force, or rather of forces, since Newton actually
    employed _three_ related but distinct concepts of force. 

    I said Newton employed three different force concepts but it
    would be more accurate to say that he employed two: 

    * inherent force.

    * motive force. (which had two kinds)
        - impulsive 
          (acting instantaneously) 
        - continuous 
          (changing the speed and direction continuously) 

    In Newton's mind there was no essential difference
    between the two kinds of motive force.

    1. innate (inherent) force
    2. motive force - impulsive
    3. motive force - continusous

    - It is ironic that the word 'dynamics' was coined by Leibniz
      to characterize a _fourth_ concept of force.

    - This was his famous vis viva, the modern kinetic energy
      (apart from a factor). 

    - But energy is the one concept that hardly features in
      Newton's dynamics, and he never introduced it as a special
      concept.

# Interesting!
    - Consider an initial condition in which the _distance_
      between the earth and the sun is not changing. 

    - In the frame provided by the distant stars, such is the
      case when 
        - either the earth is at rest in that frame 
        - or is moving with any velocity at right angles to the
          line joining the earth and the sun. 

    - But as far as the earth-sun system is concerned, such
      a motion is nonexistent (note: we treat the earth and sun
      as mass points). 

    - Thus, in a purely relational theory of motion, when we look
      at things from the point of view of kinematic relativity,
      it is completely inexplicable why the earth falls to the
      sun in only the _single case when the transverse velocity
      is zero_; 

    - in all other cases a completely different motion results.
      Thus, there is evidently much more to motion than just the
      earth-sun RELATIVE SEPARATION.


### Galileo
    As already noted, it is necessary to distinguish between:
    1. Galilean relativity.
    2. Kinematic relativity. 

    - _They are not the same thing_. 

    - One should guard against the common assumption that
      although they are not identical, Galilean relativity is at
      least a necessary consequence of kinematic relativity. 

    - It is argued that if motion is entirely relative and space
      plays no part in dynamics, any motion through 'space,'
      including uniform motion, should obviously be undetectable.

- Finally, there is a further deep reason why dynamics took so
  long to discover. In accordance with Newton's laws, the earth
  is in a state of rotation. Nevertheless, a frame of reference
  fixed to the _surface of the earth approximates an inertial
  frame_ to a very good degree, and since according to Newton,
  a state of uniform motion is indistinguishable from one of
  rest, the Galilean invariance of dynamics produces a powerful
  impression that the earth is immobile. 

- The following chapters examine how the belief in terrestrial
  immobility held back the discovery of dynamics.


- The instinctive approach is to say that motion is to be
  measured by _rods and clocks_. 

- But once it is realized that the behaviour of rods and clocks
  is itself governed by laws of nature that are not known in
  advance it becomes clear that the choice of the means of
  description goes hand in hand with the establishment of
  concrete empirical facts about motion as it is actually
  observed. _[Harvey Brown, physical relativity!!]_ 

- The rigid structure of the earth provided a perfect framework
  within which motion could be observed and studied. 

    - Because nothing significant in the framework changed, it
      acquired the status of the frame, or arena, of motion. It
      is certain that this apprehension of an all-pervading frame
      of reference occurred instinctively and unconsciously. 

    - _For practical purposes is indistinguishable from Newton's
      absolute space_. It takes an effort of will to question the
      existence of this space, which may be called _intuitive
      space_. 


- Just as the stability of the earth and heavens gave rise to
  _intuitive space_, another accident gave rise to an equally
  pervasive concept: the _uniform flow of time_. 

- The origin of this notion is clearly the rotation of earth. It
  is interesting that an inbuilt belief in the uniform flow of
  time was clearly established long before scientific evidence
  was forthcoming to demonstrate that there was a sound basis for

For now we will ignore the problem of kinematic relativity
and assume absolute space and time are somehow accessible to
human senses, so that all Newtonian concepts are well
defined. A significant feature of Newtonian dynamics is the
motion of bodies has far reaching _directional_ aspects. The
_speed_ of a body in absolute space has only restricted
dynamical significance. A more significant concept is
_velocity_, i.e., speed in a definite direction. This could
not possibly be deduced from the mere concepts of space,
time, and motion and was _a realization that came very
late_ in the discovery of dynamics.


One of the earliest of the '_shocking_' discoveries the
Greeks made was that the sun does not take the same number
of days to pass through each of these four quadrants. Its
apparent motion is _nonuniform_. 

Both results take us close to the heart of dynamics and were
established in a clean form by Kepler. The first effect,
that each planet moves in a fixed plane containing the sun,
was discovered for the earth's motion in antiquity under the
guise of the _ecliptic_. Its extension by Kepler to all of
the planets is not reflected in any special name, but it
would be highly appropriate to call it _Kepler's Zeroth
Law_ (as it is sometimes called).
