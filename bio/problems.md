Maurice HT Ling · Nanyang Technological University

1. How does the expression changes of one or more genes
   affect the rest of the transcriptome?

2. How much variation in gene expression can a cell tolerate
   (expression buffering ability)?

3. How are antisense transcripts regulated and how they
   modulate RNA transcripts?

4. Is there a mathematical model to determine the abundance
   of proteins and activity from the abundance of RNA
   transcripts (translation and metabolic capacity)?

5. How does individual proteins connect up into pathways
   (emergence and evolution of metabolism)?

6. How much is a cell line a normal cell? That is, how much
   extrapolation can be expected for studies done on cell
   lines to reflect that of a normal cell?

7. What has changed from normal/healthy state of a cell to
   disease condition(s) and how much recovery can be done?

8. How much is a mouse a rat, and how much is a mouse
   a human (extrapolation between organisms)?

9. How does a pluripotent cell knows when to be specified and
   committed towards a differentiation path, and how does an egg
   deprogram such commitment? 
    - For example, how does the cells forming the hair follicles
      of our eyebrows know that it cannot grow a strand of hair
      longer than 3-6mm while just an inch away, the follicles on
      our scalp is allowed to grow hair up to 2 metres long?

10. How does the various clock circuits work at every level of
    biology?
        - Examples: How does the opposite centrioles know when to
          start anaphase at the same time during mitosis?
        - How does each ovary knows when is its turn to ovulate?
        - How do we know when to wake up and when to sleep in the
          absence of daylight?
        - How do we consciously know the estimated time when we
          wake up in the middle of the night?
        - How does my grandmother feel rheumatic pains on her
          joints before any meteorological instruments register
          the likelihood for rain?
