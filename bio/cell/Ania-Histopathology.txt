# Info "UDH"

### TDLU: smallest unit
    - "Terminal-duct lobular Units"
    - smallest structure (Hystological)
    - Globule "grapes"
    - ducts
    - collecting duct
    - 2mm
    - normal

- These terms appear in the histopathological results

### - HELU
    - Hyperplastic Enlarged Lobular Unit
    - normal for lactating
    - can vary with hormones
    - two possibilities:
        - NUMBER of cells (hyperplasia) <--- female only
        - enlargement (hypertophia): not in breast, in prostate
    - can develop:
        - microcysts
        - `UDH`
            - ImageID: 8864
            - Usual Ductal Hyperplasia
            - no atypical cells
            - combination: some disease
            - benign mammary dysplasia
            - Result: The breast structure with fibrocystic
              changes, "apokryowa" metaplasia,
              micro-calcification, focal changes with UDH
        - `ADH`
            - Atypical Ductal Hyperplasia
            - strange shape (multiple)
            - can have big nucleus
            - (blood vessels: plasma cell red)
    - DCIS
        - Ductal Carcinoma in situ
        - in situ: inside lobule or duct
        - important: 'CANCER' = `atypical` cells
            - but not neccessarily malignant
        - even larger enlargement
        - more numbers of atypical, and diameter of ductal lobule
    - IDC
        - _invasive_ Ductal Carcinoma
        - invasive = 'malignant'
        - broken out
