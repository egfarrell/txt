
harvey weinberg equilibrium
    1. There can be no mutation (not big effect)
    2. The population must be isolated.
    3. The population must be large.
        (genetic drift, bottlenecks, founder effect)
        This is as important as natural selection.
        Note: population changes due to this is not
        the result of adaptions. They are random changes.
    4. Mating must be random.
    5. Reproductive success must be random.

Natural selection deals with points 4 and 5.
Natural selection produces non-random changes 
in allele frequencies. This is because particular
alleles produce more adaptive phenotypes.
