### Characteristic of life
    Life has a high degree of order with a hierarchy of
    _structural levels_. Living systems exhibit order
    across a range of scales and sizes.

### Hierarchy of biology
    * molecules (closest to physics)
    * cells (molecules come together)
    * tissues
    * organs
    * organism
    * populations (groups of organisms)
    * community (e.g. coral reef)
    * ecosystem
    * biosphere (planet earth)

### unique feature
    - Properties at one level of the hierarchy are not completely
      predicted by properties at a lower level e.g.
        - Supervenience!
          $MY_NOTES/physics/metaphysics/metaphysics-tips.txt:385
    - brain is made from neurons but we couldn't predict how the
      brain will act just from info about neurons. 
    - Conciousness is an _emergent_ property.

### consequence of emergence
    - to really understand how something works, we have to look
      at it across a range of levels

### two levels very important
    1. level of the cell
    2. level of the organism

### why organisms critical level
    why are organisms a crtical level in the hierarchy? Well
    at this level of organisation, _reproduction_ occurs and
    it is at this level that _evolution_ must directly acts.

### heritable
    Traits that can be passed on from an organism to its
    offspring are called heritable. But its unlikely that
    all offspring will be indentical. Theres likely to be
    errors (copy error).

### copy error
    Consequence of copy error is there will be _variation_
    about the offspring.

### better ability
    some offspring will be able to do things a little
    better, some a little worse.

### competition
    differential reproduction of the variations in offspring
    means that over time, whatever information is possesed
    by the organisms that are doing better will become more
    prevalent.

### Evolution by natural selection
    * Organism
    * variation
    * competition
    * differential reproduction
        some reproduce more
        some reproduce less
    * adaptions
        (traits that are more advantageous become more prevalent)
    * adaptions
        those traits that evolve by natural selection

### Upshot
    - everything we see biological organisms doing now is the
      result of _adaptations_ and _natural selection_. 
    - This is why organisms are so important to understand. They
      are the units that do the reproduction and are the unit on
      which natural selection acts.

### want to understand adaptations?
    - then we must understand organisms

### Cells
    so why do we care about cells then if the organism is so
    important? Two related answers:

    1. the _cell_ is the minimal unit of structure capable
       of independently doing all the activities of life!
       (its the smallest unit that could be truly called
       living)

    2. "_packaging_" - the ability to separate the inside from
       the outside, to separate one kind of molecule from
       another kind. etc. The packaging provided by the cell
       allows all the complex chemistry to occur. Different
       processes can be kept separate.

### Cells background: Two types:

    1. `Prokaryotes` - prokaryotic cells
        * enclosed by cell (plasma) membrane
        * bacteria - single cell organisms
        * (some have a flagella)
        * simple life-forms but successful e.g. E. Coli

    2. `Eukaryotes` - eukaryotic cells
        * plant, animal, fungus (i.e. all non-bacteria)
        * can have single-celled Eurkaryotes but all
          multi-celluar organisms are composed of eukaryotic
          cells.
        * not only a package themselves but have internal
          sub-packages. Each sub-package (Organelles) separated
          from the rest of the cell by its own lipid membrane.

### Eukaryotes - Organelles (sub-packages)
    Three main types:
    1. information processing
    2. energy processing
    3. Endomembrane system
       - transporting and packaging biochemical reactions

### Organelle - Information Processing
    - The nucleus. Large dark element seen by early
      microscopists. 
        - (Greek: "eu" = well, true. "karyon" = nut or kernel). 
    - So the Eukaryotes have a true nucleus. 
    - (The Prokaryotes don't have a true nucleus.)

    - Nuclear Envelope: 
        - nucleus divided from rest of cell by a double membrane
          that has tiny pores so things _can_ get in and out of
          the nucleus.

### Chromosomes
    - DNA physically packaged in long strings. For must of the
      life of the cell, DNA is dispersed.
    - Its only when the cell comes to divide that DNA starts to
      condense and bunch up and becomes visually what we would
      call Chromosomes. (the squiggley X and Y things)


### Organelle - Energy Processing

    * Chloroplasts (in plant cells)
        - uses photosynthesis to capture energy from the sun

    * Mitochondria
        - all Eukaryotes have these to obtain energy by breaking
          down carbohydrates and sugars.

    * DNA
        - Chloroplasts and Mitochondria have their own DNA so
          they can replicate themselves. It is thought that early
          cells engulfed other cells and they became organelles.


### Organelle - Endomembrane system
    - Very complicated
    - One piece - endoplasmic reticulum.
    - This is an internal transport and processing system in the
      cell. 
    - Analogous to different rooms, vats and chemical containers
      in a chemical manufacturing plant.
