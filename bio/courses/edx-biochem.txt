### You'll learn
    - structure and function of chemical building blocks of life
    - navigate protein structures using PyMOL
    - Central role of enzymes in catalyzing reactions of life
    - Primary metabolic pathways that power cells
    - Intricate mechanisms that regulate cellular metabolism
    - Integration of biochemical processes for cells, tissues,
      and whole organisms
    - Metabolic perturbations linked to various human diseases

### Overview
    - introduction to structure of macromolecules and
      biochemical approach to cellular function.

    - Topics addressing protein function will include enzyme
      kinetics, the characterization of major metabolic pathways
      and their interconnection into tightly regulated networks,
      and the manipulation of enzymes and pathways with mutations
      or drugs.

    - An exploration of simple cells (red blood cells) to more
      complex tissues (muscle and liver) will be used as
      a framework to discuss the progression in metabolic
      complexity.

    - New in this version of the course, learners will explore
      how alteration of these metabolic pathways relates to the
      development and progression of some human diseases.
