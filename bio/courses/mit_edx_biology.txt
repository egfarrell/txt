# EDX biology

- Fundamentals of Biology was designed for independent study. 

- It draws upon 3 versions of MIT's Intro Biology classes:
    - 7.012: Eric Lander & Robert Weinberg: genomics, neurobiology, and cancer cell biology.
    - 7.013: Sive and Jacks: development and cancer cell biology.
    - 7.014: Walker and Chisholm: ecology and environment.

- All three classes cover the same core material, including the
  principles of biochemistry, genetics, molecular biology, and
  cell biology. In addition, each version has its own distinctive
  material.

### hierarchy
    -  Biosphere   -->   Earth
    -  Ecosystem   -->   forest
    -  Organism    -->   human
    -  Organ       -->   eye
    -  Tissues     -->   retina
    -  Cells       -->   rod cell
    -  Organelles  -->   nucleus, mitochondra
    -  Molecules   -->   DNA, ATP

### cell size
    - prokaryotic     1-2 microns                     (dna floating around in cell)
    - eukaryotic      10-40 microns (can be bigger)   (dna packaged up in nucleus)
