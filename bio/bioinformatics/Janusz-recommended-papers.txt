        - $MY_IMAGES/bio/Greek-Babylonian-Science.jpg

### ... modeling of RNA 3D structure using Experimental data
    - A more advanced (and longer) review on how experimental
      methods are combined with computational approaches to
      determine RNA structure. This is what we do in the lab:
        - $MY_ZOTERO/2019/ponce-salvatierra_et_al-2019-computational_modeling_of_rna_3d.pdf

### Genome-wide mapping of SARS-CoV-2
    - Our recent work on the SARS-CoV-2 RNA genome, which we
      continue in various other projects - something that may be
      interesting for you from the point of view of combination
      of computational research with medicine:
        - $MY_ZOTERO/2020/manfredonia_et_al-2020-genome-wide_mapping_of_sars-cov-2_rna.pdf

- These articles have been written for readers with specialized
  knowledge and technical details may be very difficult to
  understand (and quite distracting) for someone coming from
  a very different area.

- But I hope they can give you a general overview of our interest
  and current work.

# Recommended further reading
    - See Zotero: 'Janusz-Recommended-more'

    - Nature and magnitude of aromatic base stacking in DNA and
      RNA: Quantum chemistry, molecular mechanics, and experiment
        - Rating: `**`
        - An instructive review of the concepts used in quantum
          chemistry and molecular mechanics. It makes clear why
          the origin of stacking is more akin to London forces
          than the interaction of overlapping π orbitals.
        - https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/bip.22322


    - Modeling complex RNA tertiary folds with Rosetta. (2015)
        - Rating: `**`
        - Recent developments on the very first atomic-resolution
          method for predicting RNA 3D structures inspired by
          a successful approach developed earlier for proteins.
        - https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0076687914000524


    - RNA-Puzzles Round II: assessment of RNA structure
      prediction programs applied to three large RNA structures.
      (2015)
        - Rating: `**`
        - A report of a second round of RNA-Puzzles, highlights
          progress and remaining challenges in RNA 3D structure
          modeling, as well as possible synergies between
          different approaches.
            - $MY_ZOTERO/2015/miao_et_al-2015-rna-puzzles_round_ii.pdf
            - https://rnajournal.cshlp.org/content/21/6/1066


    - TBI server: web server predicting ion effects in RNA
      folding. PLOS ONE 2015
        - Rating: `**`
        - Back references (to 2005) provide details on the
          tightly bound ion (TBI) model. It is important to treat
          [Mg(OH2)6]2+ (the real species) in a different manner
          than mean field approximations (such as
          Poisson-Boltzmann) permit.
            - $MY_ZOTERO/2015/zhu_et_al-2015-tbi_server.pdf
            - http://rna.physics.missouri.edu/MCTBI/
            - https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0119705



    - Theoretical studies of RNA catalysis: Hybrid QM/MM methods
      and their comparison with MD and QM (2009)
        - Rating: `*`
        - A frank assessment of the strengths and limitations of
          applying QM/MM to biological applications; in
          particular, pointing out the difficulty of partitioning
          and computing the QM and MM interfaces in a way that
          generates meaningful understanding of the process under
          study.
            - $MY_ZOTERO/2009/banáš_et_al-2009-theoretical_studies_of_rna_catalysis.pdf
            - https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S1046202309000905


    - Chapter Five - Computational Prediction of Riboswitch
      Tertiary Structures Including Pseudoknots by RAGTOP:
      A Hierarchical Graph Sampling Approach
        - Rating: `*`
        - Provides an introduction to the concept of using graphs
          of the global secondary structure structure to map out
          the global architecture of the structure using the
          statistics of known structures as a basis for finding
          these configurations.
        - https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S007668791400055X


    - RNA-Puzzles: a CASP-like evaluation of RNA
      three-dimensional structure prediction. (2012)
      14:610-625.
        - Rating: `*`
        - The description of the very first community-wide
          experiment aiming at evaluation of RNA 3D structure
          prediction methods and approaches. Introduces
          methodology of RNA structure assessment.
            - $MY_ZOTERO/2012/cruz_et_al-2012-rna-puzzles.pdf
            - https://rnajournal.cshlp.org/content/18/4/610


    - Sampling and scoring: a marriage made in heaven. (2013)
        - Rating: `*`
        - Though directed to proteins, the same conclusions apply
          to RNA-protein interactions.
            - $MY_ZOTERO/2013/vajda_et_al-2013-sampling_and_scoring.pdf
            - https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/prot.24343
