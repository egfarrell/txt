### IUPAC DNA Codes
    - NUCLEOTIDE       BASE                    MNEMONIC
    - A                Adenine
    - C                Cytosine
    - G                Guanine
    - T                Thymine
    - U                Uracil
    - R                A or G                  puRine
    - Y                C or T                  pYrimidine
    - S                G or C                  Strong interaction
    - W                A or T                  Weak interaction
    - K                G or T                  Keto group
    - M                A or C                  aMino group
    - B                C or G or T             Not A
    - D                A or G or T             Not C
    - H                A or C or T             Not G
    - V                A or C or G             Not T/U
    - N                any base                aNy
    - . or -           gap
