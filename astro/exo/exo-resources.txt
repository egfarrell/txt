### Exoplanet Resources


### Courses

    1. __edX - "Astrophysics: Exploring Exoplanets"__ by ANU
       (Australian National University):
    - Covers the fundamentals of exoplanet detection, including
      the transit method, radial velocity method, and direct
      imaging. It also includes discussions on exoplanet
      atmospheres.
        - https://www.edx.org/learn/astrophysics/australian-national-university-astrophysics-exploring-exoplanets

    2. __Coursera - "Exoplanets"__ by University of Geneva:
    - Provides a comprehensive overview of exoplanet detection
      methods, with an emphasis on the transit method. It also
      touches on the study of exoplanet atmospheres.
        - https://www.coursera.org/learn/exoplanets

    3. __Harvard University - "Exoplanets: Finding and
    Understanding Alien Worlds"__:
    - Available on edX, this course covers various exoplanet
      detection techniques, including the transit method and the
      study of exoplanet atmospheres through transmission
      spectroscopy.
        - https://www.classcentral.com/course/edx-alien-worlds-the-science-of-exoplanet-discovery-and-characterization-1832
    - [Link to
      course](https://www.edx.org/course/exoplanets-finding-and-understanding-alien-worlds)

    - HarvardX: Super-Earths and Life
        - https://www.edx.org/learn/astronomy/harvard-university-super-earths-and-life?index=product&queryID=e9d2908d07bf7798e4f3c515c91bf6b0&position=3&linked_from=autocomplete&c=autocomplete

### Books

    1. __"Exoplanets"__ edited by Sara Seager:
        - A comprehensive overview of the field of exoplanet
          research, covering detection methods, characterization,
          and the study of exoplanet atmospheres. It’s an
          excellent resource for understanding the science behind
          transmission spectroscopy.
            - https://www.amazon.com/Exoplanets-Sara-Seager/dp/0816540069

    2. __"The Exoplanet Handbook"__ by Michael Perryman:
    - A detailed reference book that covers the methods used to
      detect exoplanets, including the transit method, and the
      techniques used to analyze exoplanet atmospheres.
        - https://www.amazon.com/Exoplanet-Handbook-Michael-Perryman/dp/1108419775

    3. __"Transiting Exoplanets"__ by Carole A. Haswell:
    - Focuses specifically on the transit method, explaining the
      techniques used in exoplanet detection and the analysis of
      light curves. It also touches on transmission spectroscopy
      for studying exoplanet atmospheres.
        - https://www.amazon.com/Transiting-Exoplanets-Carole-Haswell/dp/1107427100

### Resources and Tutorials

    1. __NASA Exoplanet Exploration Program:__
    - NASA’s website offers a wealth of information on
      exoplanets, including detailed explanations of detection
      methods like transits and transmission spectroscopy. It
      also features news and resources for further learning.
    - [Link to NASA Exoplanet
      Exploration](https://exoplanets.nasa.gov/)

    2. __Exoplanet Transit Database (ETD):__
    - This database provides access to light curves and other
      data related to exoplanet transits. It’s a valuable
      resource for those interested in working with real data.
    - [Link to ETD](http://var2.astro.cz/ETD/)

    3. __arXiv.org - Astrophysics Section:__
    - This preprint repository hosts thousands of research papers
      on exoplanet detection, transit methods, and transmission
      spectroscopy. Search for topics of interest to find the
      latest research.
    - [Link to arXiv
      Astrophysics](https://arxiv.org/archive/astro-ph)
