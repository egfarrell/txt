herschel telescope

The telescope is equipped with three complementary
instruments: two cameras/medium resolution spectrometers and
HIFI

PACS -
    Photodetector Array Camera and Spectrometer

SPIRE -
    Spectral and Photometric Imaging Receiver

HIFI
    - Heterodyne Instrument for the Far Infrared), a very
      high-resolution heterodyne spectrometer
