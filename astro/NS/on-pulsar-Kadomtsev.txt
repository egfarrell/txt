- A neutron star is an analog of an atomic nucleus while
  a regular star is an analog of an atom.

- An atomic nucleus has a radius of order 10^(13) cm whereas the
  radius of a typical atom is of order 10−8 cm. Thus, their sizes
  differ by a factor of 10^(5). 
    - Correspondingly, the radius of a neutron star is
      approximately 10^(5) times smaller than that of a regular
      star. If the dimensions of the Sun are of order 10^(6) km,
      those of a neutron star are 10 km.
