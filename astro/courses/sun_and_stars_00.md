### Chapter 1 - Seeing the Sun
    - Basic characteristics of the sun
        - Solar luminosity 3.84 x 10^26 Watt
        - i.e. RATE at which energy radiated

    - Nature of light
        - Two simple models
            - EM wave model: `c = fλ`
            - photon model:  `e = hf`
        - Blackbody
            - emits characteristic continuous spectrum peak
              wavelength determined by WIENS LAW 
            - `λ_peak/m = (2.9 10^-3) / (T/k)`
        - Absorption/emission lines
            - cause by electron transitions to different energy
              levels
        - EM spectrum
            - all forms of radiation can be modelled by EM waves
              of decreasing wavelength or photons of increasing
              energy

    1. Photosphere ("_surface_")
        - "visible" surface of sun
        - Semi-transparent shell of gas about 500km thick
        - Temperature around 6000k (mid way down)
        - Displays limb darkening
        - Displays differential rotation (traced by sunspots)
        - Sun only star where we can study sunspots and
          granulation

    2. Chromosphere ("lower/inner atmosphere")
        - Overlies photosphere
        - Patchy layer gaseous material, few 1000km thick
        - 75% hydrogen (rest helium)
        - Spectra used to analyse it
        - Temp increases rapidly with height (changes 20,000k
          over 2000km)
        - H-alpha emission lines, plages and filaments

    3. Corona ("upper atmosphere")
        - Outer atmosphere of sun
        - Separated from chromosphere by narrow, irregular
          transition region
        - Structure highly variable, responds to level of solar
          activity
        - Spectra show highly ionised atoms => temp: several
          million degrees(!!)


### Chapter 2 - The Working Sun
    - Solar Interior
        - Temp and density INCREASE as fractional radius DECREASES
        - Core temp: `15.6 * 10^6K`,ie  `15,600,000K`
        - Composition by mass: 
            - H 73%, 
            - He 25%, 
            - Z 2% (predicted by theory)

        `X`: Hydrogen mass fraction (Mass of H in sample / mass of sample)
        `Y`: Helium mass fraction
        `Z`: Metallicity ("heavy" elements)

        - EXCEPT in central core: where `H` nuclei converted to
          `He` nuclei
        - Suns EM radiation ORIGINATES IN CENTRAL CORE, release
          by pp reactions PP reactions also produce neutrinos

        - Energy release in core reaches surface via RADIATION
          & CONVECTION.
        - Radiative transport is a GRADUAL DIFFUSIVE PROCESS in
          which radiation is in local thermodynamic equilibrium
          with material through which it passes.
        - Solar neutrino problem: deficit of electron neutrinos


    - Solar Activity
        - Solar activity primarily MAGNETIC, many active regions
          with strong bipolar fields.
        - Magnetic fields store energy from MOTION OF PLASMA in
          surface layers
        - Rapid energy release produces solar flares and coronal
          mass ejections.
        - "Magnetic reconnection" plays a role in these events?
        - Solar activity cycle: 22 yrs to return to initial state
        - (twice period of sunspot variations)

    - Solar Wind & Heliosphere
        - Corona not static - continuous outflow called "Solar
          Wind"
        - Outflow is a plasma same composition of sun - speed 300
          - 750 km/s
        - Coronal outflow interacts with suns magnetic field.
        - If field strong, flow may be restricted to field
          lines...
        - Earths magnetic barrier: "Mangetosphere" may shrink or
          expand which alters pattern of charged particle flow,
          producing geomagnetic storms and aurorae.
        - Solar wind pressure eventually drops to match ISM gas
          pressure.
        - This boundary is limit of heliosphere - `110 - 160 AU`
          from sun.
