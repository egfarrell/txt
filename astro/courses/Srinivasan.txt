### Review
    - As a beginner, if you want to learn Astrophysics with an
      emphasis on the underlying physics and background history,
      then "What are The Stars?" and "Can Stars Find Peace?" by
      Srinivasan are the best I've found.
    - Being a Space Engineer and pursuing research in stellar
      astrophysics, I hardly found another book covering the
      entire range of topics in such a lucid style, with high
      school level mathematics.
