photometry
	measure of number of photons per second in a certain passband
	optical: U 350nm,B 450nm, V 550 ,R 700,I 850
	near IR: J, H, K

apparent brightness depends on
	collecting area of source (telescope v human eye)
	distance
	intrinsic brightness of sources (# photons per second or ratio with no units)


spectroscopy
	began with newton!
	continuum: peaks at the temperature according to wiens law

classify spectra
	photographed spectra - pickering + computers manually categorised at harvard
	classification based on strength of hydrogen absorption features
	class a - deepest hydrogen absroption features
	class b - next deepest hydrogen absroption features
	metals - all others lumped together

hydrogen spectra
	n=1 lowest energy state
	lyman series:
		transitions in and out of the 1 state occur mostly in the UV part of the spectrum
	balmer series:
		transitions in and out of the 2 state occur mostly in the visible part of the spectrum
		H-alpha transitions between n=2 and n=3 states
