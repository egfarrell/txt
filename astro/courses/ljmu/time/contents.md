### Week 3 - Variable Stars

    This week we will begin exploring some _phenomena_ associated
    with the time domain, and we'll start with _variable stars_.
    There are 3 lectures this week all delivered by our resident
    variables expert Dr David Bersier.

    There are a number of other firsts for this module this
    week:

    - Recommended background reading: Carroll & Ostlie, An
      Introduction to Modern Astrophysics, Chpater 14
      "Stellar Pulsation"
    - Tutorial 1: This tutorial is covering the material
      delivered this week.  

    Here's a quick breakdown of this week's contents:

    - Lectures - The video lectures
    - Lecture Notes - PDF file of the lecture slides
    - Supplementary Material - Interview with Dr Victoria
      Scowcroft (former ARI PhD student of Dr Bersier) about
      her work in the Small Magellanic Cloud (and the
      supporting journal paper), plus a number of related
      review papers.
    - Tutorial 1 - The first formal tutorial problem set


### Week 4 - Radiative Transfer

    This week we will introduce one of the most _important_
    concepts underpinning all of astrophysics, _radiative
    transfer_ - quite simply how energy, in the form of
    electromagnetic radiation, propagates through media.
    This week will also see the setting of Tutorial #2.

    There are three lectures this week, the first,
    a revision of static radiative transfer, delivered by
    me, and two lectures on radiative transfer in dynamic
    media delivered by Prof. Paolo Mazzali.

    - Carroll & Ostlie, Chapter 5 "The Interaction of Light
      and Matter"
    - Carroll & Ostlie, Chapter 8 "The Classification of
      Stellar Spectra"
    - Carroll & Ostlie, Chapter 9 "Stellar Atmospheres"


### Week 5 - Time Domain Facilities

    This week we will explore some of the existing and
    planed future facilities used in studying the Time
    Domain Universe.

    In addition to the general overview, Dr Chris
    Copperwheat - the Liverpool Telescope Astronomer in
    Charge - will deliver a pair of lectures, one dedicated
    to the Liverpool Telescope, and the second to its
    'successor' the aptly named Liverpool Telescope 2.

    This week's content:
    - Lectures - The three video lectures
    - Lecture Notes - PDF files of the lecture slides used
      in the videos (high and low resolution versions
      available)
    - Videos - Full resolution (and often with audio)
      versions of some of the movies/animations used in the
      lectures
    - Supplementary Material - For interest, we have
      provided a number of the technical/early results
      papers related to the LT, its instrumentation, and LT2

    Suggested Reading:
    - Carroll & Ostlie, Chapter 3 "The Continuous Spectrum
      of Light"
    - Carroll & Ostlie, Chapter 6 "Telescopes"
