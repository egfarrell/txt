### Study guide: Concepts you must understand: 
    - Magnitudes: apparent, absolute, bolometric 
    - Radiometry: flux (density), intensity (density),
      power, luminosity. 
    - Stefan-Boltzman and Wien laws. 
    - Rayleigh-Jeans approximation 
    - Photon statistics and probability distributions.
      Confidence levels. 
    - Measurements, precision, accuracy 
    - Signal to noise 
    - How telescopes work at various wavelength ranges 
    - Optical aberrations
    - Fourier transforms 
    - Resolution theorem, sampling, aliasing, sampling
      theorem 
    - Fermat's principle, laws of reflection, refraction 
    - Diffraction 
    - How various types of detectors work. - Understand the
      definitions of fundamental parameters, such as
      linearity, well depth, quantum efficiency, saturation
      - *Make sure you understand* the concepts of time
        response and frequency cutoff of a detector 
    - Principles of imaging 
    - Principles of photometry 
    - Magnitude systems 
    - Principles of spectroscopy 
    - How spectrographs work 
    - Spectral parameters: dispersion, resolution, resolving
      power, spectral orders - Relation between resolving
      power of a spectrograph and grating order, groove
      density, and slit width 
    - Diffraction gratings, the grating equation
    - Data reduction for imaging and spectroscopy 
    - How adaptive optics works 
    - Parameters related to image quality and seeing: Strehl
      ratio, Fried parameter, isoplanatic angle 
    - Fundamentals of interferometry 
    - Fundamentals of radio and submm astronomy
