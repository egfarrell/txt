    # Note:
    None of these three equations has the variable
    `M_{satellite}` in them. 

    The _period_, _speed_ and _acceleration_ of a satellite
    are only dependent on: 
    - the radius of orbit 
    - the mass of the central body the satellite is orbiting

    Just like  projectile motion on earth, the _mass of the
    projectile_ has no effect on the acceleration towards
    the earth and the speed at any instant. When air
    resistance is negligible and only gravity is present,
    the _mass of the moving object becomes a non-factor_.
    Such is the case of orbiting satellites.
