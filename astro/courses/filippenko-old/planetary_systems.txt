astrometry
	measurement of position and motions of stars

doppler shifts of absorption lines
	TRANSVERSE (perpendicular to line of sight) - no doppler shift
	moving away - red shift
	moving towards - blue shift

IMPORTANT doppler shift point
	move SLOWLY - doppler shift smaller
	move FASTER - doppler shift larger


shifts very small (planets not very massive)

	more like 3/4 m/s - like a fast walk

angle of inclination and radial velocity
	can only deduce minimum mass if you dont know viewing angle.
	if you're viewing it absolutely perpendicular you'll measure no
	doppler shift what-so-ever so even though a massive planet is tugging
	on the star, so you'd deduce to small a mass.
	what you get is: mass * sin(inclination angle)

discovery 1995
	looking for specific signal: expecting long periods and small wobbles (like jupiter)
	NOT expecting short periods (4 days!) and large wobbles, which is what they found.

what are they finding: (hot jupiters)
	selection bias:
	planets below 1 AU, some 0.1 AU
	short orbital periods - 3 or 4 day periods!
	cos easier to find!

highly eccentric orbits
	(in our solar system more circular orbit)
	what happened?
	probably planets were closer together and interacted gravitationally,
	one flung out of the system altogether and other reacts
	with a kick which gives it eccentric orbit.
	but hot jupiters close to star dont have eccentric orbits
	because they're so close to the stars (tidal effects make for circular orbits)

heavy element abundance corelation
	as percentage of heavy elements
	(e.g. iron) in star INCREASES,
	from say 1% to 4% to 6%,
	the fraction of exo-planets found INCREASES.
	supports model:
	growth through gravitational accretion of gases
	on to heavy element rocky core.

rocky planets
	if close to the star they must be rocky and not gaseous
	cos the gas would be blown off by stellar winds

transit method
	expected light curve decreases when transit occurs
	atmosphere found on transiting planet, because atmosphere
	absorbed some of the stars light. Sodium was detected.

little planet transit
	jupiters cause big light dip
	earth causes 1/1000 dip in sun's light curve
	but watch for sun spots...(spots not periodic though)
	but have to monitor lots of stars to see this... cf. Kepler

thermal emission of planet
	when planet is BEHIND the star, some of the IR emission of planet
	is blocked. (planets dont emit in the optical)
	ie. total brightness of Star + planet system decreases.
	Spitzer can notice this

gravitational micro-lensing
	get a very short brightning of the light from a background star
	when a planet passes in front of the star through our line
	of sight. This is because the planet focuses the stars light
	in a micro-lensing effect
