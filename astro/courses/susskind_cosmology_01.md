### susskind trick for deriving hubble law (V = HD)
    0. Distance `D = a * ∆x`
    1. Take time derivative of `D` (Distance)
    2. This gives you velocity
    3. multiply RHS by `a/a`
    4. This gives you a `D` on the RHS
    5. and youre also left with (`a-dot/a`) on the RHS
    6. This (`a-dot/a`) is then called the hubble
       constant/parameter

### Hubble "Constant"
    - Is constant across all of space (because space homogeneous)
    - but not constant in TIME: 
        - universe expands at different rates

### Expanding Universe
    - the actual distance between 2 galaxies in meters is
      expanding as time goes on
    - but the 2 galaxies remain separated by one unit of
      coordinate (comoving coords)

### ZERO scale factor
    - when scale factor "a" = 0, 
    - then density is infinite 
    - everything is squashed on top of each other

### _comoving_ coordinate system
    - this is the coord system embedded in the galaxy
    - in this case, the metric takes the standard form
      - `d(tau)^2 = dt^2 - a(t)(dx^2 + dy^2 + dz^2)`
    - its a prefered frame of reference? 
    - the isotropic frame of reference.
    - In another moving frame, the universe may not appear
      isotropic
