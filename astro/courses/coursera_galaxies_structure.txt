### density fluctuations
	- CMB, fluctuations 1 part in a million
	- filaments, voids, factor of a 100 over-dense
	- galaxies - factor of a million over-dense

### puzzle
	- by redshift z=6
	how do we go to such large density enhancements for galaxies/clusters etc
	- by evolving the small density fluctuations we see in the CMB...
	- ie in half a billion years?

### key idea
	- early universe density fluctuations are dominated by dark matter
	- which allows strcuture to form
	- by the way, thats another reason we think theres dark matter,
	otherwise we wouldnt have this large scale structure in the universe
