### quasar mass
	- back at redshift z=6 you have 10 million solar mass black holes
	- how are they created so early in the universe

### metalicity
	- high-z quasars are very metal rich
	- so formed in high potential wells
	- ie in most massive elliptical galaxies
	- so co-forming with most massive galaxies

### SMBH host galaxy corelations
	- main point: co-evolution and co-formation

### how are corelations possible over such large scales
	- ie smbh scales of micro-parsecs
	- galaxies on scales of kilo-parsecs

	- integrate all quasar light
		- and assume 10% efficiency
		- gives you the total mass that went into creating quasars

	- gas infall

		- bursts of star formation (ie galaxy formation)
