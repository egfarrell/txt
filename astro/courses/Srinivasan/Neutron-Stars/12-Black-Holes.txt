### Synders conclusion

      down, till it becomes _frozen_ precisely at the critical
      radius

### Schwarzschild Geometry
    - This describes not only the "curvature of space" but also
      the "warpage of time" near the star.
        - This is the dilation of time due to gravity.
    - Leads to gravitational redshift of light emitted from the
      stars surface.
        - Near the star, _time flows more slowly_ than far away.
          Therefore, the frequency of light must be lowered by
          the same amount.
    - Therefore for a distant observer, the star will become
      progressively redder and redder (this is what Synder found)
    - Schwarzschild exact result:
        - note: the simple argument in this image isn't a bad
          approximation
        - $MY_IMAGES/astro/bh-accretion/Srinivasan/gravitational-redshift-formula.jpg
    - note: as a photon climbs the gravitational potential well,
      it loses kinetic energy, but `cannot slow down`.
        - Since kinetic energy is related to the frequency, there
          is a frequency shift.

    - At the critical radius, time time dilation becomes
      infinite. Time does not flow at all!
    - As seen by an outside observer, frequency goes to zero.
        - The wavelength of radiation becomes infinte.
        - $MY_IMAGES/astro/bh-accretion/Srinivasan/Schwarzschild-radius.jpg

### Coordinate systems
    - Eddington had already proposed a coordinate system in "The
      mathematical theory of relativity" where there is no
      singularity at the Schwarzschild radius.
    - Was re-discovered in 1958 by Finkelstein.
    - So a star becomes black in GR at the same radius as John
      Mitchell's star.
    - But there's a fundamental difference.
        - Mitchells star
            - isn't black for everyone, just distant observers.
              (e.g. if you could get close enough to his Black
              star you'd see the light "jump up" before falling
              down again)
        - GR
            - no matter how close you get to the critical
              surface, no-one can see any light emerging

### Important point:
    - Dont think of the Schwarzschild radius as where the escape
      velocity = the velocity of light.
    - That is the wrong concept.
    - Instead, the radius is the place where the `frequency
      shift` becomes infinite.

### Some facts
    - The surface of a BH is the Event Horizon
    - It's a semi-permeable surface. Things can enter the
      horizon, nothing can come out.
    - Inside the Event Horizon, there are no allowed trajectories
      with a _constant distance_ from the centre.
        - once you're inside the EH, you can't say let me fire
          a rocket and get out
        - or let me `stay` at this current radius
        - the only timelike worldlines are those that point to
          `r=0`
        - timelike worldlines have velocity  `< c` (ie they are
          inside a light cone)
    - in-falling matter cannot arrest its collapse. The only way
      it could stop is by travelling at greater than light speed
    - even light has to keep going to the singularity at the
      centre

### Kerr black hole
    - static limit (edge of the ergosphere)
    - once you come inside this limit, you can no longer be
      stationary. You have to start `rotating` whether you like
      it or not.
        - Because the inertial frames are being dragged there by
          the `dt/dφ` term in the metric
        - it applies to material particles and light
    - $MY_IMAGES/astro/bh-accretion/Srinivasan/kerr-BH-horizons.jpg

### Plot
    - "Angular motion without angular momentum"
    - A particle is dropped _radially_ from rest at infinity onto
      a Kerr BH
    - notice: as it gets closer to the BH it temporarily moves
      away from the BH !
        - that's because the BH is rotating and it's dragging the
          inertial frames, and the particle is being pulled a bit
    - finally the particle is made to go _around_ ...
    - so now it has angular motion, but it had no angular
      momentum!
    - in a Schwarzschild BH, if you throw something in radially,
      it'll go in radially
	- $MY_IMAGES/astro/bh-accretion/Srinivasan/angular-motion-without-angular-momentum.jpg

### Conflict! (Information Paradox)
    - This deep discovery of Hawking raises a serious conflict
      between QM & gravity (if the no-hair theorems are correct)
    - The information paradox
    - no solutions ?

    - what goes in is a pure quantum state, what comes out is
      thermal radiation
    - and thermal radiation will have a huge entropy
    - but QM requires the evolution of a system is unitary
    - (ie the probabilities of a system are conserved).
        - or: the quantum entropy is _constant with time_
    - Possible solution: "soft hairs" (Quantum micro-states)
