No energy conservation comes from Noethers symmetry theorem, more
specifically it comes from time translational invariance.


In newtonian mechanics,
the stage is fixed,
so translational invariance,
so energy conservation.


In GR, space and time are dynamical,
universe is expanding,
so stage or playground is not invariant under time,
so energy is no longer conserved in an expanding universe.

But although theres no energy convservation in GR,
there is a rule which tells us HOW the energy changes as the universe expands.

you have to put energy IN to increase the volume,
ie to pull out the piston.
