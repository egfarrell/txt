Time is like space

A clock that moves around measures a different time than a clock that stays still...

Interesting twist when viewing time as similar to space...
The LONGER distance spacetime path  results in a SHORTER time..
If you just sat there in spacetime, time would be "longer"

Newtons rule: you have to move foward in time
Einstein: You have to move forward (slower than) the speed of light, no faster

Gravity works the same on everything... this means gravity is undetectable
