### Recommended Books
    - "Discrete-Time Signal Processing"
        - The Bible of DSP by Oppenheim & Schafer (1999)
        - exceedingly exhaustive, it is a must-have reference.

    - "Signals and Systems". Oppenheim and Nawab (1997) 
        - Background info on signals and systems.
        - Good start.

    - Other comprehensive DSP books:
        - Digital Signal Processing. SK Mitra (2006)
        - Digital Signal Processing. Proakis & Manolakis (2006)

    - Analysis by its History. Hairer and Wanner. (1996)
        - Fascinating examination of calculus origins.
        - $MY_BOOKS/Physics/Maths/Analysis/Alt-View/Analysis-by-its-History.pdf

    - Everything and More. 
        - David Foster Wallace (2003)
        - Compelling epistemological essay on the continuum.
        - Manages to be both profound and hilarious
        - $MY_BOOKS/Physics/Metaphysics/Everything-and-More-David-Foster-Wallace.pdf
