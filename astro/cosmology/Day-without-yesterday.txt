- For them, lambda `Λ` represented something more, something

- Lemaltre in particular began to treat the constant as the

    - Unlike Einstein, who used the lambda term on the left side
      of his field equations (the terms governing the metric

    - Lemaltre used `Λ` as a real negative pressure, a force that
      was negligible on the small scale of the solar system, but
      grew proportionally with distance on the cosmic scale.
      According to Kragh's 'Cosmology and Controversy':
        - In his 1933 address [to the American Academy of
          Sciences] he suggested an interesting interpretation of
          the constant, namely, that it could be understood as
          a _negative vacuum density_. 
        - "Everything happens as though the energy in vacuo would
