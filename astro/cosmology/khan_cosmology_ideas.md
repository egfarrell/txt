### Singly ionised helium
    - one electron knocked off. 
    - positive charge.
    - cooler environment than doubly ionised helium, 
    - but still hot! more transparent.

### Doubly ionised helium
    - both electrons gone. really a helium nucleus. 
    - double positive charge.
    - must be really hot environment! 
    - more opaque. 
    - nucleus absorbs light photons.

### Cephid Variables cycle - Dim
    - outer surface is full of doubly ionised helium.
    - Doesn't allow light to pass through. Absorbs photons,
      heats and expands.
    - Then because further away it cools.
    - Because cooler it picks up an electron and becomes
      singly ionised.

### Cephid Variables cycle - Bright
    - Bright - surface now full of singly ionised helium.
    - Allows light through but not getting heated anymore.
    - So collapses back into star. As they fall back towards
      the core
    - they become heated again and become doubly ionised
      helium (again!).
