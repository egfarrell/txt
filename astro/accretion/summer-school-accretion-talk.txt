# Childs Garden of Astrophysical disks

### Disk systems
    - Galactic disks (hot gas above our disk)
    - SMBH (eg Sgr A\*)
    - Stellar mass BH
    - Neutron Stars in binary systems have luminous disks
    - dwarf nova system
    - protostars (HL Tau disk)
    - around planets:
        - debris disks around Staturn (has density waves)
        - proto-moon disks

### Anomoulous Spiral arms
    - NGC 4258
    - in the centre of a galaxy
    - comes from a jet
    - water maser spots
        - $MY_IMAGES/astro/bh-accretion/water-maser-spots.jpg
        - position and velocity of master spots
        - agree with a keplerian curve. This tells us it's a low
          mass disk and also a thin disk
    - WARPS: This disk doesn't lie in a _plane_. It's made up of
      a series of rings that may be tilted and warped
    - Maser disk: far out from central object. Accretion energy
      per unit area is small. Most heating in this disk comes
      from illumination from a central accretion flow.

### Dimensionless parameter
    - Scale height: 
        - `H / R << 1`
    - Magnetic Reynolds number: 
        - assess coupling to magnetic field? 
        - assess non-ideal effects in the plasma 
          - (around a young star etc). 
        - Proto planetary disks: `<< 1`. Large parts of the disk
          may be de-coupled from the magnetic field.
    - measure importance of self-gravity in the disk.  if this
      parameter <= 1, then self-gravity becomes important in the
      disk
