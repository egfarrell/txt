
### - BHI colloquium
    - What kind of radiation comes from the accretion disc versus the
      radiation from the jet. Because the systems have both accreting
      gas and ejected material in the jet.

    - But these radiating areas are different. The disk is dense and
      relatively lowly magnetised while the jet Is less dense but
      highly magnetised.We imagine the radiation processes are quite
      different in these

    - In our simulations we can take the Kerr metric and simulate the
      motion of gas but radiation is more complex.

    - Radiation depends on what the particles are doing. How are they
      heated what energy distribution do they take. Is it thermal or
      non-thermal? Does it have a power law distribution? We don’t
      have a clue of any of these things. 

    - Mainly because we don’t have an understanding of the plasma
      physics in accretion disk. How particles are heated.

    - Andrew Chael has changed the code so that in each parcel of
      fluid you can follow what the electrons are doing distinct from
      the ions. Michael Rohan is doing interesting plasma physics
      work.

    - Electrons are what we are interested in. When they are hot
      produce the radiation.

    - We’ve got a code that can follow a whole distribution of
      electrons in each cell. Follow it as it moves along within the
      magnetic field. From this we can compute the radiation.

    - But he is missing one key input. In each cell as you produce
      heat from all the accretion physics how is this heat
      manifested? 

    - What kind of distribution does it put into the electrons. And
      this is a plasma physics issue. 

    - So we are hoping that plasma physics simulations will tell us
      how to heat up the electrons and Andrew can put that
      prescription into the simulation and run it.

    - And then will see what the radiation is. And in the meantime
      the EHT will see what it says to.

    - And hopefully by comparing against the real EHT data we can
      refine our understanding as to how particles are heated and how
      it differs between the ??? project.
