### Astrophysics Web Based resources
    - _NED_: NASA Extragalactic Database: find images and
      data on galaxies, AGN, galaxy clusters
    - _SIMBAD_: within our galaxy but outside the solar
      system
    - _ING_ have useful software for observation planning:
      STARALT (object availability for any night)
    - _Skyview_ virtual observatory: images of night sky
    - Observatory data archives: HST, ESO, ING
    - VizieR: download _reduced_ data
    - _IPAC_ site: home if IR astronomy (image data and
      catalogues of data derived from IR surveys)
