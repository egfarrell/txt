HIGH MASS Giant ellipticals
    * Red and dead, form early and grow in size but by
      accretion, not by star formation.
    * No late star formation
    * But high mass ellipticals really grow by a factor of
      3-5 over time...

Computing the universe,
    * Huge dynamic range of scales,
    * need somthing megaparsecs big (bigger than the scale
      on which things are non-linear) but ALSO need very
      high resolution to resolve all the very small things
      in it...

Computing methods
    * particle methods - put down particles and move
      according to forces 1000^3
    * fixed mesh - very efficient, but not high resolution
      2000^3
    * AMR - adaptive mesh refinement - can resolve the
      details but much more costly
    * structured mesh - latest method

(Heuristic!) Algorithm for star formation
    * if you have cold gas in a galaxy, turn it into stars
    * Why? Well we dont see much cold gas in galaxies, so
      Nature has found a way to do it
