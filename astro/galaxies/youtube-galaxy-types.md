- spiral galaxies make up _60%_ of all galaxies we see
- they have a disk, bulge and spiral arms
- the arms of spiral galaxies are mainly "blue":  and blue
  stars are mainly O and B type stars.
- blue stars live for a shorter length of time (theyre high
  mass stars) so the stars in the spiral arms are mostly
  hot, bright and young stars

- elliptical galaxies: we dont see a lot of blue stars and
  we dont see a lot of gas and dust
- this means elliptical galaxies probably have a lot of old
  red stars in them
- where did all the dust and gas go? we're not exactly sure

- galaxies that arent disk (spiral) or ellptical are called
  irregular galaxies
