# star is a body that:
    1. is bound by _self-gravity_
    2. radiates _energy_ supplied by internal source

# implications
    1. the shape of a star is _spherical_ (or spheroidal if
       axis-symmetric forces present)

    2. stars must _evolve_ 
        - internal energy released => changes in structure
          + composition

    3. stars die:
        * from violating condition 1:
            - breakup of star and scattering material into
              inter-stellar space

        * from violating condition 2:
            - exhaustion of nuclear fuel, star fades away
            - slowly, gradually cooling off, radiating away
            - energy accumulated earlier. Eventually becomes
            - extinct - disappears from all telescopes, dead star.

        * combination:
            - partial breakup (shedding matter) and
            - extinction.

# basic assumptions
    * Isolation
    * Uniform initial composition
    * spherical symmetry
