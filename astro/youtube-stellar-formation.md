### gas pressure
    - radiating and pushing outward (expansion) will cause star
      to cool down

### biggest factor:
    - in determining sequence and lifetime of star is its _mass_
      (asumming not in binary system)

### stars form from ISM
    * hydrogen (neutral and ionised),
    * helium gas,
    * dust particles which are surrounded by frozen water
      and made from carbon, nitrogen and water...

### sun is:
    - a third generation star! (Population "1"?)

### molecular clouds:
     - giant clumpy
     - size: 50 - 200 Ly
     - density: 100 -1000 atoms per cm^3 
        - (just above vacuum really)
     - temp: 10K (so can form molecules)

### clumps
    densest part of cloud, becomes unstable and contracts
    under its own gravity. But something has to cause the
    instability to trigger the collapse

### star-creation triggers:
    * self-gravity (just from random motion)
    * radiation pressure from nearby stars (cloud heats up)
      leads to collisions
    * shock waves / compression waves (from novas)

### proto-star
    forms deep within molecular cloud
    opaque to visible light but not
    opaque to infra-red

### mass of protostar
    the larger the mass, the quicker it forms
    sun took 30 million years to form, short time!
    sun will last for 10 billion years...

### clumps/clusters
    * its CLUMPS within the cloud that collapse. 
    * open clusters and globular cluster all probably formed
      from same cloud

### kinetic energy
    particles heat up as cloud collapses and heated
    particles show up as IR radiation

### plasma
    as temp increases, so does average motion of the
    molecules, they are moving in random motion and will run
    into each other, and eventually break apart, forming
    ions and eventually just free electrons and atomic
    nuclei...

### core
    everything else in the star responds to whats going on
    in the core

### radius
    as mass of proto-star increases,
    radius decreases and everything is pulled in closer,
    and rotation speed increases...
    (comes from conservation of angular momentum)
    also as it speeds up, it starts to FLATTEN out...
    which will form our little solar system

### stellar winds/bipolar flow
    * protostar stars to give off electrons and protons in
      a stellar wind,
    * because of rotation, the flow comes from both poles
      (0.5 million miles/hours) (seen in T Tauri stars)
    * these winds cause nearby gas to be blown away causing
      holes, columns and also causing additional compression
      in the cloud which triggers more star formation(!)

### minor clumps
    form planets - but stellar winds are deadly on these
    smaller clumps

### thermonuclear reaction
    * protostar is shining by light produced by heat
    * but when radius small enough to produce large
      temperatures and densities, you get nuclear reactions!
