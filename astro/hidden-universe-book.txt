### Wavelength bands:
    - radio:          centimetre (cm)     hundredth  of a metre
    - microwave:      millimeter (mm)     thousandth of a meter
    - infrared:       micron (μm)         millionth  of a meter (micrometer)
    - visible:        nanometer (nm)      billionth  of a meter
    - ultraviolet:    nanometer (nm)      billionth  of a meter
    - X-ray:          nanometer (nm)      billionth  of a meter
    - X-ray:          picometer (pm)      trillionth of a meter (thousandth of a billionth)
    - gamma ray:      picometer (pm)      trillionth of a meter


### Wavelength Regimes:
    - Sub-divisions of a wavelength band
    - e.g. near-infrared, mid-infrared, far-infrared

### natural colors
    - representative colors (false colors)
    - enhanced colour images (narrowband filters)

### Blackbody radiation = thermal radiation
    - Everything emits it - even humans!
    - How HOT is something? Look at its blackbody spectrum.

### Also:
    - synchroton radiation (particles in MAGNETIC field)
    - bremsstrahlung radiation (fast moving charges deflect one another)
