Here are the mathematical equivalents of Kepler's three laws in ASCII format:

### 1. First Law (Law of Ellipses):
   *The orbit of a planet is an ellipse with the Sun at one focus.*

   ```
   r = a(1 - e^2) / (1 + e*cos(theta))
   ```

   where:
    * r is the distance from the planet to the Sun
    * a is the semi-major axis
    * e is the eccentricity
    * theta is the true anomaly (angle from perihelion)

### 2. Second Law (Law of Equal Areas):
   *A line joining a planet and the Sun sweeps out equal areas in equal times.*

   ```
   dA/dt = constant
   ```

   or

   ```
   r^2 * d(theta)/dt = h = constant
   ```

   where `h` is the specific angular momentum

### 3. Third Law (Law of Harmonies):
   *The square of the orbital period is proportional to the cube of the semi-major axis.*

   ```
   T^2 ∝ a^3
   ```

   or (with units)

   ```
   T^2 = (4*pi^2 / G*M) * a^3
   ```

   where:
       * T is the orbital period
       * a is the semi-major axis
       * G is the gravitational constant
       * M is the mass of the Sun (assumed central body)
