"i" eclipses

- Placenames that begin with b, c, d, f, g, p, and t can be
  eclipsed. 
- Also: "i" becomes "in" before vowels. `in Áth Cinn` 

i mBaile Átha Cliath in Dublin
i gCamas             in Camas
i nDoire Iorrais     in Doire Iorrais
i bhFoirnís          in Foirnís
i nGaillimh          in Galway
i bPo                in te in  Pointe
i dTuairín Mín       in Tuairín  Mín
