### Questions:
    - MODULE 1: What is proofreading? Explore the role of a proofreader. Which elements should you look for when proofreading?
    - MODULE 2: What is a style guide used for?
    - MODULE 3: Discuss the typical series of stages for proofreading you will follow once a piece of writing has been passed to you.
    - MODULE 4: Elaborate on the methods and techniques carried out for proofreading on paper.
    - MODULE 5: Discuss the common errors to check for while proofreading.
    - MODULE 6: Explore the role of a copy-editor. What are the six rules of a copy-editor?
    - MODULE 7: What is line-editing? Which factors should a line editor point out?
    - MODULE 8: List the factual elements of a piece of writing that will need to be checked.
    - MODULE 9: What is a reference mark? What are the most commonly used reference marks? Why should we use reference notes? How are footnotes and endnotes used?
    - MODULE 10: What is plagiarism? Write a short note on Libel.
    - MODULE 11: What are the reality checks for you to weigh up, to be able to properly assess whether you think you’re capable of being a self-employed proofreader or copy-editor?
