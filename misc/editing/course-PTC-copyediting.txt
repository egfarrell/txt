### Sending in your work
    - When you have completed all the exercises and the
      assignment for each module, and noted any queries for the
      tutor, email them to us at:
      `dlediting@publishingtrainingcentre.co.uk`
    - Please send in your exercises, showing all the work you did
      on them, as they may highlight particular problems or
      misunderstandings on which your tutor can advise.
    - Our aim is to turn each module round in under four weeks
      (maximum), during which time it has to be:
    - • logged in by PTC
    - • sent to your tutor for marking
    - • returned to PTC and the marks recorded
    - • sent back to you.
    - Please be patient and use this time to re-read the module
      you have just worked on, or to study the next module. We
      strongly advise you not to start subsequent exercises or
      assignments before you have had marked work back from your
      tutor. If you do so, you run the risk of compounding errors
      from previous modules and of getting lower marks than if
      you had waited for your tutor’s feedback.
    - After your tutor has marked your assignment they will
      complete an Assignment Assessment Sheet, with comments on
      your progress and answers to questions you may have asked.
      The PTC will return the work to you after recording the
      marks.

### Marking system
    - Upon successful completion of the course you will receive
      a certificate indicating the overall grade achieved. We
      will send you details of the marking system when we return
      your first assignment.

### Course duration
    - Although Essential Copy-Editing is designed as a flexible
      course, you must aim to complete it within `18 months` of
      purchasing the course.
    - This is to ensure continuity of learning, as the
      assignments are progressive in nature. If you fail to
      complete the course within 18 months, you will be required
      to pay a re-registration fee if you wish to continue. After
      three years, your registration will automatically lapse.
