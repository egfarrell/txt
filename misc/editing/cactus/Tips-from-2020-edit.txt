### Phrases to remember

    - `ME`:        We are, therefore, ...
    - `PUBLISHED`: Therefore, we are ...

    - When developing 4D detectors ...
    - In the development of 4D detectors...

    - ... in order to keep the gain uniform ... 
    - ... while maintaining a uniform gain...

    - the main culprits in degraded time resolution are ...
    - the main factors in degraded time resolution are...

    - a good quality time resolution
    - a robust time resolution

    - ... that allows the evaluation of time resolution in a much easier manner
    - ... that enables a significantly easier evaluation of time resolution

    - After removing noise events ...
    - After eliminating noise events ...

    - In what follows, we require...
    - In the following, we require...

    - ... was fitted to a Gaussian function ...
    - ... was fitted with a Gaussian function ...

    - ... which may be considered due to an increase ...
    - ... which is attributed to an increase ...

    - On the other hand, at higher voltages, the time resolution deteriorates
    - Contrarily, the time resolution deteriorates at higher voltages,


    - ... in order to achieve a similar gain.
    - ... to achieve a similar gain.

    - ... would be of interest in the Biosciences.
    - ... would be of interest in the field of bioscience.

    - (conclusion)
    - ... we reported on a desktop sytem...
    - ... we report on a desktop sytem...

    - We also performed a TCAD simulation...
    - Moreover, we performed a TCAD simulation...

    - Prototype AC-LGAD sensors are to be fabricated, based on the results of the present study. 
    - The results of this study will facilitate the fabrication of prototype AC-LGAD sensors.

    - ... very fruitful
    - ... significantly fruitful

    - ... very inspiring
    - ... highly inspiring
