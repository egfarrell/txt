### Taking on assignments:
    - Once you are done with the reading, log in to
      https://linkprotect.cudasvc.com/url?a=https%3a%2f%2fcrm.cactusglobal.com&c=E,1,_jeLZTNZY0syIsXT5fqdHXxlxTgeHOBXVR2eOJ8J6VG9Qw-rh7b-Rwk086UyRCyztI79N6raqkmJALgkWpo6_z2H5JnYRm3ETXUBSTYMbsOw0nTj&typo=1
      to accept your first assignment. If you don’t see any
      assignments, write to wfm@cactusglobal.com immediately and
      copy physics@cactusglobal.com on this email.

    - For a guide to using your CRM account, visit Editing
      Resources >> Resources >> CRM Guide for Editors.

    - We want to make sure that you get oriented to our quality
      benchmarks and processes gradually, so please try to adhere
      to these requirements in your first week:
        * Only accept assignments with deadlines that allow you
          to work on no more than 2,000 words each day
        * Ensure you refer to previous feedback regularly and
          apply it to subsequent assignments. You’ll be able to
          see the comments and ratings for each assignment from
          the My Report section of your dashboard.

    - Ensure that you're going through the job page for each
      assignment immediately after accepting an assignment and
      uploading the required files before the internal deadline.

    - You should be able to complete at least `10` assignments
      and `20,000` words in the next `15` business days. Write in
      if you think this is not doable. Note that the benchmark
      for clearing Phase 1 is an average quality index of 2.8.

### Whom to contact:
    - You'll find answers to most questions on Editing Resources,
      but if anything is unclear, write to me at
      physics@cactusglobal.com.

    - Should you have any questions when working on assignments,
      please click on the “Contact Us” (Create new query) button
      on the top right corner of your dashboard. This will open
      a page with a category-based menu, using which you can send
      us your question. We shall route your question to the
      relevant team and get back to you at the earliest.

    - For job-related issues or problems with meeting your
      deadline, please contact us as soon as possible (don’t wait
      until the day your job is due to be uploaded) to allow us
      sufficient time to find a solution—the sooner you inform
      us, the better.

   - You can also see all the questions you’ve sent to us:
     Contact us >> View past queries.

    - Please ensure you use the “Contact Us” button for all job-
      or deadline-related communication, as your immediate PoC
      may not always be available to answer questions related to
      assignments, due to differences in work timings, time
      zones, etc.

### Support teams:
    - The Workflow Management team (WFM) takes care of all work
      allocation requirements for the editing teams. WFM may
      contact you in matters pertaining to job
      allocation/tracking.

    - The Skill and Knowledge Management team (SKM) oversees all
      requirements related to onboarding and processes for the
      editing teams. Over the course of Phase 1 and Phase 2, you
      may receive emails from SKM seeking feedback regarding your
      experience during the Introductory Period. You may also
      receive emails regarding process-related updates.


    - Download the CACTUS CRM app for editors to work with us on
      the go! You’ll be able to view and accept assignments,
      download files, view assignment pages and do much more! The
      best part is that you’ll receive personalized notifications
      for things like client questions, new editing rounds for
      manuscripts you’ve edited previously, and deadline
      reminders for your accepted assignments.
