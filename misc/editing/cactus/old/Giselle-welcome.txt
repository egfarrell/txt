Before you begin, here are a few things you should know.

Allocation of assignments and deadlines:

-         Over the first 4 assignments, we will gauge your editing speed,
          quality, and technical expertise. Allocation during this period may be
          slow. Allocations may be suspended if our quality standards and
          assignment deadlines are not met.
-         CACTUS commits to timely delivery of assignments. For deadline
          breaches caused by the editor, clients are given a full refund and the
          editor does not receive a fee for the assignment.

Availability:

-         If you plan on not taking up work on certain days of the week or for
          an extended period, make sure that you update the My Unavailability
          page (under the Navigation pane of your Whiteboard account).

PoCs:
    - freelancer@cactusglobal.com         Clarifications regarding a particular job
    - physics@cactusglobal.com            Clarification regarding client feedback/reviewer ratings, updates on your (un)availability
    - freelancerpayments@cactusglobal.com Query regarding payments/invoices
    - freelancer@cactusglobal.com         Any other queries


Resources:
-         Before taking on your first assignment, ensure that you sign into your
          Whiteboard account, visit Editing Resources >> Resources from the menu
          on top, and go through the Whiteboard Guide for Editors and all the
          articles under Getting Started.

-         You can explore the other articles under Editing Resources once you
          have completed your first assignment.

-         If you haven’t gone through the Orientation to CACTUS presentation,
          this would be a good time.

Once done with the resources, don’t forget to click on the “Ready to begin”
button below. This will tell us that you are ready to take on your first
