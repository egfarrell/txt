        - MISSING COMMA?

              guardians have the required professional skills
              forces Socrates
                - ah found it: I should have a comma:
                - "When correcting a comma splice, that is when
                  joining two independent clauses with
                  a coordinating conjunction, `put a comma before
                  'and'`"(!)
        - "PURPOSED":
            - wrong: should be "proposed". 
            - (Brain often fills in the missing letters)
        - NUMBERED LISTS
            - Always check the spacing and sequencing of numbered
              items. Clients often manually number lists, with
              mixed results.
        - NO COLON NEEDED
            - Approach behaviors include`:` "physically ... etc"
            - See:
              https://www.grammarbook.com/punctuation/colons.asp
            - Useful: A colon means "that is to say" or "here's
              what I mean." Colons and semicolons should never be
              used interchangeably.
        - SLASH
            - I had "`approach/avoidance`" behaviours
            - ** Only use a slash to mean ‘or’, 
                - do not use to mean ‘and’, 
                - and only use when there’s a good reason to
                  prefer the symbol over the word.
                - (e.g., the space in a table is limited, or
                  convention dictates that you use the slash, as
                  you would in a financial or academic year).
        - EN DASH
            - I had: "Mehrabian/Russell"
            - The model is named after two people, so the names
              are conventionally joined using an _en dash_.
            - The same rule applies whenever the two elements of
              a compound are considered equivalent, so
              parent–child relationship, cost–benefit analysis,
              etc.
        - EN DASHS
            - 23(7), 517-527
            - "23" should be italicised
            - "517-527" should have an en dash
            - Remember that APA uses en dashes for all ranges,
              including page ranges.
        - APA REFERENCES HEADING
            - I had: "References" in normal font
            - Should be: Bold, centred, and started on a new page
              in APA 7.
        - APA AUTHORS IN BRACKETS:
            - ... (Milliman, 1986; Grayson & McNeill, 2009; Jones
              et al., 2009)
            - In APA, multiple studies sharing the same set of
              parentheses must be _alphabetised_.
        - APA REFERENCE LIST
            - _Alphabetise_ both the citations that share
              a single set of parentheses and the references in
              the reference list in APA.
