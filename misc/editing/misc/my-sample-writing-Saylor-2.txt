Enda Farrell - Short Article Sample (300 words approx.)

- The Current State of Physics
- Physics is in crisis. Following years of success, the field has
  stagnated and researchers are feeling desperate. Experimental
  tests no longer verify the latest theories and the delicate
  interplay between mathematics and physical reality is broken.
  The result? A proliferation of wild ideas such as multiple
  universes and worm-holes. Fantastical notions presented as hard
  science, despite the lack of experimental evidence. So how has
  this impasse been reached?
- In the golden age of the early 20th century, Physics was
  a simpler affair, ruled by two tribes. The theorists sifted
  experimental results for clues, while experimentalists eagerly
  tested the theorists' offerings. Each needed the other.
  Theorists guided experimentalists on what tools to build, while
  the imaginations of theorists were constrained by cold
  experimental fact. Working together, each group contributed to
  the modern edifice of Physics, standing proudly on the pillars
  of General Relativity and Quantum Mechanics.
- However, by the early 1970s the situation began to change. The
  standard model of physics had been erected, but slowly the
  field stagnated in its aftermath. A dearth of successful ideas
  left both camps floundering. The theorists wandering, lost in
  their imaginations; the experimentalists dwarfed by their
  particle accelerators, nothing to show for the technical
  extravagance.
- Some may dispute this view, but there is no doubt the symbiotic
  relationship between both groups has withered. Einstein may
  have conceived of relativity while staring out the window, but
  it was only accepted after years of experimental confirmation.
  While Quantum Mechanics was born from precise spectroscopic
  experiments, raw data sifted and analysed until a mathematical
  theory emerged. Once again, the world is waiting for an
  other-worldly figure to bridge the divide - a Copernicus,
  Newton, or Einstein - to fuse theory with experiment and
  restart the adventure of Physics.
