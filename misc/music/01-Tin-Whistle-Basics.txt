### Tin Whistle Basics
    - $MY_VIDEOS/misc/whistle/01-Basics/

    - This course is aimed at the absolute beginner whistle
      player. By the end of the course, the learner will have
      a repertoire of 17 popular Irish session tunes, the
      ability to play cuts, taps and rolls as well as a deeper
      understanding of the Irish music tradition. 

    - Your tutor Kirsten Allstaff will guide you every step of
      the way. First, you'll learn how to hold the instrument,
      proper hand & finger posture and good basic technique,
      which are all important fundamentals to get right before
      moving on.

    - Then, there is a progressive study of scales, articulation,
      and ornamentation with relevant exercises throughout, all
      learned through the tutors precise choice of tunes for the
      appropriate level of ability.

    - The 'Whistle Basics' course will equip the beginner
      whistler with the necessary rudiments to begin a journey
      down the long and adventurous road of Irish whistle
      playing.
