### Tin Whistle Progressions

    - Progressions is designed as a continuation course to Tin
      Whistle _Foundations_.

    - Kirsten continues to build on and consolidate techniques
      previously taught, whilst taking you up to the next level
      of ornamentation: short rolls, bounces, and articulation
      techniques.

    - The repertoire of Irish dance tunes encompasses Jigs,
      Reels, Hornpipes, and the well-known Air Inis Oírr.

    - Throughout the second half of the course, Kirsten explores
      and demonstrates how the recognition of melodic motifs,
      patterns, and arpeggios in the tunes can aid learning by
      ear more quickly and efficiently.

    - The tunes are:
        - Ah Surely
        - Cuil Aodha
        - The Standing Abbey
        - Seamus Connolly’s Jig
        - The Boy in the Gap
        - The Man of Aran
        - The Donegal Lass
        - The Ivy Leaf
        - The Battle of Aughrim
        - Naughton’s Reel
        - Tom Billy’s Jig
        - Inis Oírr
        - The Cuckoo’s Nest
