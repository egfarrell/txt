[book](https://www.amazon.co.uk/More-Precisely-Math-Need-Philosophy/dp/1551119099/)

- I chose this 200 page-book as a guide to learning mathematical
  principles of philosophic discourse in Plato's Timeaus.

- It has 8 parts, along with sections for further reading,
  glossary of symbols, references, and index.

1.  Sets                     (Collections of Things        ->  Cartesian Products)
2.  Relations                (Functions                    ->  Classes)
3.  Machines                 (Rules                        ->  Turing Machines)
4.  Semantics                (Words                        ->  Truth Conditions for Moral Statements)
5.  Probability              (Spaces                       ->  Knowledge and Information Flow)
6.  Utilitarianism           (Active Agents                ->  Moral Qualities of Actions)
7.  From Finite to Infinite  (Recursively Defined Spaces   ->  Supertasks)
8.  Bigger Infinites         (Transfinite Ordinal Numbers  ->  Degrees of Divine Perfection)

- The author starts with set theory and ends with transfinite
  recursion, demonstrating in a section on Degrees of Divine
  Perfection how the perfection of God can be shown in symbolic
  terms as "an absolutely infinite perfection," walking the
  reading through the process (of divine creativity).
