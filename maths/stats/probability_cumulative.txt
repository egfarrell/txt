### cumulative probability
    - If you want the probability of something happening,
      you have to integrate the formula for the normal
      distribution:

    - But doing the _integral is hard_ sometimes, so we use
      the cumulative distribution function.

    - The Cumulative distribution is probability from -∞ up
      to a certain number.

    - E.g. To calculate the probability of your value being
      between -1 and 1:

    1. Get the cumulative probability from
        -∞ probability up to _1_

    2. and subtract the cumulative probability from
        -∞ probability up to _-1_

    - The _difference_ between the two values gives your
      desired probability.
