Markov idea
    - The future is independent of the past..... 
        - ... given the _present_!!

- Quite natural really when you think about it...

- If youre looking for information `Xn+1` then `Xn` will give you
  the most information! Recent data tells you more...

- 1st order model
    - Prediction depends on current state only

### 2nd order models
    - when the prediction depends on the TWO previous states

### ergodic
    knowledge of intial state fades over time
    And the chain approaches some asymptotic state
    ie the states mix!
    The speed at which the intial state gets lost is called the mixing speed.

### Random walk
    a 2-D markov process with discrete time _and_ discrete
    space

### Transition probabilities
    describes liklihood of changing from one state to
    another

### Transition matrix
    The entries in the matrix should all be less than one.
    The individual rows in the matrix should add up to one.

### caveat
    You cant assume to have perfect knowledge of the current
    state. There must be some hidden/latent info in the
    state. This is modelled by _hidden markov models_ HMM!!
