### arithmetic mean
    - defined as the point where the sum of the deviations is zero
    - if you have scores 3,4,5 then
    - the mean is 4 and
    - the deviations is +1,0,-1 which equals 0

### geometric mean

### moving average
    - Compute average of data points in CHUNKS
    - e.g. average of 1,4,5,10 is 5.0
    - But a moving average taking 2 measures at a time
    - gives the avg(1+4) + avg(5+10)
    - which gives 4.88!
    - Sometimes more accurate cos takes account of
    - extreme or unique data points

### weighted mean
    - sum all values of (Value . Frequency)
    - then divide by Frequency
    - Its a quicker way than adding up many individual scores.
