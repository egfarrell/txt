### Sample Question 1:
    An event promoter has scheduled an outdoor concert and
    expects to make a £12,000 profit if it does not rain. If
    it does rain she expects to make a £5,000 loss. The
    probability of rain on the day of the event is estimated
    to be 0.3. What is the promoter's expected profit?

    - £100
    - £3,500
    - £6,900
    - £7,000

    - The correct answer is C.
    - The expected profit in `£12,000 x 0.7 - £5,000 x 0.3p = £6,900`

### Sample Question 2:
    A particular car achieves, on average, 35 miles per
    gallon. Taking 8 Km as equivalent to 5 miles, and one
    gallon as the same as four and a half litres, which of
    the following is the consumption figure, expressed in
    litres per 100 Km?

    - 7.56
    - 8.04
    - 10.53
    - 12.48

    - The correct answer is B.
    - 35 miles is 56Km, so we get 56/4.5 Km/litre.
    - So number of litres consumed in 100 Km is: `100/(56/4.5) = 8.04`. ppp
