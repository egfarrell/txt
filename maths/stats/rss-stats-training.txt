### Radio   
    - More or Less is a half-hour show on BBC Radio 4 where Tim
      Harford and the More or Less team try to make sense of the
      statistics which surround us. BBC World Service broadcasts
      a short edition over the weekend.  
    - Moneybox, hosted by journalist Paul Lewis, also on BBC
      Radio 4, explores how to understand money and how to handle
      finances by thinking statistically.  
    - TV programmes and videos  
    - Professor Hans Rosling proved that statistics is anything
      but boring in The Joy of Stats.
    -  Tails you win: the science of chance is presented by
      former RSS president Sir David Spiegelhalter.  
    - Climate Change by Numbers is a BBC show where a group of
      mathematicians clarify all the important questions around
      climate change.
    - The Royal Statistical Society's YouTube account features
      a plethora of talks, webinars, seminars and meetings that
      we have hosted over the past few years.
