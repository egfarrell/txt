### Week 1 Routine (Mix and Match)

### Day 1:
    - Watch StatQuest intro and 3Blue1Brown’s “Bayes’ Theorem”
    - Play with Seeing Theory interactive
        - https://seeing-theory.brown.edu/bayesian-inference/index.html
    - Do one basic coin/dice Bayes problem in Python

### Day 2:
    - Read “Think Bayes” Ch. 1–2 (try to do the first code
      example)
    - Listen to "Not So Standard Deviations" podcast ep. 104

### Day 3:
    - Watch more StatQuest (focus: likelihood, prior, posterior)
    - Read “Bayes’ Rule With Python” ch. 1–2 (quickly, mostly
      code)
    - Do one small toy notebook (coin flips, parameter
      estimation)

### Day 4–5:
    - “Bayes Rule: A Visual Introduction” — work through one
      slider example
    - Read “Think Bayes” Ch. 3–4
    - Try a toy problem: e.g. update a prior for the probability
      of rain based on weather reports

### Day 6–7:
    - Watch “Learning Bayesian Statistics” podcast ep. 1
    - Summarize your own “rules of thumb” for what a prior,
      likelihood, and posterior really mean
    - Make a notebook with 2–3 vivid examples (e.g., coins, dice,
      disease test, or a fun scenario from a blog/book above)
