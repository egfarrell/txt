# Probability: a question of philosophy
    * Frequentist: 
        - frequency of REPEATED events
    * Bayesian: 
        - our own certainty or uncertainty of events

# Both groups are analysing different things
    * Frequentist:
        - analysing the variation of DATA and derived quantities
          from the data
    * Bayesian:
        - are analysing variation of BELIEFS
    * Frequentist:
        - the models are fixed,
        - and the data can vary around those
    * Bayesians:
        - we observe the (fixed) data,
        - and the model can vary around those

# bayes rule
    ```
    postierior = liklihood * prior
                 -----------------
                  model evidence
    ```
              (often a normalisation)

# photon flux example
    * Frequentists:
        - take many many measurements.
        - Multiply the probabilities together which gives the
          "maximum likelihood"

# pool table example
    - Frequentists: 
        - nuisance parameter 
        - (how the table is divided) is fixed
    - Bayesians: 
        - marginalisation: nuisance parameter is integrated out!
