


# Bayesian Resources: Intuitive / Vivid

    ### 1. Video & Visual Explainers
        - StatQuest with Josh Starmer (YouTube):
        - Search for “StatQuest Bayes Theorem” — uses analogies,
          visual intuition, super clear step-by-step.
        - See also: “StatQuest: Bayesian Statistics, Clearly
          Explained!” (full playlist).
        - 3Blue1Brown: “Bayes Theorem — The Simplest Case”
        - Short, animated visual of how Bayes’ rule works, with
          cubes and colors:
        - https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HZGCoVF3YvM
        - Seeing Theory: Bayesian Inference Interactive
        - Click and drag to see how priors and likelihoods
          combine, visually.
        - https://seeing-theory.brown.edu/bayesian-inference/index.html

    ### 2. Podcasts / Story-Based
        - Not So Standard Deviations Podcast (Hilary Parker,
          Roger Peng):
        - Ep. 104 “Bayesian Thinking in Real Life” — less
          technical, more on how Bayesian ideas show up in
          decisions, science, and data work.
        - Apple/Spotify/Google podcasts.
        - Learning Bayesian Statistics (Podcast, interviews):
        - Episode 1, “What is Bayesian Statistics?” — very
          conversational, includes real-world analogies.
        - https://learnbayesstats.com/episodes/

    ### 3. Playable / Interactive Tools
        - Seeing Theory (again) — play with priors, likelihoods:
        - https://seeing-theory.brown.edu/bayesian-inference/index.html
        - “Bayes Rule: A Visual Introduction” (Will Kurt):
            - Visual and gamified explanations, includes online
              calculators and slider tools.
            - https://www.countbayesie.com/blog/2015/2/18/bayes-rule-a-visual-introduction

    ### 4. Engaging Blogs & Books
        - “Bayesian Thinking” chapter in “The Art of Statistics”
          (David Spiegelhalter):
        - Written in vivid English, lots of real-world examples
          (e.g., criminal justice, medical testing).
        - Try to borrow from a library or find a summary if you
          don’t want the whole book.
        - Blog: “All of Statistics for Data Science” — Bayesian
          Intuition
        - https://seeing-theory.brown.edu/bayesian-inference/index.html
        - (yes, it’s so good it’s listed three times...)
        - Book: “Bayes’ Rule With Python: A Tutorial Introduction
          to Bayesian Analysis” (James V. Stone)
        - Very readable, code-focused, with dice, coins, and
          practical examples.
        - PDF freely available via Google search.
