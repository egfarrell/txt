# Lesson 08  - Estimation (confidence intervals)
------------------------------------------------

# point estimate:
    took a sample (n=35)of klout users who all use the bieber
    tweeter. The sample mean was 40. So if the whole
    population of klout users started to use the beiber
    tweeter our best guess of their average klout score
    would be 40. This "40" is called a point estimate.

# margin of error
    `2 * (σ/sqrt(n))`

# confidence interval
    In general, 95% of sample means will be between 2 standard
    deviations from the population mean. Most likely, this
    sample with a mean of 40 is one of the 95% that'll fall
    between two SD's of the new mean of the sampling
    distribution if everyone uses the bieber tweeter app..

# interval of new predicted population mean
    `[μ{bt} - 2(σ/sqrt(n),  μ{bt} + 2(σ/sqrt(n)]`
    and a mean of 40 must be somewhere in between
    the endpoints of the above interval:
    `[μ{bt} - 2(σ/sqrt(n) < 40 <  μ{bt} + 2(σ/sqrt(n)]`
    which leads to:
    `[40 - 2(σ/sqrt(n) < μ{bt} <  40 + 2(σ/sqrt(n)]`
    and this is the 95% confidence interval

# correction (1.96)
    we used to say 95% of samples will be within 2 standard
    deviations of the mean. Well its not exactly 2 SD's, its
    _1.96_ if you check the Z-table.

# confidence interval summary
   `x̄ +/- (z-score)(σ/sqrt(n))`
   where x̄ is the sample mean
   and z-score is usually 1.96 (ie. nearly 2 standard
   deviations from the mean)

# critical values:
    * +/- 1.96 are the critical values of z for 95%
      confidence
    * +/- 2.33 are the critical values of z for 98%
      confidence


margin of error
    is half the confidence interval
    `(z-score)(σ/sqrt(n))``
