
### Green's Function

- Green's function is a concept in mathematical analysis and
  physics to solve certain types of differential equations. 
    - You can think of Green's function as a kind of "_response
      function_" or "influence function" that tells you how
      a system or physical quantity _will behave in response_ to
      a specific stimulus or input.

- Consider a musical instrument: Imagine a guitar string, and you
  pluck it at a specific point. The resulting sound you hear is
  a combination of various vibrations and harmonics. 
    - Now, suppose you want to know the exact motion of the
      string at any point in time after the pluck. To determine
      this, you can use Green's function for the guitar string.

- The Green's function for the guitar string would give you the
  precise motion of the string at any location and time in
  response to a localized pluck at a specific point. It
  encapsulates the entire behavior of the string, taking into
  account the physics of the system, such as tension, stiffness,
  and damping.

- Similarly, in mathematical equations, Green's function is used
  to solve differential equations by expressing the solution as
  a sum or integral of the Green's function multiplied by
  a specific input or stimulus function. It provides a way to
  determine the behavior of the system or quantity of interest
  based on the given input or stimulus.

- In summary, Green's function is a mathematical tool that helps
  understand how a system or physical quantity responds to
  a specific input or stimulus. It allows us to analyze and solve
  complex problems by breaking them down into simpler components
  and considering their individual responses to specific inputs.
