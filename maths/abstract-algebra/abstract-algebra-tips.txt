

### Equivalence Classes (Summer 2024)
    - Finally understand equivalence class after hearing about it
      in Aberdeen years ago. Here's the example. Let A be the set
      of relatives, let the set A be the set of relatives, and
      let the distinguishing criterion R be their gender.
    - Then the notation Victoria equivalent Emma indicates that
      the two relatives are equivalent in the sense that they are
      both female.
    - And an equivalence relation has the following properties.
    - A is equivalent to A, every element is equivalent to
      itself, so Victoria is equivalent to Victoria in the sense
      that she is just female.
    - Symmetry A is equivalent to B implies that B is equivalent
      to A and vice versa.
    - So Victoria is equivalent to Emma implies that Emma is
      equivalent to Victoria because they are both female.
    - And transivity, if A is equivalent to B and B is equivalent
      to C, it implies that A is equivalent to C.
    - So Victoria is equivalent to Emma and Emma is equivalent to
      Alice. It implies Victoria is equivalent to Alice.
