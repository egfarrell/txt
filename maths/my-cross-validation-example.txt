Give an example of when you have demonstrated this competency

For my Physics degree, I used *Cross-Validation* to
fine-tune a Decision Tree model which predicts the distance
to a galaxy based on telescopic colour measurements.
Decision (Regression) Trees are used when the response
variable is continuous and they can be fine-tuned by
a maximum depth parameter. I chose this model because the
input data doesn't need to be normalised and they handle
non-linear relationships.

To assess the bias and variance of my model, I split the
data into training, cross-validation and test datasets.
I used the training dataset to create regression trees for
a variety of depth settings. I then applied the trained
models to the cross-validation dataset and plotted “learning
curves”: training *errors* against cross-validation *errors*
as a function of the depth parameter.

I determined the optimal parameter setting by repeatedly
adjusting the depth based on the learning curves. Initially
I found the model to be highly biased. I then adjusted the
depth to higher values, re-checking the curves for high
variance. (If the depth is too high, the decision tree
learns the fine details of the training data and over-fits)
I finally achieved an optimal depth and executed the
optimised model on the final test dataset.

During the work I found the training data was too sparse for
certain types of galaxies and some colour combinations are
not-unique resulting in catastrophic errors. I overcame this
by sourcing alternative data with more sample cases.
I successfully optimised my model and my results matched
those of a published Astronomy paper.
