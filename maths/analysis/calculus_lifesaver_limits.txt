# Limits

### How do we know if theres a horizontal asympotote?
    - Check limits at infinity. As x--> infinity, f(x) --> L

### what about sin(1/x) at zero?
    - DNE. has to keep going between 1 and -1 but at shorter and shorter intervals

### Misconceptions
    - asymptote - Cannot be crossed?
    - No! see sinx/x

    - horizontal left and right totes must be the same ? 
    - No! inverse `tan of x`

### Sandwich theorm
    - Evaluating Limits at "a", Rational Functions
    - If you can plug in "a" and get something sensible then limit exists at "a"
    - If you get something insensible (zero/zero) then its an indeterminate form.
    - zero on the bottom, then verticle asymptote.
    - tricky square root sign above or below, multiply by conjugate.

### tricks: 
    - factorise
    - divide by highest degree term and multiply by same term
      This zaps all the other terms down to zero

### Summary: 
    - degree on top higher? Goes to +-infinity
    - degree on top lower? Goes to zero.
    - Degrees equal? Goes to ratio of leading coefficients

### Trick with negative square roots!
    - We know `x` is negative so whats the sqrt(4x^6)? Its MINUS
      2x^3!!! 
    - Dont forget minus. Cos we're squaring an (ODD) cube
      function.

### Limits for absolute values.
    - Should: Convert into piecewise function.
    - basically for the standard formula of absolute value, 
    - stick in whatever youre trying to evaluate.

    - Has nice example with |x-3|/x-3 ... just watch you cant
      have zero in denominator. (Its a step function)
