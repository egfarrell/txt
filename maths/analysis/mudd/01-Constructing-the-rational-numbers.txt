

### Wrestling with the infinite: pi
    - can you construct `pi` using a straight-edge and compass?
    - no you can't construct `pi` because it's transcendental,
      and constructable numbers are always algebraic, and
      therefore not transcendental
    - Newton and Pi: Showed how pi can be constructed by an
      infinite series, that you can sum to give pi
        - but what is an "infinite" series
        - murky waters, calculus was a "toolbox" at first
    -  what does it mean for a series to converge?  
    -  what is a limit? Are there enough numbers to capture all
       limits?
     - or can a sequence of numbers converge but don't have
       a limit??  
     - also: 18th centurey: mathematicians became uneasy with
       fourier series: infinite sums of sin/cosine
