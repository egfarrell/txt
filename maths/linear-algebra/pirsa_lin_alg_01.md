pirsa linear algebra 01

### dual space:
    - set of _operators_ taking you from your vector space `V` down
      into the field
    - scalar product belongs to dual space `V*`
    - bra vectors belong to dual space `V*` too

### misc
    - Linear operator A
    - Adjoint operator A+
    - Kets: Represent states as [column]  vectors
    - Bras: row vectors
    - Operators as matrices
    - `H |ψ⟩ = E |ψ⟩`
    - this imples `|ψ⟩` is an eigenvector, `E` is an eigenvalue

### Set of all eigenvalues = spectrum of an operator
    - Representing an operator as a matrix gives an easy way to find its
    eigenvalues and eigenvectors, simply solve the characteristic equation

### Hermitian operators
    - (Have real eigenvalues, so theyre useful in QM)
    - (also have orthogonal eigenvectors)
    - `H = H+` (Its equal to its own adjoint)
    - `H` has to be equal to its adjoint because `E` is a real value => `E = E*`
    - Must describe all physical observables in QM

### Unitary Operators
    - (Important  because they preserve probabilities)
    - UU+ = 1 = U+U
    - Example: e^(-iHt/h-bar)

### Unitary operators have "unimodular" eigenvalues
    - `U|ψ⟩ = |ψ⟩`
    - `|λ|^2 = 1`
