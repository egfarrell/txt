

If you haven't checked out Christopher Olah's blog, you
really should. Even though most of the topics he discusses
are beyond the scope of this course, the illustrations and
explanations are worth their weight in gold for the
motivation alone.

[Word Embeddings
](http://colah.github.io/posts/2014-07-NLP-RNNs-Representations/)
encode words as vectors in a very high dimensional space. If
you've watched the first few week 0 videos, you know that we
could have a function that maps some word (say "cat") to
a cartesian product R x R x ... x R. 

Given enough data we can come up with a "space" in which to
embed words such that these vectors are not only useful, but
encode surprisingly deep relationships between words. In
other words, we can ask in what way certain words are "close
to" other words.

This article really helped me _understand why_ vectors and
vector spaces are so cool.

His other posts are just as good, and I really hope he
continues to publish.
