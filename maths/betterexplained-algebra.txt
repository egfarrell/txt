betterexplained algebra

    - When we write a polynomial like `x^2 + x = 6 ` we can think
      at a higher level.

    - `^2`, `x` and `6` are all "numbers", but now we’re keeping
      track of how they’re made:

    - `x^2` is a component interacting with itself
    - `x` is a component on its own
    - `6` is the desired state we want the entire system to become.

- After the interactions are finished, we should get 6.
- What number is hiding inside of x?

- Our original system is `x^2 + x`.
- The desired state is `6`.

- A new system:
- `{x^2 + x - 6}`
- will track the difference between the original system and the
  desired state.

- When are we happiest?
- When there’s NO DIFFERENCE:
- `{x^2 + x - 6 = 0}`
