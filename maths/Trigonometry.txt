### Small angle approximation!
    - Discussion of continuous symmetries in:
        - $MY_BOOKS/Physics/Undergrad/Overview-Marburger-constructing-reality.pdf
    - Imagine an infinitesimal rotation by tiny angle `ε` (ε ≪ 1).
    - Use small-angle approximations:
        - `cos ε ≈ 1`
        - `sin ε ≈ ε`

### Reciprocals: Co-secant and secant
    - if you take the reciprocal of
        - `sin` you get cosecant
        - `cos` you get secant

### Inverses
    - sin --->  arcsin
    - cos --->  arccos
    - tan --->  arctan
