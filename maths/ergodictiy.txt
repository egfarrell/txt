    - Gaussian noise of this type is assumed to be identical in
      character to the noise voltages generated in resistors and
      amplifiers and is sometimes called Johnson noise. Such
      waveforms are usually assumed to be stationary and ergodic,
      that is, ensemble averages and time averages converge to
      equal values.
